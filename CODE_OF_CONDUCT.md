# Code of Conduct

Individual Zero is an open research project. Productive disagreement is expected and useful.

Contributors should critique ideas, mechanisms, methods, and evidence rather than people; distinguish observation from assumption; document uncertainty; credit code, research, data, and ideas; respect privacy; and help keep experiments reproducible and understandable.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor's work, and knowingly presenting fabricated results as evidence are not acceptable.

No contributor is required to share every intuition behind the project. Competing hypotheses are welcome when they can be discussed clearly and, where possible, tested.

Concerns should be raised privately with the maintainer when public discussion would worsen the situation.
