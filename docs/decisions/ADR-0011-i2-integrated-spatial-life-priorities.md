# ADR-0011: Integrated spatial life as the next I2 development priority

**Status: ACCEPTED research and engineering-direction decision, owner-approved 3 November 2026.** Coordinator [#921](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/921); actionable [I2 roadmap](../../src/i2/docs/INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md). This decision **does not** register/activate I2, authenticate independent M11 or establish adaptive scientific evidence.

## Context

The native local I2 prototype now has an internally born, paid, committed SQLite body/decision loop, birth temperament and lived resource-action associations. Exact-source #916/#919 show actual changes of chosen actions, and also inactivity and researcher-authored arbitration. Despite this progress, `collecter` remains a single local **nonspatial** opportunity. An individual cannot remember a physically visited site and return when its needs recur; improving action scores alone cannot answer that question.

## Decision

**Stop adding ad hoc Competition heuristics as the default research route.** Prioritize a **minimal traversable I2-only world** (P1), personally experienced site × current need association and chosen revisit (P2), renewable multi-need ecology (P3), then longitudinal independent local research identities with learning ON/OFF, adverse controls and real cost/censor tracking (P4). P0 documentation/source-pin accuracy precedes the source change. P5 safety, physical I1 parity and independent M11 remain separately tracked; [#888](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/888) and [#926](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/926) must not be confused with an active-engine coding prerequisite. Dream replay [#902](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/902) stays deferred.

Full issues: [P0 #922](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/922), [P1 #923](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/923), [P2 #924](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/924), [P3 #925](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/925), [P4 #906](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/906), [P5 #926](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/926) and [#888](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/888).

## Invariants and scientific falsifier

No I1 runtime or inherited memory, no parallel owner, motor script, hidden-quality oracle, free UC, timed stale-resource bonus, guaranteed place stock, terminal recovery or automatic promotion. The same individual must **physically access** the place, perceive its actually admitted body consequence in a later capture, and potentially use the memory when a real physiological need recurs. A future need can alter subjective priority, but does not prove the world changed. The test must show a **chosen paid physical return**, with a matched no-learning/negative case, or preserve a null result. Merely obtaining site-score differences, green CI or completing modules does not prove natural learning.

**DRIFT DETECTED:** when work piles extra score patches and heavy synthetic checks ahead of the spatial-return critical path, stop, name the falsifiable gap and redirect to the smallest missing physical or cognitive link. Source-lock all experiments, their null/adverse outcomes, UC and cold replay.
