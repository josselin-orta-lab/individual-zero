# ADR-0003 - Integrated living I2 as the next scientific milestone

**Status: ACCEPTED as a project-owner research-priority decision, 1 November 2026.**
This decision confirms and sharpens [ADR-0002](ADR-0002-scientific-prototype-first.md).
**Later owner correction:** [ADR-0008](ADR-0008-remove-obsolete-i2-external-g1-review-gate.md) authorizes isolated I2 prototype implementation and research-only internal birth/actions without prior third-party G1 reviews. It does not establish living M11, scientific H1/H2/H3 proof or active-engine promotion.

## Basis and identified drift

The I2 proposal currently owns a non-living SQLite v5 cognitive fixture,
paid synthetic Competition, chosen-outcome and H2 studies. Its `lab/` and
`runtime/` remain placeholders; `engine.json` has no executable entrypoints.
[PR #779](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/779)
falsified autonomous chosen-only H2 adaptation when matched counterfactuals
were unavailable. [PR #780](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/780)
showed useful and adverse synthetic adaptations, but its exploration was
forced by a research controller. The G1 review in
[issue #662](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/662)
remains open. Isolated G2 guards and CI PASS cannot answer I2's H1/H2/H3.

**DRIFT DETECTED:** continuing to perfect synthetic guards without a path to
a living, observable loop displaces the scientific objective.

## Decision

1. **Milestone:** an independently born I2 with no inherited experience must
   perceive its body/world; form a candidate through its own cognition;
   arbitrate and pay UC; pass normal safety and motor admission; apply a real
   WorldBody effect; receive the later *subjectively accessible* consequence;
   and demonstrably alter a subsequent choice from that experience.
   Include death/terminal behavior and identity/provenance guarantees.
2. **Minimal domain:** a deliberately tiny world, body, need, and
   collect/consume-like action sequence are sufficient. Do not make realism,
   an elaborate self-model, or full cognitive completeness prerequisites.
   An observed failure is scientifically useful.
3. **Falsification before elaboration:** compare independently initialized
   learning-enabled and learning-ablated I2 runs under common physical laws,
   with stable, adverse and role-reversed worlds. Record behavioral
   transitions, UC, admitted actions, true body outcomes, non-offers/vetoes,
   mortality and all null/negative results. Fix confirmatory study design
   and sampling **before** G4/G5, not after favorable pilot results.
4. **Current shortest route (ADR-0008 supersedes the old external review hold):**
   implement one minimal I2-owned internal WorldBody, paid UC, ordinary
   safety/admission, subjective feedback and fresh local genesis for #664
   **now**. Record faults, terminal/rollback limits and missing external M11
   witness honestly. G0 custody #661 continues in parallel; formal
   I1/I2 comparison and registration require their own evidence and
   deliberate owner decision. No outside reviewer signature is a coding gate.
5. **Complexity budget:** prefer extending existing I2-owned components
   over another owner, compatibility layer, parallel simulator or synthetic
   adapter. New G2 work must eliminate a specific shortest-path blocker or
   produce a new falsifiable integrated observation. Flag new drift
   explicitly to the project owner, with the cheaper alternative.
6. **Evidence discipline:** no I1 runtime/state inheritance, hidden-world
   oracle, free UC, fabricated unchosen outcomes, forced actions in claimed
   autonomous experiments, postmortem recovery or activation by a green CI.
   Keep tests narrow and expensive cohort CI reserved for justified gates.

## Immediate authorized engineering increment (G2 only)

To study one concrete gap before G1 is reviewed, let existing paid
Competition re-evaluate an actually tried but stale action from **only
earlier chosen outcomes**, with an explicit no-reconsideration ablation.
The research controller may follow the resulting nonmotor advice, but must
not impose an exploratory choice in this arm. Report recovery benefits,
adverse costs, intervention provenance and equal synthetic UC.
This is an *offline heuristic study*, not a living individual's
intrinsic curiosity, genuine WorldBody actuation or H2 causal validation.
Do not let the short experiment expand into another open-ended G2 campaign.

## Next checkpoint

The independent two-reviewer G1 decision request #662 is obsolete by
ADR-0008. Prioritize implementation #664 with a bounded internal research
world, explicit checks and no automatic activation. Report the observed
engineering evidence and unresolved trust/provenance gaps, never a fabricated
scientific GO.
