# ADR-0021: I3 Stage 2 methodological HOLD without mandatory external reviewer

**Date:** 13 November 2026. **Status:** ACCEPTED AS INTERNAL RESEARCH-GOVERNANCE DISPOSITION under the owner's instruction to remove the RP-A1 reviewer and the bounded documentary delegation in [ADR-0020](ADR-0020-i3-owner-selected-development-reference.md). **Not an accepted Stage-2 scientific success/failure verdict, experimental permission or engine activation.**

## Context

The [original Stage-2 question](../../src/i3/docs/STAGE2_LAUNCH.md) concerns an individual's autonomy to decide whether its own **additional, actually paid computation** is worthwhile for its bodily stakes. The frozen E1/E3 screening observations and exploratory 80/81, B1, RANDOM/content and next-action data are useful but cannot support a general Stage-2 effect without a relevant estimand, independent units, complete natural body cost and prospective inference.

The [12-Nov C01 internal review](../../src/i3/docs/STAGE2_C01_INTERNAL_FUTILITY_REVIEW_2026-11-12.md) already found `HOLD_NO_VERDICT_FOR_GENERAL_STAGE2`. A later [proposed roadmap](../../src/i3/docs/STAGE2_AUDIT_DELTA_AND_CRITICAL_PATH_2026-11-12.md) made external review a blocker. On 13 November the owner explicitly removed that requirement **for RP-A1**, without granting new scientific cohorts. The [13-Nov C01 disposition](../../src/i3/docs/STAGE2_C01_INTERNAL_DISPOSITION_2026-11-13.md) records the internal adjudication and symmetric falsification of the “E3 0/2 = universal failure” and “B1 +8/96 = success” arguments.

## Decision

1. **RP-A1 / S2-C01 is an internal source-traceable methodological decision.** A human/external reviewer, signature and deadline are **not prerequisites**. Its possible outcomes are `CLOSE_BOUNDED`, `DISTINCT_SOURCE_REQUIRED` and `HOLD_NO_VERDICT`, with explicit source, preannounced hypothesis, contrast, unit, power and limitation. An agent's conclusion is not a peer-review certificate.
2. **For existing evidence, adopt `HOLD_NO_VERDICT` for the generalized Stage-2 question** and keep the canonical scientific verdict `NOT_ISSUED`. E1 and E3 remain negative solely for their exact frozen software interventions. The B1 positive exploratory difference neither establishes benefit nor survival non-inferiority; no negative result is erased.
3. **RP-A2 records suspension, not scientific closure.** The project retains I3 P1.3 funded-v2 as the internal scientific reference, with existing inherited cognition budgets. There is no Stage 2.2/2.3 scientific promotion and no automatic Stage-3-to-6 scientific gate release from this ADR.
4. **Research-engineering can continue independently** on source isolation, birth identity, M11 durability, anti-rollback and cold recovery under [ADR-0020](ADR-0020-i3-owner-selected-development-reference.md). Development of a new cognition predictor on exposed E3 or “past-error” fixtures is not justified by this decision. An autonomous agent may improve documentation/diagnostics without seeking a C01 relecteur.
5. **Do not alter scientific admissions.** `fresh_lived_worlds_allowed`, `scientific_gate_authorized`, `other_world_indices_authorized`, `next_scientific_cohort_authorized` and `all_new_worlds_currently_authorized` remain **false**; historical DEVELOPMENT/selection/validation/reserve cohorts remain consumed/closed. `reference_generation=I3` and `active_generation=I1` remain distinct. Do not change the I3 engine proposal/entrypoint contract.
6. **Prospective work has separate gates:** an action-relevant first-person source specified before testing, no-World native-action/cost falsifier, full endogenous computation and opportunity costs, independent ecology/estimand/power, sealed exact-source rehearsal, and new narrowly scoped owner World/index authorization. No retrospective power margin or reused exposed seed may substitute.
7. **No inherited reviewer gate is silently generalized.** This removes a reviewer from **the RP-A1 documentary adjudication**. It does not certify statistical independence, waive scientific rigor, or relax future research authorization or optional external publication review.

## Consequences and falsifiers

The route `RP-A1 -> RP-A2` ends in a **documented scientific HOLD**. The agent coordinator must point to this disposition without asking for an external C01 signoff. It must still fail closed if I3's live contract/manifest changes or any scientific permission is granted unexpectedly. A falsely reported `CLOSE_BOUNDED` of Stage 2, a new executable scientific World, a mutated old original, or accidental I3 registration would violate this ADR.

Future general scientific Stage-2 closure is possible only from a new valid, adequately powered, relevant prospective result. A valid `HOLD` can be published and revisited on **new evidence**, not by repeating a synthetic grid. Adjacent native-engine engineering may proceed without claiming adaptive thought has been proven.

## Scope / evidence

I3 architecture and research governance only. No Mind, World, BodyLaws, genome, original experiment, historical protocol, runtime or scientific cohort was changed in adopting this decision. No new scientific lives or local experimental tests were run.
