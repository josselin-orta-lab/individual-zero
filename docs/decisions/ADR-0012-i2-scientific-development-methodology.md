# ADR-0012: I2 scientific development through integrated behavior, generalization and convergence

**Status: ACCEPTED. Owner-approved 3 November 2026.** Normative development method for the I2 research prototype, not an approved cognitive mechanism, experiment, scientific result, engine activation or M11 certification. This complements [ADR-0002](ADR-0002-scientific-prototype-first.md), [ADR-0004](ADR-0004-evidence-driven-development-and-g2-stop-rule.md), [ADR-0010](ADR-0010-stable-world-evidence-based-association.md), [ADR-0011](ADR-0011-i2-integrated-spatial-life-priorities.md) and the [P0-P5 roadmap](../../src/i2/docs/INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md). The operational, nonduplicated procedure lives in [I2 scientific development method](../../src/i2/docs/SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT_METHOD.md).

## Context

I1 accumulated technically credible isolated mechanisms and passing checks without establishing sustained integrated adaptive learning. I2 now has an independently owned local research loop and source-locked behavioral probes: [#933](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/933) observed a paid, memory-associated site return but no second drink; [#939](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/939) preserved a negative purpose-continuity intervention; [#941](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/941) observed a second physically admitted drink after isolating noncontextual movement credit. These are narrow designed-world research observations, not a general learning or survival result. Many individually useful experimental flags and small PRs can still recreate the architectural complexity seen in I1.

The next question is not how many mechanisms can be built, but whether the same individual, from its own physically grounded history and present needs, can change course across repeated, competing and adverse conditions. The owner approved an explicit development method to prevent local fixes from becoming an endless optimization loop.

## Options considered

- Continue adding local scores and protections after each failed trial: **rejected** as the default, because it risks tuning to known scenarios without demonstrating integrated behavior.
- Require full realism, authenticated living-engine authority or long confirmatory campaigns before all research coding: **rejected** as a prototype blocker. Preserve the actual safety and promotion boundaries separately.
- Adopt a behavioral, falsifiable, convergence-oriented development cycle within the existing I2 P0-P5 plan: **accepted**.

## Experimental evidence or constraints

- The source-locked place-return trial [#933](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/933) distinguishes changed motor choice from useful bodily outcome.
- Negative follow-through [#939](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/939) demonstrates why a valid local mechanism need not improve the integrated sequence.
- The subsequent narrow positive [#941](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/941) supports one constructed-world causal link, not generalization or durable survival.
- [ADR-0001](ADR-0001-strict-generation-autonomy.md) prohibits I1 runtime/state inheritance; first-person physical truth, paid admission, nonresurrection, honest controls and original immutable negative outcomes remain binding.

## Decision

1. **Behavioral question before new mechanism.** A substantive cognitive or ecological intervention names a concrete H1/H2/H3 question or its shortest critical-path blocker; freeze a falsifier, observation plan and credible negative/learning ablation before running it. A new feature is not itself a scientific milestone.
2. **Continuing individual as the integration unit.** Prefer an uninterrupted locally born research identity that experiences accessible resources, acts voluntarily through paid Competition and Action Control, perceives subsequent bodily consequences, remembers and later revises behavior. No artificial motor script, hidden-world oracle or resurrection to force completion.
3. **Generalization before further cognitive complexity.** After a meaningful behavioral result, challenge the unchanged mechanism across prospectively fixed alternative physical contexts, competing/recurrent needs, resource depletion and genuine costs before proposing another specialized scoring rule. This does not block urgent correctness/safety fixes or completion of missing minimal ecological substrate.
4. **Periodic architectural convergence.** At milestone boundaries and before P4, audit experimental flags, overlapping mechanisms, owners and documentation. Prefer the smallest coherent I2-owned policy; consolidate or retire variants only on evidence, with explicit birth compatibility decisions and preserved historical source-locked data. Do not silently rewrite already born histories or replace negative trials.
5. **Three separate evidence claims.** Classify software/CI validity, observed motor and bodily consequences, and independently supported general scientific claims separately. A deterministic two-interpreter replay is technical reproducibility, not independent scientific replication; a single successful short life is not robust adaptation.
6. **Negative and unidentifiable cases are results.** Retain NO_ACTION, refused/vetoed actions, UC cost, exposure history, physiological trajectories, death and horizon censoring. Distinguish NO_EXPOSURE (the relevant situation was never encountered) from tested failure; do not retune a frozen protocol or select only favorable lives.
7. **Budget, scope and drift control.** Use one coherent change and the narrowest valid checks. Run full CI and independently born experimental cohorts only when warranted by the changed boundary or prospective question. Signal DRIFT DETECTED with evidence, proposed reduction and next falsifier when complexity or CI volume grows without increasing scientific information.
8. **No new activation authority.** The native local research prototype is not registered living I2, independently witnessed M11 or scientific G3/G4-G6 GO. No external actuation or automatic engine promotion follows from this ADR.

## Consequences and owner of truth

[The I2 method](../../src/i2/docs/SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT_METHOD.md) is the sole operational procedure. [The P0-P5 roadmap](../../src/i2/docs/INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md) keeps task order and scientific milestones; [master issue #921](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/921) keeps live coordination. Root and I2 agent entrypoints link these sources rather than reproducing an independent checklist. Existing completed studies, source pins, negative evidence and gates are not rewritten by this documentation decision.

Method adoption is **documentation-only**: cheap static/navigation checks; no I2 behavior change, new experimental birth, forced CI campaign, registered engine, M11 approval or cross-generation promotion.

## Individuals / axes concerned

I2 research and the cross-cutting architecture/integration, memory/learning, homeostasis/motivation, action/decision and experimental-method axes. I1 remains a historical comparative reference only.
