# ADR-0016: I3 generalization without imposed behavioral rules

## Context and source

On 6 November 2026 the owner asked to integrate the supplied I3 scenario into
development. The [unaltered report](../../src/i3/docs/GENERALIZATION_SCENARIO_SOURCE_2026-11-06.md)
is dated and source-pinned to `e4eb3e80c9dc48e131e05383d952909bd86213b7`
(PR #1058); SHA-256
`e3a282e839c597cd610682fecb852340dd30f96ea8038874bebd2367b0a8753c`.
Its further P5e analysis is attributed post-hoc interpretation, not a new
independently executed result. Literature-derived benefits remain hypotheses.

The implemented P1.3 funded-v2 reference has strong bounded dependence on
learned action effects and scored curiosity, while general evolutionary benefit
and stable acquired behavioral individuality remain unestablished. P5e does
not show generally defective Competition and does not explain P5d's masked-arm
carried-food non-consumption. Original evidence remains unchanged.

## Options considered

| Option | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Add rules or bonuses to rescue drinking/eating after each failure | Rejected: prescribes successful behavior rather than testing the individual's own stakes |
| Rewrite Competition as the next primary intervention | Rejected as priority: unsupported by P5e; retain a low-cost transversal audit |
| Add all proposed learning mechanisms at once | Rejected: obscures causal attribution and increases untested complexity |
| A staged, paid, first-person generalization program in one existing plan | Accepted as research direction, with one mechanism per prospective gate |

## Decision

1. Use the [existing development plan](../../src/i3/docs/DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) as
   the sole operational roadmap, continuing #1002 and #1026. The source report
   is an attributed input, not a competing current plan.
2. Evaluate new cognition through the individual's predicted cumulative bodily
   distress, including energy/vigilance costs. Replace additive curiosity,
   relevance and prudence prospectively with information value, rather than
   stacking another designer reward. No behavioral script or hidden oracle.
3. Adopt the order: Stage 0 experimental adequacy/energy/individuality/P5d;
   Stage 1 paid information value; Stage 2 paid value of computation;
   Stage 3 change detection, latent causes, then attribute factorization;
   Stage 4 successor features/delayed credit; Stage 5 learned topology/F1;
   Stage 6 multi-family persistence-selected learning dispositions.
   Competition remains a low-priority transversal audit.
4. Require one targeted change per gate, selected-genome evaluation under
   ADR-0015, exact source and prospective disjoint worlds, adverse/null results
   retained and no tuning on exposed validation. New validation allocations
   >=120 must actually be unopened; original P4 reserve 1200000..1200199 stays sealed.
5. Plan at least eight genuinely independent populations for new confirmatory
   designs and at least ten generations for Stage 6. Report realized costs as
   part of the phenotype, not fake cost parity. Directional 7/8 agreement is
   separate from significance; the plan records exact-test limitations.
6. Preserve ADR-0001 generation independence and ADR-0014 generic-only
   inheritance, irreversible death, motor authority and paid first-person
   cognition. Never transmit learned state or reset living individuals to
   manufacture a common challenge. F6 multi-individual competition is outside
   this scenario's current implementation scope.
7. Preserve the supplied source and prior current ledgers byte-for-byte, while
   clearly separating historical next-task instructions from the current plan.
   Keep undefined family R and zero-cost-control semantics explicit until
   resolved prospectively. Do not silently turn a cognitive veto into physics.

## Consequences and limits

This decision changes research priorities and developer constraints only.
It does not implement Stages 1-6, authorize validation exposure, reclassify
past outcomes, activate I3 or grant a scientific GO. I1 remains active.
No new mandatory external-signoff gate is created.
