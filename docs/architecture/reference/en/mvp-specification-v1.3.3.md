## 2. Stable requirements

### V133-MDL-01 — Typed epistemic outputs

Every predictive output MUST declare its model version, immutable source-corpus identifier, model scope (exact or partial), knowledge origin (first-person lived or external laboratory), prediction preparation time, known/unknown components, abstention reason, and whether it has ever been authorized for live planning. The partial model MUST NOT implement the existing full-state `EquipmentModel.predict` contract or claim `EquipmentPrediction` unless new evidence and a separate normative migration establish every required state component. Unknown, absent and not-computed are not false, safe or zero risk.

### V133-MDL-02 — Observable six-component prototype

The initial research variant may estimate only `dx`, `dy`, `energy`, `food`, `water` and `inventory.berry_b`, from prior observed nonterminal transitions and the visible eligible action. `integrity`, terminality, full future adjacent geography, toxic effects, motor/food safety and distal responsibility MUST remain unresolved. The initial version is restricted to observed east movement and non-ingestive B collection; another primitive requires new evidence. Even an exact six-component match grants no safety certificate.

### V133-MDL-03 — Evidence admission and no leakage

Training input MUST consist of authenticated, causally ordered *preaction observation → physically executed command → accessible outcome* receipts with original source references and version. The frozen preaction prediction MUST precede the scored heldout consequence. No withheld WorldBody, Oracle, Bench or future outcomes may be used as model inputs. External laboratory data MUST never be installed as an I0 individual's lived memory. Scientific replay, archive review and duplicated deterministic Python executions MUST retain their source-cohort identities.

### V133-MDL-04 — Conservatively fail-closed uncertainty

When a supported command lacks relevant source examples, a required visible cue, a stable empirical delta, applicable observable context, a legal numeric boundary, or adequate runtime budget, the candidate MUST abstain with a machine-auditable reason. A hidden hazard cannot be 'solved' by increasing nominal confidence. A numerical error on hidden forced failures is an error, not a reason to suppress evidence. Confidence attached to a full exact key is not calibrated safety.

### V133-MDL-05 — Isolation and action control

The research model MUST be default-OFF in live cognition, unable to dispatch commands, veto existing Stage 9 protections, replace the exact planning simulator, alter observation, or supply a predicted complete world state. Any future shadow evaluation inside an autonomous life requires separate explicit birth-time operator-approved policy, a canonical owner, bounded UC, durable receipts, authenticated M11 cold recovery and nondisruptive control/experimental matched lives. No silent insertion into Stage6 BFS or expanded planning horizon is allowed.

### V133-MDL-06 — UC and degradation

Offline laboratory CPU time is **not** I0 UC. No lived UC saving or cost has been demonstrated by A2.30-ZO/ZS. Before any live evaluation, declare deterministic positive per-operation debits, owner attribution, bounded number of candidates and search horizon, budget refusal, effect on alternative cognition and the distinction between low UC and epistemic unknown. Refused work produces an explicit unavailable result; it never implies a negative causal attribution.

### V133-MDL-07 — Memory and lifecycle

Source identity, distinct experiment/world/life, observability, action-result chronological order and lineage remain immutable across learning, journal, consolidation and Stage 12 judgements. Preserve six private consolidation passes, cutoff/postcut handling, atomic publication, exhaustive journal and current-generation-only M11. No postmortem knowledge, rollback, restoration of deceased identity or retrospective relabeling of historical memories.

### V133-MDL-08 — Scientific activation gate

A new autonomous capability is **NO-GO** until an independently preregistered campaign demonstrates prospective predictions, controlled matched comparisons against the current exact table, meaningful actual first-person I0 choices and transitions, full external physical scoring, fail-closed hidden-lethality controls, UC charges, terminal/unknown accounting, long trajectories and independent raw-artifact audit. Positive nominal accuracy, increased plan counts or archived SHA integrity alone are insufficient. Obtain explicit operator authorization after such evidence; Stage 9 and Stage 12 validation decisions remain independent.

### V133-MDL-09 — Reproducibility and software acceptance

Python 3.11 and 3.12 tests MUST include supported nominal transitions, visible-obstacle rejection, unstable training, unsupported ingestion, body boundary abstention, hidden hazard uncertainty, source rejection, empty evidence, serialization stability, owner isolation, M11 and Stage 9/12 regressions. Archive source SHAs and identify the HEAD actually tested. Scientific statuses MUST remain distinct from test execution, PR merge and norm publication.

### V133-MDL-10 — Operator change control

Increasing horizon, changing dangerous-action admissibility, using laboratory priors inside a living I0, enabling partial predictions as decisions, or altering Stage 9, WorldBody, mortality or Oracle isolation require explicit separate operator authorization and a new validation protocol. This document does **not** authorize any such change.
