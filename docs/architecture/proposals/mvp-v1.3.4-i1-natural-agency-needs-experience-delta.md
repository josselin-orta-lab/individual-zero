# Proposal: MVP v1.3.4, I1 need-driven agency without action scripts

**Status: DRAFT NORMATIVE DELTA, not adopted; no retrospective experiment amendment.** Date: 2026-10-16. This file is a proposal against normative v1.3.3, not a replacement for the unchanged protected v1.3.3 text. Applies only to an explicitly opted-in experimental I1 generation. Original I0, WorldBody and all A2.38/A2.39 experiments remain immutable.

## Motivation and precise limits

Two full physical I1 cohorts A2.38/A2.39 demonstrated a non-teleological need problem: 1,170 total real berry collections, **zero food ingestion**, repeated dominant-need refractory reviews, and hundreds of geographic moves at the expense of locally accessible water. A2.39 risk-taking H2 accessed an uncertain food location (8/8 safe) but caused death when the hidden world was perceptually identical (8/8 lethal); BASE individuals also died in the matched worlds. That contrast establishes neither danger foresight nor a defect in an incapable low-information mind. Learnable evidence, not omniscience, is the legitimate research target.

Main source `src/lab/world.py`: both food and water decay continuously each admitted physical step, but absent other damage, zero food or zero water is not currently a terminal reason. This must be specified **explicitly**, not changed by an unnoticed experimental fixture.

## Draft normative requirements subject to review

**N134-01, autonomy from innate needs:** The individual has measurable bodily state, urgent physiological pressure, temperament-dependent exploratory preference, finite UC and autobiographical experience. No stage or experiment-specific contract may hard-code a target-action sequence, assign a secondary need merely to pass a test, or guarantee preservation of life. Existing GoalManager, Stage6, Competition and Action Control remain responsible for proposals and selection.

**N134-02, epistemic humility:** An unknown cell has unknown hidden lethality. An individual may choose to enter it from first-person cues, expected opportunity, reserve and temperament while acknowledging uncertainty and a potentially fatal outcome. Absence of evidence is neither evidence of safety nor evidence of danger; failures to predict invisible hazards are not scored as cognitive misconduct. Oracle, Bench, scenario IDs, future changes and privileged WorldBody data never enter the cognitive state.

**N134-03, experience-sensitive action consequences:** Claims that an action is edible, beneficial or harmful require distinct authentic preaction experience and accessible postaction effects. External laboratory training is explicitly typed non-autobiographical. Observation, association, causal attribution, memory and learned policy change must be measured separately. Exploration, refusal and memory retrieval carry actual UC costs.

**N134-04, real nutritional closure:** Stage6 may enumerate `manger` only from perceived/carrying state and ordinary available primitive contracts. GoalManager and Competition may favor or disfavor consumption based on bodily pressure, uncertainty and experience; no `collecter→manger` forced macro or extra motor executor is permitted. Real physiological food gain, inventory decrement and possible first-encounter harm are determined solely by WorldBody after one admitted command.

**N134-05, principled tradeoff:** Competing homeostatic and exploration options must remain live under adequate UC, with documented deprivation, uncertainty and lived feedback. Sustained frontier movement is permissible if the agent actually values it, but must not be the *automatic result of selection-channel starvation* of other present feasible options. Avoid a universal needs-first override; justify any intervention through objective diagnosis and held-out comparisons.

**N134-06, H1/H2 lifecycle:** Historical `H1` (unplanifiable-goal refractory review) and `H2` (bounded unknown-movement commitment) remain *dated experimental ablation labels* only. They are **not** mandatory cognitive contracts or psychological subsystems for future I1. They may be retired or replaced on a separately versioned I1 birth fingerprint after a preregistered comparison; never silently reinterpret A2.38/A2.39 flags. Their UC receipts and negative results remain historical truth.

**N134-07, physiological terminality question:** The physiological interpretation of food=0, water=0 and energy=0 is OPEN. A separate normative decision must specify whether depletion causes graded harm, immediate terminality, or no further harm, with measured observable timescales. Current I0 physics must remain frozen. If choosing altered I1 physics, use an isolated versioned experimental WorldBody profile, prove that leak-free first-person symptoms are accessible only after actual transitions, and preregister death outcomes. Do not use a mortal condition merely to make a food-behavior benchmark green.

**N134-08, invariants retained:** No weakening of v1.3.1–1.3.3 Stage9/DC-05, M11 atomic current-generation custody, terminal closure, six private consolidation passes, hash-chained exhaustive journal, one physical transition per admitted command, irreversible death, no postmortem cognition, individual memory isolation, exact source/seed/UC/birth freeze and independent lab audit.

**N134-09, scientific activation and versioning:** New behavior is NOT declared scientifically GO until separate preregistered held-out independent mortal lives validate genuine choices, nutrition and water recovery, evolving exploration, possible harm/death, actual UC, memory provenance and longitudinal M11 restart. A green CI is necessary but insufficient. Defects diagnosed from A2.38/A2.39 cannot be corrected then judged confirmatory using those same cohorts. Proposed I1 changes default OFF until proved. Maintain a versioned conformance matrix before normative adoption.

## Proposed acceptance path

1. Audit the choice suppression mechanisms and actual WorldBody depletion contract on unchanged versions.
2. Review N134-01..09, decide zero-resource mortality explicitly and remove/reclassify H1/H2 if appropriate.
3. Implement isolated versioned I1 candidates, preserve I0 and all safety/UC guarantees.
4. Preregister distinct A2.40 physical lives and their criteria *before* births; include mixed needs, unknown lethal twins, repeated outcomes, harmed first encounters and cold restarts.
5. Run and audit independent full cohorts; publish GO/NO-GO per claim; adopt the new MVP contract only after normative review and scientific evidence.
