## Registered population and aggregate physical outcomes

Exactly **160 distinct mortal I1 births** were executed, maximum 20 steps each, 32 matched five-arm physical cases. **2,744 real physical transitions**, **24 terminal deaths** (all in the invisible-lethal food-first stratum on step 1), **0 technical life failures**, **136 lives still physically active at the finite test horizon**. The last 136 were *not* given explicit durable operator-stop receipts; a finalization/custody limitation that must not be obscured by the journal PASS.

Actual primitive counts: **1,202 waits; 790 berry collections; 688 movements; 64 drinks; 0 eating**. Total accepted cognitive UCs **130,118**, including H1 reconsideration **1,400**, strict/D2 review **336**, and novel H2 risk review **48** (16 commitments × 3). For each of those 16 food-first uncertain decisions, an additional **1 D2 UC** was accepted, both reviews stated uncertainty/no hidden label, and the actual proposed Stage6 move was admitted through normal action control. No new command was artificially injected.

All five arms were born with the same external-prior I1 Stage6 model, D1 water learning and recall on, D2 on, only designated H1/H2 factors and geographic configuration toggled. Each matched quintuplet used the same hidden physical layout and parameters. Eight safe versus hidden-lethal east-target pairs were born perceptually indiscernible. None of the 24 deaths was deleted or replaced.

Cross-arm counterexamples matter:

- In the safe food-first worlds, **BASE and H1_ONLY also reach and collect east berries 8/8**, *without A2.39 H2*. In the matched invisible-lethal worlds, **BASE, H1_ONLY and FOOD_FIRST_RISK all die 8/8**, while STRICT_UNKNOWN and H1_GEO do not. Thus F3 identifies a **paid tradeoff relative to the strict D2 ablation**, *not* an incremental capability or improved hazard discrimination over BASE. Geographic arm avoidance reflects moving elsewhere, not knowledge of the lethal east zone.
- In ACQUIRE_THEN_EAT_LOCAL, H1_ONLY, STRICT_UNKNOWN and FOOD_FIRST_RISK physically collect 8/8, but never eat. The repeated collection policy depletes berry stock while physiological food decays. Stage6 can enumerate `manger` given inventory in its candidate generator, while the live I1 experimental partial forecast supports move/collect and continues to propose collections from visible ground. This is a **strong mechanistic lead**, not a causal proof that a single stage solely caused ingestion failure: measure Stage6 plan generation, Competition, Stage9, Action Control and actual ingestion separately in a new campaign.
- In BOTH_LOCAL_GEO, the geography-on H1_GEO arm executes **20/20** exploration movements in each of its eight lives (160 movements), gains neither water nor food, and ends at mean water **15.88** and energy **28.5**. Matched H1_ONLY drinks and collects in **8/8**, with mean end water **92.8**, energy **54.78**. Across all **32 geography-enabled lives**, all **640 physical movements** were admitted from registered Stage11 geographic frontier options, with no realized food or water gain. This is a substantial homeostatic action-selection regression, not evidence of hidden danger prediction.
- STRICT_UNKNOWN performs **20 waits per life** in both food-first strata, pays D2 reconsideration UC and suffers no lethal east exposure, but obtains no food. A conservative refusal can sacrifice physiological opportunity and does not demonstrate anticipatory classification.

Descriptive within-stratum paired binary contrasts are `8/8` in their specified direction for F2 and F3 vs STRICT_UNKNOWN; F1 is `0/8`. Exact 95% binomial intervals are approximately **[0.631,1]** for 8/8 and **[0,0.369]** for 0/8; a two-sided sign probability for eight identical discordances is 0.0078125, *descriptive only*. These small, specifically constructed strata are not independent evidence of population-wide adaptive decision making; do not modify registered thresholds based on this effect size.

## Causal evidence and remaining limits

The independent audit traced all 160 journal chains and real WorldBody transition receipts. For **all 16** food-first H2 risk commitments, the journal contains a **paid D2 evaluation (1 UC), paid H2 review (3 UC), explicit uncertain fatal-outcome possibility and first-person berry cue**, followed by admitted `se_deplacer`. No such review used an observed positive water gain; hence the inherited A2.38 summary warning that *all H2 requires prior water learning* **does not describe A2.39 food-first H2**. The new food-first choice nonetheless has zero evidence of knowing the hidden hazard.

The Stage11 path was independently inspected: all **32 H1_GEO** lives register frontier proposals and all 640 admitted movement commands cite the geographic options. This supports the tested geography diversion mechanism. It does **not** show that a new homeostatic corrective arbitration succeeded.

No consumption, general food outcome learning, consolidation-dependent change, durable M11 restart or longitudinal cross-context transfer was established. All five variants share an external learned model, not self-trained I1 food consequences; Stage12 proximal decisions and autobiographical traces alone are not distal responsibility or learned safety. An identical Python 3.11 repeat, if carried out, would test software reproducibility, not independent scientific replication. The normative [MVP v1.3.3](../../architecture/reference/en/mvp-specification-v1.3.3.md) still does not authorize autonomous cognitive deployment; its I0 isolation, UC, ignorance and mortality invariants are preserved by the A2.39 I1-only change.
