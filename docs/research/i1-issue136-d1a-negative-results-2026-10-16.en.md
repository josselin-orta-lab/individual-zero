## Raw-artifact investigation (post-result explanatory, NOT preregistered success)

Independent inspection of `i136-d1-py3.11` artifacts showed every life made a Stage11 geographic `se_deplacer(north)` on the first world step, including when **water was present at the birth cell**. `drink` became the active goal only at cycle 2 of that first world step. Stage11's geographic frontier proposal crossed Competition's threshold that cycle, before Stage6 could use the now-active drink goal in a subsequent planning cycle. Since the individual had already walked off the birth source, no local `boire` trial was possible on world step 2. The same geographic priority repeated at the remote relocation step 3; all 80 first transfer-step actions were also north. In the north stratum, an experimental water trial appeared at world step 4 **only because geographic exploration happened to reach the remote water source**, but its preference score was weaker than the frontier option and was not physically selected.

The failure is therefore upstream of the proposed learning update: **motivation/action scheduling and competition of exploratory water vs geography do not permit a first positive lived sample**, not a demonstration that updating a model from a genuine bodily response is impossible. The smoke/unit/M11 tests exercised construction, persistence and negative contracts; they did not provide affirmative proof of live water acquisition. This distinction is central to #136.
