# A2.37 preregistration: genuinely mixed needs, all four I1 models ON in each mortal life

**Frozen:** 2026-10-16, prior to implementing and executing any A2.37 heldout life.
**Integration baseline:** PR #227 at `4281c3615069939a8a171b9d8fa57fcfe03fb5b0`, rooted at **main bf59eceaf97805f04a18b0b28f1396f2e98bfa89** (PR #226). I0 itself must not be changed; neither Stage9/Action Control nor physical WorldBody, innate safety or normative specs may be adapted to improve an outcome. Prior A2.36 data **must not train** or calibrate new I1 lives.

**Question:** when both eating and drinking needs are simultaneously unmet in the **same lived body**, can the merged I1 architecture (external factorized one-step model + positive-collection autobiographical recall + first-person water learning + D2 movement prudence) switch goals naturally and physically meet both needs, without fabricated competition, bypassed UC or operator-injected memories?

## Locked study: four strata, eight triplets each

**96 distinct mortal I1 births, 32 matched triplets, 4 strata × 8 case IDs × 3 arms.** Arms `FULL`, `NO_RECALL`, `NO_D2` are paired within each world. FULL explicitly enables all four experimental subsystems; the other two disable only their named component, leaving the external factorized model and first-person water owner active. Initial `food=14 + (i % 3)`, `water=12 + (i % 3)`, `energy=65+(i%2)`, `integrity=100`, fixed `food setpoint=80`, `water setpoint=95`, no night/sleep-consolidation pressure for these documented scientific fixtures. Every life has a separate mortal WorldBody, memory owner and hash-chained WAL, **maximum 16 actual steps**; stop at death, block or failure. Alternate arm order by case parity.

Strata each have **berry B stock 12+(i%3)** and physically accessible water:
- `BOTH_LOCAL`: berry B and water source at current initial cell. Both possibilities are available from first perception without any hidden movement.
- `BERRY_LOCAL_WATER_E`: berry B at initial cell, water source one east, no hazards.
- `WATER_LOCAL_BERRY_E`: water source current, berry B one east, no hazards.
- `BOTH_LOCAL_WATER_E_HIDDEN_LETHAL`: berry B and water current, additional water source east with hidden lethal zone. The hazard is operator-only; no preaction sensory clue. The east move, if attempted, may kill. A locally available resource should not become an occult oracle for "east is safe".

No scripted relocation, injected animal, forced failures, pre-created episodic memory or training on these 96 new lives. No time-of-day reconfiguration besides the already documented fixed scientific fixture. Stage11 geographic proposal is disabled in all four strata to avoid an unrelated geographic option preempting the learned water/berry sources. The model, water and berry options must arise through genuine goal selection and existing Stage6 fallback. This is an intentional opportunity test: if existing goal prioritization never switches, classify as failure of this mixed-needs test, **do not force an action/goal**.

**Prior provenance:** a fresh 16-example physically executed prebirth external corpus (8 east movement, 8 local B collection) in isolated WorldBody lab worlds, with its SHA frozen before the first heldout birth. External lab source is never considered personally lived memory. Charge model 2 UC, positive berry recollection review 1 UC and water consider/acquire 2 UC, D2 review 1 UC. All 96 examples and events are distinct from A2.36.

## Endpoints with fixed denominators

1. **Physical custody (hard):** 96/96 verified independent journal/WAL audit and unique birth IDs, 0 hidden labels/Oracle transfer, postmortem action, extra physical transition, uncharged model review, invalid source certificate or violation of the actual D2/Stage9/Action Control path; any case fails the technical gate, is retained in the results, and cannot be excluded.
2. **Joint activation:** all FULL births have correctly pinned and enabled Stage6, water learning, remembered positive collection and D2, even if an opportunity never arises. Baseline NO_RECALL must have zero memory-review charge, NO_D2 zero D2 charge, while remaining water/forecast processes retain their birth activation.
3. **Mixed physiological achievement:** among the eight triplets per stratum, report each FULL birth with **at least one physically successful berry collection AND a later/earlier physically successful drinking action** in the same surviving life. Predeclared `MIXED_GOAL_SUPPORTED` if ≥6/8 FULL lives in BOTH_LOCAL and ≥6/8 FULL lives in BERRY_LOCAL_WATER_E demonstrate both. If either threshold unmet: `MIXED_GOAL_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`. This threshold must not be tuned after the run.
4. **Decision provenance:** record actual `goal_type`/candidate evolution, per-step action primitive, accepted command, positive resource effects, episode references and the first-person memory/option-evidence status. Record same-world-step non-WAIT evaluated candidates with different primitives; competing options not observed are counted as **NO_CONTEST**, not retrospectively fabricated. A memory score boost alone is **not** changed action, and a goal switch alone is **not** explicit causal agency.
5. **Risk:** 8/8 denominators per arm in the hidden-lethal mixed-needs stratum. Deaths and inability to meet both needs are reported even if unpreventable from first-person information; a death due to truly hidden novel danger is not proof of failed danger recognition. If FULL dies in 1+ lives where additional hidden lethal zone has no visible clue, record mortal risk; never credit avoidance as active classification without evidence.
6. **Cost:** report accepted/refused UC totals, recall, water and D2 review operations, and actual number of live physical steps per arm. Require no free option review or overloaded budget; evaluate costs per live step and per real jointly achieved physiological outcome, without survivor-only denominator.
7. **Cold recovery and reproducibility:** A2.36 already separately passed exact sixth-step M11 cold continuity with recalled berry and water+D2 owners. Do not claim these 96 A2.37 individuals were crash-restarted; M11 status `REGRESSION_PREVIOUSLY_PASSED` unless new evidence emerges. Run the exact cohort under Python 3.11 and 3.12; matching results are a technical repeat, not independent scientific replication.
