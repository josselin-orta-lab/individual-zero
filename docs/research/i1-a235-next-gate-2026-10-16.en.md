# A2.35 next scientific gate: decision divergence and causal limits

Status: preregistration for a future held-out experiment, **not an achieved result**. PR #222 exact-head CI 38866545941 passed on Python 3.11 and 3.12.

## Primary hypothesis
An I1-only, birth-enabled, UC-charged recalled **proximal collection success** increases the probability that a model-origin `collecter` option wins a genuinely contested Competition and receives Action Control admission, relative to paired birth-disabled mortal lives with otherwise identical visible worlds and policies.

## Critical interpretation
The current software witness yields 0.35 -> 0.45 for eligible evidence, but **all 30 actual commands are collecter**. It establishes no choice divergence and no survival gain. Do not treat model evidence as causal agency, safety or reward prediction.

## Prospective protocol
Freeze model/corpus digest, source commit, scoring constants, seeds, stratum assignment, birth configuration, pre-step schedules and analysis before births. Create separately identified paired ON/OFF lives with genuine two-option availability. Independently validate that both candidates are registered and present in competition traces before interpreting any decision effect. No experiment may mutate the live agent state to manufacture a crossing.

Predefine primary endpoint: fraction of eligible matched pairs with **different physically admitted primitive**, conditioned on an authentic pre-decision recalled, verified collection-success episode. Report intention-to-treat denominator including absent recalls, refusals, deaths, and unavailable alternatives. Secondary endpoints: physically collected units, food trajectory, terminality, per-life accepted/refused UC charges, safety vetoes, journal verification and M11 recovery fidelity. Publish raw receipts and matched-pair uncertainty; never claim GO based only on conditional successes.

## Required negative controls
- First collection failed; no bonus until a later authentic success.
- No recalled episode, stale episode, wrong action, mismatched event refs, incomplete command/transition pairing, redacted hidden failure reason.
- No resource currently visible, no UC available, safety veto and terminal death: no model-derived guarantee.
- Hidden lethal versus observationally indistinguishable nonlethal world, including externally scheduled perturbations.
- Cold M11 restart on/off at the same committed boundary, with byte-for-byte journal and next-choice consistency.

## Stop rules
Any hidden-world leak, uncharged review, postmortem write, fabricated source ref, unsafe bypass, failed M11 recovery, or mismatch between independently reconstructed first-person evidence and the emitted bonus is a hard NO-GO. No change to I0/main; leave stacked PR #222 draft until the scientific gate is independently passed.
