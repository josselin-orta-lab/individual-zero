- H1/H2/H3 evidence remains **NOT_ESTABLISHED** for a natural, generalizable integrated individual. The manually designed recency score, physical deadline rule and emergency priority are experimental mechanisms; they do not establish natural emergence. The survival reflex is **innate equipment**, compatible with autonomy at low abstraction, not a learned reward or researcher-issued motor command. Reflex removal is a mechanism ablation, not a requirement for calling the individual autonomous. See [innate-reflex contract](../../../src/i2/docs/UNSTABLE_WORLD_REAPPRAISAL_CONTRACT.md).
- The completed one-shot non-living G1-LAB 16-arm v1-r1 need/aversion study recorded 128 modeled captures, specific need/aversion differences and null comparisons, with no established general survival advantage over fixed priors. [Original execution report](../studies/i2-g1lab-need-aversion-protocol-2026-11-01/execution-report.md).
- Merged [#881](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/881) reports a predetermined **64-local-life** 2x2x2 learning/deadline/reflex factorial over two hidden food regimes, two initial energies and two finite blind-water stocks. Merged [#882](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/882) audits **96 single-mechanism first-choice contrasts** in the same deterministic configuration. Only actually matched *predecision* bodies, prior actions, affordances, subjective history, clocks and UC can support source-specific mechanism attribution. They do not support a general statistical synergy claim or causal interpretation after histories diverge. #882 exact-head Python 3.11 and 3.12 I2 software CI succeeded, workflow [41503398217](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/actions/runs/41503398217).

Its documented five-seed/four-policy panel includes a duplicate environment schedule (211=307), hence four distinct worlds. In the draft readout only reflex-only and combined policies have a right-censored energy-6 endpoint in the duplicated world, and **combined and reflex-only have identical chosen actions/endpoints**, a **null incremental composition benefit**. The proposed source and Python 3.11/3.12 job success are historical, **not merged evidence** or an independent confirmatory finding. Preserve the draft and negative evidence without claiming it passed current main.
- The no-learning switch in the isolated prototype disables **both** use of previously chosen outcomes for preference scoring **and** their use for blind-water plan-viability assessment. Therefore the factorial learning-on/off comparison does not separately identify the causal contribution of those two experiential pathways. [#883](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/883) states the unexecuted matched falsifier. Existing innate reflex activation is independently controlled; no-learning is **not** no-reflex.

I2 external M11 anti-rollback/terminal provenance, full born-engine G3, confirmatory G4/G5 and promotion G6 are **NOT_ESTABLISHED / NOT_STARTED**.
