# I1 #136 D1-B: pre-registered exploratory remedy for missing first-person water trials

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Status:** registered *after* the negative D1-A campaign and its independent raw-artifact diagnosis, but strictly *before* any D1-B code change or physical cohort. **D1-A** (`504be6c0c2b057da51d3ee4da9783c5db69e2d28`, run [38866267354](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/actions/runs/38866267354)) remains a frozen 80-life/960-physical-step negative result. D1-B is an **exploratory, explicitly tuned successor**, not posthoc D1-A success, not blinded confirmation, and never a Stage12 GO.

## New hypothesis and fixed intervention

The historical D1-A trace shows all 80 first actions were Stage11 geographic movement north, despite an active `drink` goal being adopted only during step-1 cycle 2; the geographic option crosses Competition before a water action can be proposed under the frozen goal-input rule. A local water probe in a birth-cell source was thus never executed. Mechanistic hypothesis: retain Stage11 geographic exploration, but place the initial **real physical source on the first naturally reached north cell**, and give a *separately labeled* higher preference to the uncertain water probe once direct local water and active thirst are visible. The novel preference is NOT a learned water reward or predicted safety. It exists equally in ON and OFF learning arms. A learned second-source movement remains possible only with authentic first-person evidence.

**One fixed experimental score profile:** `PROBE_SCORE=0.70`, `LEARNED_SCORE=0.72`, both explicitly non-simulation instrumental preference in existing Competition. These values were chosen in response to the D1-A geographic competition witness (geographic evidence ~0.53), are therefore tuned/developmental, and must never be presented as unbiased confirmation or empirical calibration. Do not adjust them again after observing D1-B results.

## Prospective physical allocation

Reuse the *allocation dimensions* of D1-A but create **80 NEW independent mortal I1 births / 40 matched ON-OFF pairs** (8 pairs each in nominal second-source north/east/south/west and adversarial hidden-lethal-east). All are new world and life IDs, journals, owner states and WALs. WorldBody code and physical constants unchanged. Keep explicitly developmental water setpoint `95`, initial energy `65+(i%2)`, food `80`, water `1+(i%3)`, integrity `100`, 32x32, conventional day/night, Stage11 geography enabled, low-fatigue profile and zero consolidation *motivational weight* (not disabling core consolidation). Frozen exact E baseline identical in both arms, background external food model OFF. No external prebirth water model or source map.

**Training opportunity:** water exists **only** on initially unseen `Position(2,1)` at the origin locality, not at birth `Position(2,2)`. Stage11 may voluntarily explore north; it is not forced to. It must actually enter source, then choose `boire` on the following cognitive world step. Any first-person water evidence must be admitted only when the next separate authorized capture follows the physically applied drink, with positive UC debit, same source cell, immutable original applied-command provenance. A mere preference, predictor or proposal must not count as training.

**Held-out water:** at world step **4**, the same logged before-step `set_position` external environmental relocation moves each life to `Position(12+i,12)`. Another physically distinct, previously unobserved water source is adjacent in the fixed test direction. The movement caused by `set_position` is never credited as self-agency or learned navigation. Hidden-lethal-east adds an unobservable fatal zone at that adjacent water cell. No external force performs the subsequent move or drink; each must cross normal Competition and Action Control. The intervention gives a novel *adjacent* transfer opportunity only, NOT proof of autonomous distant search.

Maximum 12 genuinely executed physical commands per life (960 total if all survive and advance). No resurrection, terminal continuation, retry after technical uncertainty or postmortem learning. Repeat the **same** preregistered cohort under Python 3.11 and 3.12 for software reproducibility, not twice the independent scientific sample.

## Frozen estimands and requirements

**Stages of mechanism** with denominators including all births: (1) voluntary geographic move to training source; (2) first actually applied `boire` and positive water gain, not forced; (3) strictly subsequent first-person model owner update based on t+1 capture, provenance verified and paid; (4) after external relocation, autonomously admitted and physically applied **learned-origin** movement into the adjacent unseen-source cell, distinct from exogenous relocation; (5) subsequently applied `boire` increasing water at that second cell. A full successful transfer requires all five. Report every ON/OFF death, missing observation, refusal, UC consumed/refused, journal/WAL/owner digest and safety uncertainty. The scorer may know external hidden world truth; living cognition may not.

**Technical HARD NO-GO:** false first-person evidence, uncharged model update, leaked hidden hazard, causal attribution of external relocation as self, postmortem action, double WorldBody apply, invalid WAL or M11 cold state, Stage9 veto bypass, fake safety/terminal certainty, unexpected collapse of I0 regression. Lethal unknown cells may kill, even when predictions never falsely guarantee safety; any new ON-vs-OFF mortality excess is independently reported as **SAFETY NO-GO**, irrespective of positive nominal transfer.

**Nominal mechanism threshold:** full transfer in at least 6/8 ON births per direction, greater than OFF, full life and UC/WAL/M11 integrity, with no hard violation. If not, `NOT_DEMONSTRATED`, even if probe and update work. If yes but hidden-lethal deaths excess, `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED_LIMITED_WITH_SAFETY_NO_GO`, never GO for live deployment or Stage12 acceptance. At any rate this is an exploratory tuned cohort, not final scientific validation. The only legitimate transfer is to the visible adjacent location following an external relocation; independent natural exploration and cold-recovered long-lives remain future gates.

**Do not change after reading D1-B outcomes:** score settings, geography opt-in, birth body, move schedule, source placement, 8 matched pairs/stratum, thresholds or failure denominators. Archive both positive and negative trajectories. I0 and external `src/lab` remain unchanged.
