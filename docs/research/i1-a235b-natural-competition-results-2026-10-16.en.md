## Integrity and reservations

WorldBody, Action Control, DC-05, Stage9, I0 main, Stage12 prior judgement and the mortal boundary were not modified. The runner reports an operator-only hidden truth outside the I1 cognition. The recorded zero error count only certifies the checked scorer conditions; it does not audit every possible hidden-input leak or fully prove the semantic relationship between every source receipt and each option.

The registered A2.35-B protocol called for complete chronological pairwise `t_monde`/`cycle` traces to independently reconstruct all option admission and competition provenance. The current scorer's `competition_traces` record lacks per-trace `t_monde` and `cycle` context, and counts natural competition conservatively from evaluated options and contemporaneous traces without independently verifying full temporal equality. Thus even the 0/32 eligibility count is a **reported scorecard result**, not proof that no concurrent options existed anywhere in raw histories. This limitation must be addressed in a new independent scorer before a positive scientific claim.
