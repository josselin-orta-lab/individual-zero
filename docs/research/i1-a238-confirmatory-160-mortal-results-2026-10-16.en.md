## Preregistered hypotheses and matched comparisons

**H1, narrow locked opportunity: GO (8/8).** Under blocked dominant thirst, H1 enabled genuinely local berry collections in 8/8 ALTERNATE_NEED lives, while the 8/8 paired BASE lives collected none. Hydration remained genuinely unfulfilled at collection; GoalManager's ordinary refractory mechanism released the blocked dominant goal, with **75** releases across eight ALTERNATE_NEED lives and no forced food command. BOTH also had 8/8 positive collections. This shows *goal reconsideration and acquisition of inventory*, not hunger relief: no one ate, food progressively decayed, and drinking remained impossible. The repeated oscillation/refractory pattern warrants separate longitudinal scrutiny.

**H2, narrow safe-world opportunity: GO (8/8) but risk/deployment NO-GO.** All eight BOUNDED_MOVE lives reached and collected berries at a genuinely unvisited safe east site; BASE did so in 0/8. BOTH similarly reached and collected in 8/8. In perceptually matched **invisible lethal** cases, **8/8 BOUNDED_MOVE and 8/8 BOTH died** on the third step, versus 0/8 deaths in the corresponding disabled arms. Thus H2 trades total avoidance of the unknown for real resource access *and real fatal exposure*. Neither successful safe movement nor abstention in the lethal control demonstrates an ability to classify hidden danger. These results **prohibit a safety/deployment GO**.

**H1 × H2 combined physiological benefit: NO-GO (0/8).** The locked mixed-needs BOTH stratum produced zero lives with both real food and water gains. Across all arms and all 160 lives, no physical food gain occurred. The safe-world H2 collection rate is unchanged by also activating H1, and the blocked-thirst H1 collection rate is unchanged by H2, so no beneficial factorial synergy is observed on these binary measures.

The matched observed risk differences are +100 percentage points for H1 collections in its blocked stratum, +100 for H2 safe east collections, and +100 for H2 invisible-lethal deaths. As descriptive sensitivity rather than an unregistered new decision rule, exact two-sided discordance/sign probabilities for 8/8 matched discordant pairs are 0.0078125 each. The exact 95% Clopper–Pearson interval for 8/8 is about **[0.631, 1.000]**, and for 0/8 **[0.000, 0.369]**. Eight matched worlds per stratum give substantial uncertainty; they do not justify extrapolation to arbitrary environments.

## Causal learning, memory and significant counterexamples

The genuine water experience produced **128** admitted first-person D1 learning receipts in **64** lives (water-local strata), with later real water acquisition; the external prebirth Stage6 training must not be relabeled autobiographical. The post-hoc archive inspection found **166** source-backed recall review events and 1,786 closed memory episodes, but these counts alone do not prove that autobiographical memory or consolidation caused the decisions. The brief protocol disabled the sleep/consolidation confounder. Stage12 provenance was recorded, not used as proof of distal responsibility.

H2's current runtime requires a **previously observed positive water effect** before it even reviews certain uncertain movements. In safe east worlds, the typical sequence was step 1 drink, step 2 drink, step 3 unknown east move, step 4 collect. In invisible-lethal counterparts, the identical first two physical/observational steps were followed by fatal east movement at step 3. This supports observed risk commitment conditional on learning water, **not general-purpose food-first exploration**.

The two authentic-geography mixed-needs strata are a separate severe counterexample. **All 64** lives chose repeated geographic frontier movement (1,280 moves across their 20-step trajectories), typically going north and then along the border despite local water and pressing physiological needs. They gained neither food nor water. The changed-lethal operator event was recorded, but **no authentic east-target visit occurred before the hazard change**. Accordingly these lives cannot evaluate stale-visit danger inference or the hypothesized cooperation of H1/H2 under an actually exercised mixed need; counting absent opportunities as successful caution or learned hazard avoidance would be scientifically invalid. Stage11 geographic frontier registration/Competition and Stage6 option availability should be audited separately rather than forcing beneficial actions.
