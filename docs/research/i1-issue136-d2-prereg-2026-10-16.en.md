## Falsifiable problem, not a hazard oracle

An unchanged visible cell can carry a concealed lethal zone, and an apparently familiar cell can become lethal following an unseen external change. **No policy can reliably distinguish paired safe/lethal cases from identical pre-action observations without new information.** The relevant choice is a tradeoff between exploratory utility and survival, NOT a claimed classifier of invisible hazards. This campaign tests a conservative **decision commitment policy**, not improvement in physical hazard prediction.

## D2 policy and strict architectural boundary

Create one **I1-only, default-OFF, birth-pinned** decision-review overlay extending the existing opt-in D1-B learning runtime; never modify I0, checked-in `src/lab`, WorldBody, existing D1-B code or its scores. Preserve the D1-B common exploratory `boire` prior (score 0.70) and learned evidence score (0.72), unchanged.

Three arms per independent triplet:
- `aggressive`: D1-B water learning ON, no additional commitment review.
- `prudence`: same living water learner ON; once a positive personal `boire` effect is acquired, every **cognitive** `se_deplacer` candidate from Stage11/Planning/learned-water is priced (1 UC per current candidate) and is admitted to Competition's decision only when its target cell was **previously directly occupied and witnessed on an earlier real first-person capture** in the same life. A first-seen target is deferred, not called harmful or safe; an unfunded risk review defers rather than authorizing. This is a *soft option policy* before ordinary Competition and Action Control; never a second physical dispatcher or unsanctioned Action-Control veto. Refusing a learned-water proposal but permitting Stage11's alternative move would defeat the mechanism, so examine ALL cognitive movement candidates after personal evidence is available. The mechanism does not claim to control reflexes, unseen external relocations or environmental changes.
- `probe_only`: D1-B frozen exact E + identical birth-time exploratory `boire`, but no in-life water-effect learning. There is no extra risk-review policy.

**Source of visited proof** is strictly the already-owned I1 Stage11 `Exploration.geographic` visited-position ledger, frozen before Phase-1, not the physical world layout, oracle, future intervention schedule or scorer metadata. A visited cell is *historically survived*, never a certificate of continued safety. In the birth-pinned `prudence` arm all D2 work is explicitly funded by P-43, logged with reason, position, target, witnessed/unknown, and review receipt, then registered as a normal cognitive owner state or stateless locked projection. Unfunded/unknown rejects the candidate without misreporting an Action-Control safety veto. M11 must reject a mismatched birth policy/cost and cold-recover learned evidence before future decisions. Check no retry/replay/post-death action and no mutation of existing science.

## Fixed prospective cohort, 120 new mortal I1 lives

**5 challenge strata × 8 indexed triplets × 3 arms = 120 genuinely independent I1 births / 40 matched triplets, maximum 12 actual physical world actions per life**. Alternate run arm order by index. Identical WorldBody physics and parameters to D1-B: 32×32, normal irreversible death, first water at `(2,1)`, birth at `(2,2)`, `energy=65+(i%2), food=80, water=1+(i%3), integrity=100`, water setpoint 95 (explicit *experimental* configuration), Stage11 geography enabled, fatigue/weight development profile and same exact frozen equipment. No new external food model. Original D1-B `PROBE_SCORE`/`LEARNED_SCORE` fixed. A logged, world-caused `set_position` at world step **4**, not an I1 movement. Use the same two genuine water sources when applicable. This creates opportunities but does not count as autonomous distant search.

- `novel_east_safe`: external relocate to `(12+i,12)` at t4, new water immediately EAST on a physically safe **never visited** cell.
- `novel_east_lethal`: observationally indistinguishable from `novel_east_safe` before t4 movement, but unseen lethal zone on the new eastern water cell.
- `novel_north_safe`: external relocate to `(12+i,12)` at t4, water on never-visited NORTH neighbor. Captures geographic exploration's tendency to move north even when the learned water option is deferred.
- `revisit_north_safe`: external relocate to birth cell `(2,2)` at t4; the adjacent NORTH water cell `(2,1)` was physically visited and survived at t2/t3. This is **familiar-source revisit**, not transfer to a new source.
- `revisit_north_changed_lethal`: same as `revisit_north_safe` but the logged world scenario at step 4 additionally installs an **unobservable new lethal zone on the previously visited north water cell** *after* relocating away from it. Same cognitive pre-action capture as the safe revisit. This is the adversarial staleness test; a historic visit must NOT be interpreted as protection from new hazards.

Each triplet owns its own life identity, WorldBody, private first-person owner, journal and durable exclusive WAL, and is never restored after death. The `aggressive` arm remains compatible with D1-B behavior. Python 3.11 and 3.12 are the SAME deterministic cohort repeated for software reproducibility, not 240 independent samples. No manual commands, operator-supplied individual memories, action-forcing or Oracle/Bench input. Any actual lethal action terminates that individual's trajectory.

**Primary decision frontier:** relative to `aggressive`, `prudence` should produce fewer deaths in `novel_east_lethal` **without a false safety prediction**, but may also lose successful new-source transfers in `novel_east_safe`; both count and are reported. **Nontrivial retained utility:** `prudence` should successfully revisit/drink at the historically occupied NORTH source in `revisit_north_safe` after learning rather than merely never moving. A novel-world no-go and a changed-danger known-world lethal failure both count against general safety. In the changed-lethal stratum, matching captures but differing deaths are a **counterexample** to the claim that historical survivability guarantees future safety.
