# I1 #136 D1 preregistration: water-effect learning during a mortal life

**Freeze date:** 2026-10-16. **Starting parent:** #221 at `a50333d93e3d0622d1c0a91391d5bf30891f4b5e`, rooted in distinct I1 #219. **State:** protocol committed before D1 software implementation, before D1 physical results. ## Question, estimands, mechanistic hypothesis

Can a *living* I1, after a genuinely chosen and physically applied first exploratory `boire`, admit the next authorized body observation into a life-owned empirical water-effect model, and use this same-life evidence to autonomously move towards and drink from a geographically unseen adjacent water source? This is an **observed association** claim, not guaranteed distal causal agency, physical safety, or a model of unobserved terrain. An external laboratory record must NEVER be represented as its first-person learning.

**Primary software hypothesis:** adding a default-OFF I1-only first-person learner and a minimal, common birth-time exploratory `boire` probe permits a positive source-lineaged effect record before transfer; a learned I1 should have more **autonomously selected** transfers into newly perceived water than otherwise identical probe-only fixed-E I1. A new proposal must pass ordinary Competition and Action Control, and no private operator/scorer world labels can participate in the choice. If the experiment shows no autonomous transfer, mark learning **NOT_DEMONSTRATED** regardless of successful probes or journal events.

**Common exploration prior** in BOTH arms: with an active `drink` goal and water directly perceived on the current cell, permit an explicitly uncertain, UC-paid, one-step exploratory `boire` option. This is *engineered exploratory opportunity*, NOT evidence the individual discovered the concept of drinking or autonomously learned its first action from nothing. Neither arm is given prebirth water-effect samples, a prebirth source map, or an operator-directed physical command. Only the ON arm may record the actual personally observed change and use it to pursue an adjacent *visibly* perceived water source. No automatic action dispatch, terminality or safety probability is implied.

## Fixed physical protocol and allocation

**80 independently born mortal lives, 40 matched pairs**: 8 pairs in each transfer-direction stratum `north,east,south,west`, and 8 `hidden_lethal_east` adversarial pairs. Every pair compares `learning=ON` vs `learning=OFF` with the same common exploratory action prior and exact frozen E, identical birth conditions, each own WorldBody, identity, owner state, journal, WAL, and future external input schedule. Alternate arm order with pair index parity. No inter-life learning, resurrection, command replay, operator-created memories or data-derived parameter tuning. Run at most **12 truly executed physical commands per life** (maximum 960 in one cohort); only realized moves/actions count toward the 600-step development requirement. Replications under Python 3.11 and 3.12 reproduce the SAME deterministic cohort, not two biological samples.

WorldBody remains the normal 32x32 irreversible mortal grid with unchanged physical algorithms, `day_steps=300`, `night_steps=100`, `drink_water_gain=40` and original body costs/decays. Default development Stage11 geography and Stage12 are retained. **Experimental-only** hunger context is `initial_energy=65+(i%2)`, `food=80`, `water=1+(i%3)`, `integrity=100`; Stage4 and Stage6 **water setpoints are set to 95** consistently, solely to ensure the thirst goal remains active across two ordinary 40-water-gain drinks. Other goal weights are inherited. This is a cognitive configuration perturbation and must not be described as normative baseline. Sleep development profile `fatigue_gain=0,night_weight=0,consolidation_weight=0`, same as D0. No command forcing or Oracle labels reach the individual.

Training opportunity: the birth cell `(2,2)` contains a water source, and the experiment gives the ordinary cognitive cycle an opportunity to propose `boire`; no external action is executed on the individual's behalf. The learner must wait for the NEXT actual permitted capture (t=2), match its pending applied command/position and first-person body readings, explicitly debit learning UC, and persist the source receipt. Failed, missed, terminal or confounded followups are not successful learning. There is NO external pre-birth drinking equipment. Both arms may repeat exploratory drinking while still thirsty.

**Transfer stimulus:** at world step **3**, a preregistered `set_position` environmental intervention relocates the individual to `(12+i,12)`, in an initially unobserved part of the SAME world, where a different water source is adjacent in the named test direction. The only environmental scenario injection is this logged relocation, applied at the normal before-step boundary to both arms. This repositioning is **world-caused, not an individual action or navigation success**. In the lethal stratum, the adjacent eastern water cell also contains an invisible lethal zone. The transfer action, if any, must still be selected in the living cognitive cycle by Competition and admitted by Action Control. The operator intervention creates a controlled *opportunity* to transfer; it is not autonomous discovery of a distant source and does not certify general route learning. Keep the original source at birth; no hidden test position is supplied to I1 cognition.

## Preregistered traces, acceptance and failure policy

All lives record source/prereg SHAs, owner model at birth and death, external intervention receipts separated from the individual, before/after authorized body and geography captures, physical actions, first `boire` application, next-capture source-lined learned effect, internal exact equipment digest (expected immutable), option proposal/evaluation/competition/admission references and whether the command really happened, first time water is encountered at the new cell, final water, physical death/noncompletion and all accepted/refused UC including model-owner updates. Preserve complete per-life canonical journal exports and independently audited WAL heads, without using the journal or operator truth as learning input.

**Primary endpoint (per pair, including all births):** a *learned-origin* move toward the visible newly encountered water source **after** a strictly earlier, first-person, nonterminal water-effect update, with actual WorldBody movement to the targeted cell, followed by actual `boire` with higher bodily water at that same transfer source. Both steps must be voluntary I1 actions; transfer relocation is never credited. Compare ON vs OFF with exact denominators 8 pairs per stratum; missing/blocked/dead remain denominator failures. Break out separately (a) exploratory first drink; (b) verified first-person model update; (c) learned-origin move that passes Competition/Action Control; (d) actual transfer drink; (e) goal progress and deaths; (f) UC total/delta.

**Independent secondary endpoints:** same-location learned re-use; model update temporal ordering; no model update after failed/no-change action or terminal step; exact immutable E baseline parity; external intervention only at t=3; no oracle/bench; movement and ingestion never certified safe, causal or terminal; no false attribution of exogenous relocation to self. Hard NO-GO for hidden-truth leakage, forged observed water, postmortem learning/actions, command authority bypass, double physical transition, invalid WAL/M11 or rejected budget path still updating a model. An observed adversarial death is reported as a safety failure, not retroactively interpreted as knowing about invisible danger.

**Decision:** the D1 run is a software-development exploratory cohort with preregistered allocation and endpoints. Do not choose a posthoc threshold. `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED_LIMITED` requires valid evidence for at least 6/8 nominal ON transfers **in each named nominal stratum**, strictly more than matched OFF transfers, no hard violation, and all owner/WAL/UC/M11 gates passing. Otherwise `NOT_DEMONSTRATED` or `NO-GO` on hard violation. Even a supported mechanism does NOT close #136 or declare Stage9/Stage12 GO: transfer is adjacent after an external relocation, not genuinely autonomous distant water search; independent unmanipulated and longer-horizon multi-world lives are still needed. A2.31 lethal disparity remains on record; no survivor-only success reporting.
