A refused open does **not** reset a current cycle or its counters.

Reject bool, float (including `1.0`), fractions/decimals, NaN, ±infinity, text, None, negative and containers **before UC arithmetic or mutation**, raising `ValueError`. An unopened Scheduler still raises the established `RuntimeError` for all `charge` attempts. Do not coerce or truncate any value.
