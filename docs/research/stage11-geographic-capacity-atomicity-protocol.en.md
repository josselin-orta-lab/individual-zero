## Precise open issue

The opt-in observed-frontier router may be invoked when the physical visit ledger has already reached `GeographicExplorationConfig.max_distinct_sites`. An indirect route across previously visited cells cannot then achieve its unvisited information objective. Searching, preflighting or proposing such transits at capacity would consume UC and risk repetitive routing with no possible novel arrival. Separately, `observe_capture` clears previously offered transit references as soon as the world step advances, **before** it checks whether a genuinely new physical position would exceed the visit-ledger capacity. On an overflow exception this could leave the owner partially mutated. Such exceptions must leave the owner snapshot unchanged.

## Fixed implementation contract

- In Stage11 **Phase-1 Planning**, before `geographic_route_search` UC, compare the length of the owner ledger **frozen before Phase 1** with the configured maximum. When full, suppress all optional routed geographical information targets and route-search charges. Record at most one bounded `stage11_geographic_route_capacity_exhausted` diagnostic per physical step; this records a capacity limitation only, not proof of danger, novelty, physical arrival, or a learning reward. The final Action Control veto and unrelated reflex/goal behaviors remain untouched.
- In `GeographicExploration.observe_capture`, validate any overflow for a **new position** before clearing offered transit state or mutating the capture ledger. A refused physical novel visit raises `ValueError`, leaves the entire owner causal state bit-for-bit unchanged, and must not silently invent a new registered site or claim that the real world has not moved. This atomic refusal is a narrow owner-state invariant, not a claim that external physical arrival beyond capacity has been safely handled by the whole runtime.
- At subcapacity, route search, ordinary charges, physically recorded surveys, Planning/Competition and the final veto operate exactly as previously. Any genuine capture is the sole reason to count a geographic site. No hidden layout truth, SafetyFacts copy or global map may enter Exploration.
