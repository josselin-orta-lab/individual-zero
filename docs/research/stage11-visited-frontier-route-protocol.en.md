# Stage 11: opt-in return toward a physically observed frontier

**Registered on 11 October 2026 before implementation, tests, or new CI.** Class: *public development-only mechanism and evaluation*, not an independent scientific, held-out, Golden or Stage 9 acceptance campaign. Sole normative source: `docs/architecture/reference/en/mvp-specification-v1.2.1.md` (§14 Exploration, §9.4 Action Control, DC-07, DC-11, INV-08 and snapshot/physical-observation boundaries). The previous [786-pass negative junction record](stage11-observed-junction-results-2026-10-11.fr.md) is immutable as historic evidence; the purpose is to test a **different, opt-in software configuration** against that baseline, not to reinterpret it.

## Hypothesis and observational separation

At a physically reached dead end, a bounded, funded Exploration service can identify a still-unvisited *frontier that was actually seen from a previously visited position*, and Planning can propose one **adjacent** transit step through a physically visited position. The resulting ordinary option is selected by Competition and rechecked by canonical Action Control. Reaching a visited transit cell **never** becomes a new geographic visit, a new learned causal observation, or direct novelty reward. A motivational contribution for reaching a previously observed *unvisited frontier* is prospective interest in that frontier, not novelty at the transit site. It must use a separate evidence reason and signal ID. No oracle, absolute map, invisible hazard, private simulator truth or unobserved arrival may be read.

## Versioned integration design

- **Strict opt-in geographic routing configuration**, separate from and backwards compatible with the existing `GeographicExplorationConfig` and its v1 owner snapshots; default Stage 9, default Stage 11, and previous geographic ablations remain byte-for-byte behaviorally unchanged.
- The existing Exploration owner records only `PerceptualCapture.adjacent` passability/creature-free local neighbors **after the Phase-1 Exploration observation UC is accepted**. Each survey stores its observed world step. Visits remain real step-opening Perception captures only, including reflex-only steps. Unknown danger classifications are never part of a survey.
- **Before Phase 1**, freeze a read-only, immutable snapshot of previously recorded surveys and confirmed visits. **In Phase-1 Planning**, only when no genuinely new adjacent target is available, first charge `geographic_route_search = 1 Planning logical UC`, then search solely this frozen physical-history snapshot for a still-unvisited observed frontier. Search is breadth-first on **previously confirmed visited sites**, with at most **8 transit hops**, lexicographic deterministic ties, never across a site not physically captured. The immediate step must remain adjacent and passable/creature-free in the **current** physical Perception capture. A stale/invalid route fails closed; reevaluate on each subsequent world step.
- An accepted route target still requires the separate existing **1-UC Planning Action Control preflight**, **2-UC Planning plan-build**, ordinary Competition, and final Action Control veto. A rejected UC may not be counted as consumed. A refused charge, degraded mode, unknown simulator prediction, failed safe-policy lookup, stale target, or lost source survey must not become positive evidence.
- The optional route target has distinct **canonical route ref**, destination = visited *next physical hop*, and an **observed but unvisited frontier objective**. A transit step produces a different learning signature and intrinsic-motivation reason from a novel adjacent frontier. No new primitive, bypass or cross-phase producer output. Version the routed owner snapshot and strictly validate/atomically restore its survey and offered-target state, including malformed route, observation chronology and duplicate position controls.
- Use the original public ten-step `junction_open` and `junction_sealed` layouts, with identical physics/needs, and a **third independent opt-in routing copy** per case; ordinary Stage 11 remains the unchanged baseline control. No replay tuning, threshold modifications, injected destination or hand-authored command sequence. The positive result (return to (2,2), then capture (2,3)) is an *observed endpoint*, not a test requirement. If the routing implementation is technically correct but the individual still does not act, document it as negative and do not force a success.

## Required checks and custody

1. Default-off v1 geographic snapshot/output remains structurally unchanged; Stage9 remains unchanged. Opt-in config/source version and snapshot have a distinct identifier.
2. One captured survey per physical world step at most; repeated cognitive cycles, proposed options and rollback do not create new surveys or physical visits; no survey from Phase-1 Exploration may affect Planning in that same phase.
3. Search uses only captured visited sites plus previously recorded local observations. Reject attempts to route through unseen sites, stale/damaged proposed targets, malformed saved paths, or to label an unknown zone as safe. Current-step locally blocked/creature-occupied transit must not be proposed.
4. Charge order: Planning `geographic_route_search` accepted before searching, `geographic_admissibility_preflight` accepted before read-only Action Control consultation, and `planning_build` before plan construction; test 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 UC budgets and observe exact deferral reasons without false claims.
5. Exact repeated public trajectories, journal and owner snapshots; physical capture sets equal geographic visit ledger; no unobserved terminal arrival; negative conditions and all hashes preserved.
6. Keep Python 3.11 full verification and Goldens distinct from Stage 11 targeted development checks. No new costly workflows.

**This experiment may show narrow bounded frontier-return behavior, but it cannot prove general graph planning, hazard learning, or long-horizon intelligence. Stage 11 stays IN PROGRESS. Stage 9 scientific held-out stays NO-GO.**
