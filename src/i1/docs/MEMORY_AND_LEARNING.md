# I1: knowledge acquisition, autobiographical memory and agency

The canonical individual begins without experiential facts inherited from broken historical cohorts. Learning is constrained by what it can sense and causally attribute, not by an operator's knowledge of the hidden environment.

## Distinguish four information layers

1. **External technical truth:** `runtime/world.py` and the unabridged technical journal are custody for the Lab and debugging. Hidden effects and the complete map remain inaccessible as beliefs.
2. **First-person experience:** `runtime/journal_views.py`, `modules/perception.py` and lived event projections admit only information the individual could observe. Technical journal completeness is not autobiographical knowledge.
3. **Internal hypotheses and predictions:** `modules/causal_model.py`, `modules/lived_prediction.py`, `modules/td_values.py` may change following lived evidence, under their own budgets and provenance. Prediction accuracy alone does not establish causal responsibility.
4. **Consolidated published forms:** `runtime/i1_cognitive_publication.py` validates owner-specific retained evidence; this is distinct from online learning, a physical world change and a newly certified safety rule.

## What an agent should investigate

Trace the originating environmental event; its first-person visible projection; the relevant episode/event reference; the cognitive owner's actual update; the UC charged; and the decision in which that update might later be consulted. If the evidence was redacted or ambiguous, the owner must preserve uncertainty.

For subjective agency, compare `modules/agency_comparator.py`, `modules/stage12_goal_agency.py` and the living outcome. A world transition that looks compatible with an intended action may have an external cause. Do not relabel observational compatibility as verified personal causation.

## Negative cases

- An externally bestowed item must not automatically establish success of an attempted collection.
- An unseen toxic effect or hazard coordinate must not become a learned hazard identity.
- A plan whose predicted outcome occurs for unrelated reasons is not thereby causally verified.
- A complete technical journal entry is not necessarily eligible for autobiographical projection.
- An ambiguous episode cannot be promoted to certain safety knowledge merely through replay.

## Evidence needed

Fresh subject identity, exact SHA, first-person observation, source event reference, owner state before/after, UC expenditure, counterfactual alternatives and an independently inspectable outcome. If the current observer does not expose an owner state, write **NOT_OBSERVED**. Historical A2/H1/H2 findings are not prospective acceptance criteria.
