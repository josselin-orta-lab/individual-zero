# I1 A1 scientific campaign: verification cadence and experiment gate

Decision approved 29 October 2026, recorded in issue #304 before this revision.

## No more full I1 CI for every aggregator adjustment

1. Develop operator-only A1 tools and tests as one coherent batch. Run Python syntax, focused unit tests, negative tests, and read-only M11 audits locally or in a **lightweight A1-specific workflow**, without launching eight A1 research lives.
2. Review the complete scientific instrument on frozen archived M11 data: SHA and source custody, eight distinct identities, exact physics and birth temperament, TD observations tied to committed Planning/Stage8/Competition, admission and world transition matching, death and censoring, all negative controls.
3. Once stable, run **one full I1 CI on the exact final SHA**, both Python versions, before the protected squash merge. If the SHA changes, the old full CI is not a validation of the new SHA. No merging from structure-only green statuses.
4. Only after the observer has been merged and its launch protocol fixed, execute the A1-P cohort on **one source SHA**. Keep all eight original SQLite archives including any failed or dead lives; no selective reruns, no model modifications based on the observed score.

## Scope of conclusions

An external monkeypatch log is not a native M11-signed reading. An observer can verify that these logs are consistent with signed Planning, Stage8, Competition and physical outcomes, but cannot turn the source floats into independently attested events by assertion. A1-P reports exploratory descriptive contrasts only. An absent or inconsistent observer yields MEASUREMENT_BLOCKED or INVALID_EXPERIMENT, never an adaptive-learning GO.

No physical I1 engine, memory, Q update, motor authority, UC, or I0 changes are authorized by this verification-policy decision. A1-P stays NOT_RUN until the final instrument and workflow are green.
