## Experiment, isolation, pairing and audit

Separate processes for each `(preset, fatigue_weight)` arm, using the actual I1 generated source and natural birth. The WAL-only experimental runner setting `--experimental-fatigue-weight` is restricted to exactly `0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3`; before any birth, in-memory research-only override modifies only the sleep-pressure coefficient and appends its identity to the birth policy version. It never edits canonical source, injects an oracle, selects actions or changes mortality. Every newborn stores its **effective** weight and journal receipt. The default CLI without that flag remains canonical 0.20. Never use the experimental override to instantiate M11 custody. The module override is process-scoped; do not combine two experimental weights in one process.

The manual / push laboratory workflow uses the six existing **immutable** physical presets: oasis, foraging, dry-valley, empty-land, hazard-edge and blocked-route. The first pilot is 2 seed blocks x 3 newborns x 4 arms x 6 worlds = 144 genuinely separate birth identities, each up to 120 steps, not an effective 144 independent *world* draws. The manually dispatchable second tier uses 10 blocks x 3 newborns x 4 arms x 6 worlds = 720 new lives, with an optional 300-step endurance horizon. Fixed world layouts mean distinct seed IDs do not constitute independent environmental realizations. Eight scenario families remain a campaign objective, **not yet fulfilled**; introduce and review delayed-memory, spatial-redistribution and exploration-novelty presets before declaring the full scientific question answered. A life ends irreversibly on death. No replay, rollback or survivor-only reweighting.

Provenance: retain the exact commit, preset SHA-256, weight, seed, life ID, body, action, WAL head, owner hash observations, death and truncation information. The observer analyzer `tools/analyze_i1_fcog_arm.py` distinguishes steps, applied actions, and contiguous sleep command runs; these command runs are not automatically equivalent to Stage10 internal sleep sessions. It verifies the source report, effective parameter, WAL head and event count, and refuses invalid custody. Pure hashes, receipts or increased sleep duration are not proxies for causal learning. All sensor fields stay external to the individual's cognition.

## Pre-registered primary endpoints, thresholds and limitations

Primary paired outcomes by preset: survival at fixed horizons (including all newborn deaths), sleep-step fraction, immediate sleep after physical wake when independently attributable, applied useful actions/step and water/food critical transitions; paired comparisons against control 0.0 and sensitivity 0.1/0.3. Secondary outcomes: real Stage10 consolidation receipts, per-owner semantic before/after and provenance, memory retrieval influencing later action, and delayed retained spatial/resource information.

An eventual GO requires: verified effective 0.2; no irreversible-custody violation; no observed emergency behavior defect with accessible resources; no sustained re-sleep pathology; statistically credible non-inferiority of survival; *and* independent evidence of nonempty consolidation/retrieval/learning in controlled tasks. Provisional numerical non-inferiority margin is -5 percentage points survival versus weight 0.0 at matched horizons, assessed at scenario level with two-sided 95% CIs. Sleep-pathology screening trigger: greater than 50% sleep steps or more than half of observed critical-alert wakes followed immediately by another chosen sleep action. These are **diagnostic triggers**, not forced behavior rules or sufficient NO-GO alone. Because fixed maps and repeated births limit world-level independence, do not treat 30 lives as 30 independent landscapes; use paired cluster/bootstrap by environment and report seed pairing separately.

A technical pass does not prove scientific GO. Record `NOT_ESTABLISHED` for semantic memory retention, autonomous attributable learning, actual influence on decision, learning curves and interval estimates **until** the additional instrumented studies and raw data are available. Do not infer improvement from counting `stage10_consolidation_committed`. Only after physical new-run outcomes exist may charts be plotted and their CIs interpreted.
