# First-divergence factorial mechanism audit

Follow-up to the full 64-life factorial in merged #881. Re-run the same
source-declared worlds, energy levels, finite blind-water stocks and three
binary mechanisms. No new physics, engine or parameter choices.

For each of the 96 ordered single-mechanism contrasts (two hidden worlds,
two energies, two stocks, four backgrounds, three switches), capture only
the FIRST actual divergent PAID choice. At that decision require identical
public energy, inventory, resources, affordances, physical clock, available
subjective chosen outcomes, UC, and previous chosen actions. Output the
two chosen actions, candidate values, paid UC and emergency-priority flags,
plus both terminal/censored observations.

If a mechanism never alters a paid action in the bounded life, record
a null first divergence rather than claiming an effect. Opposite future
physical outcomes after a first divergence are NOT prechoice matched
causal effects. The descriptive grid is deterministic and nonindependent;
neither broad synergy nor general survival is established. Never change
the preregistered parameters to produce favorable mechanisms. M11 is
not independently authenticated, and engine activation is forbidden.
