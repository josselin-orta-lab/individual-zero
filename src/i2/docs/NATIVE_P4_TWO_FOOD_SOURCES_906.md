**Prospective 2-by-2 configuration was fixed in [#906 comment 6632556735](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/906#issuecomment-6632556735) before creating this test, and after the physical substrate was proposed but before the #975 CI ended.** Exact baseline native v21 merged main `930e6cd19a77bfdd18307852d62993d9fab8268b`, technically accepted from [#975](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/975) at same head Python 3.11/3.12 PASS. This new panel is exploratory, not a held-out or confirmatory general result. Original negative and favorable cohorts remain unchanged.

## Frozen individual and physical world

Four independently born and paid native SQLite research-only individuals, maximum 96 actually committed ticks or true biological death, not stitched episodes. Same birth `Body(85,35,35,100)`, `BirthTemperament(0,.5,.3,0,0)`, existing `camp-passage-source` adjacency and water only at camp: stock 2, restock 8, +40 when genuinely consumed.

Two food sources: primary `source` and secondary `passage`, each real starting stock 1, independent 8-step restocking, both +35 actual food restoration. Counterbalanced hidden injury: `harm-passage` has passage -12 and source 0; `harm-source` has source -12 and passage 0. Within each world `learning=False` versus `True`. All four have `origin_novelty_tie=True`, `target_food_credit_enabled=False`, `contextual_movement_credit=False`, `evidence_tie_exploration=False`, `sleep_consolidation=False`, `place_return_tie=False`, `purpose_followthrough=False`. Physical laws, innate traits and abilities same for every learning pair, budget 950 UC. No motor tape, injected memory or predicted quality from birth law. Ordinary `manger` still shares historical chosen-result semantic across both sites; the lack of generalizable per-place outcome attribution is itself a possible falsifier.

## Actual primary and negative readouts

The running test `tests/i2/test_native_p4_two_food_sources_906.py` reports every real admitted motor and `NO_ACTION`, locations, local offers, site-specific meals with food gains/integrity injury, water and four-body minima, first true harmful meal, later genuine safer-site consumption, whether that safer location was personally visited before harm, UC spend, terminal death or 96-step right censor, and independent full cold replay of every individual. Analysis distinguishes researcher-known reachable alternatives from sources actually personally encountered or consumed; a physically present hidden resource is not a personally known strategy.

For each harm world compare the **first** ON/OFF motor divergence with exactly identical preceding actions and pre-decision body, prior food exposures and personal injuries, including pre-exposure and null outcomes. Between counterbalanced worlds assert the same physical choices and bodies until first actual harmful meal, never credit injury knowledge before it occurs. No postdivergence same-world causal purity is implied when body states or personal histories differ.

**Possible outcomes:** no exploration of either food, only first source visited, safe food before any injury (not learned reappraisal), harm then failure to switch, harm then genuinely paid switching and safe food, harmful repetition, dangerous starvation, terminal death, technical censor. All valid. A first acquired switch must follow personal harm/experience and a feasible alternative, but even this small selected cohort does not establish generalized adaptive survival, G3/H3 or independent M11; separate held-out maps and lifetimes would be necessary.
