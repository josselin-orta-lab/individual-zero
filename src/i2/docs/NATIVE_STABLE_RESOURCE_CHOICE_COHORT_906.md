# Native I2: prospective stable-resource action-choice cohort (#906)

**Protocol frozen 2026-11-03 BEFORE execution** by issue #906 comments
6604047708 and feasibility amendment 6604128128, under ADR-0010.
This is a research-owned exploratory source-locked **new native v9**
cohort, NOT a rewrite of #912's abrupt-reversal result.

Four one-life, one-owner SQLite cells, crossing learning ON/OFF with two
**unchanging** physical water qualities (+10 and 0). Birth body
(85 energy, 90 food, 35 water, 100 integrity), temperament (0, .5,
.3, 0, 0), sleep consolidation disabled, 300 UC, maximum 24 physical
ticks, 0 food stock, **8 initial old-water offers**, 0 recurring
refills, secondary discovered water gain +10 after the individual's
own voluntary `collecter` action (never pre-announced).
The originally written 32 old-water offers were out of the immutable
native birth bound 0..8. The correction to 8 was recorded in #906
**before any births**; this protocol does not alter the engine bounds.
No forced action, resurrection, free UC, prescribed new search
or hidden quality phase is available to the individual.

Report the entire chosen action and paid-advice trace, physical body,
source exposure, efficacy (water actually increasing after drinking),
physical access and first discovery, the first motor divergence under
matched observable predecision facts, and all null cases. Include
`NO_ACTION`, UC debits, first-encounter timing, cold replay, terminal
or right censoring. A decrease in advice score is NOT success unless
the actual chosen motor behavior and its alternatives substantiate it.
The stable-good source must remain a valid positive-control world; an
ineffective source that is never personally tried is `NON_IDENTIFIABLE`.

World quality is measured by the **researcher only after each committed
action**; the current claim may never contain the undisclosed quality.
No resource-quality changes in time. No stale or automatic retry
bonus. If previously failed resources are revisited under genuine
need, that is a competing-motive choice, NOT a predicted world
reversal. Qualitative positive and negative outcomes are preserved.
The four fixed worlds are deterministic controls, not independent
statistical replication. Natural generalized learning, independent
M11 and registered living I2 are **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.
