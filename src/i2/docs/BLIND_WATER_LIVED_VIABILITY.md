# Blind-water lived-viability experiment (2 November 2026)

Question: can the existing paid I2 owner distinguish a useful accessible
restorative option based on **its own observed net body result**, not a
researcher-published property of the world?

Scope: a fresh, opt-in `water_effect_blind=True` genesis with finite water,
the existing `restorative_water_study` physics switch hidden from the
individual, and no changes to original historical experimental cohorts.
This setting is an in-world sensory limitation, **not** a new engine mode
or migration. Both opposite physical water effects must emit the same
initial first-person affordances and evidence. On later capture, a paid
DRINK net +4 (under +6 gross restoration, -2 action energy) supports
`VIABLE`, while a paid net -2 is `UNKNOWN` rather than pretending the
world is irreversibly inert. With `learning=False`, physical feedback
remains audited, but the plan evaluator receives no chosen-result history
and cannot claim learned viability.

For this narrow model, COLLECT remains a known immediate non-restorer;
nothing general about multi-step plans, hidden future nutrition, or
long-run survival follows. The new plan evidence is bound to an actual
previous chosen-outcome reference, and the unchanged owner audit recomputes
the resulting Competition preference on every paid event. All normal
fresh Action Control, UC and modeled terminal restrictions remain.

Falsifiers: an initial known-water evidence reference, differing initial
captures across hidden water effects, a viable assessment before a paid
positive outcome, a viable result under the no-learning control, a forged
or future evidence reference, a score that cannot be audited, or a change
to existing opted-out study traces. This is a software/behavioral prototype
test, not proof of natural learning, independent M11, or scientific G3.
