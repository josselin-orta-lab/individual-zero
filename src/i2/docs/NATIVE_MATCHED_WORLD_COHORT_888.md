# I2 native learning comparison: predeclared 2 x 2 world cohort

**Engineering protocol, not scientific proof of adaptive survival.**
Follow issue #888 / ADR-0009 and integrated native body, sleep and discovery
PRs #900 and #903. The deferred dream/replay work remains in #902.

## Fixed before running

Four independently born native I2 SQLite lives. Cross two factors:
- Cognition: learned personal chosen outcomes on/off. The innate survival
  reflex is present in **both** arms.
- Physical discoverable water: **empty** (gain 0) versus **restorative**
  (gain 30). This is a birth-locked external world condition, never
  exposed to first-person valuation before a real discovery.

Hold everything else equal: initial body (energy 80, food 60, water 3,
integrity 100); five birth traits (0, 0.5, 0.3, 0, 0); old water always
ineffective, 4 old-water stock, 0 food stock, 80 starting UC, four
physical/cognitive cycles, and **sleep consolidation disabled** to isolate
the learned-experience pathway. The one search site is physically available
from birth in **all four** lives and can be tried once. Only actually
committed exploration can disclose whether new water exists.

## Predeclared observations

Audit each life cold from the birth and actual journal. Record available
actions in the first capture, four admitted physical actions, discovery
events including empty outcome, paid UC remaining, final body, terminal,
and whether newly found water is used. A positive found event can only
change the *next* first-person capture. No imagined or future result may
appear in its source claim.

Expected local software checks:
1. Same initial body, traits and physical search affordance in every arm.
2. Learned individuals may reconsider failed old drinking without being
   granted unique access to exploration. Controls retain innate reflexes.
3. Empty exploration must create **no** beneficial drink or fictional water.
4. A real found source may be used after admission, producing actual
   restoration; absent discovery must not.
5. Across the two external worlds, a no-learning individual's policy and
   body should not change when it never discovers anything.
6. Distinguish a **water-state difference at tick four** from a **survival
   advantage**, which four short cycles cannot establish.

## Hard limitations and next science gate

This is a deterministic, one-site, one-source **local G2 model** with only
four world ticks and physically simplistic sleep, food/water, locations and
fatigue. A single chosen environment is not a generalization study.
Negative-world results must not be hidden. No independent M11 attestation
or registered I2 executable is claimed. Repeated, independent worlds,
pre-registered outcome thresholds, realistic physical clock and I1/I2
physiology parity are necessary before general adaptive survival claims.

The related tests deliberately do **not** force a motor tape for the
autonomous cognition. Time, resource limits, the physical action owner and
paid safety review remain operative.
