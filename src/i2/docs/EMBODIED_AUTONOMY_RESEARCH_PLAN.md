# I2 embodied autonomy: architecture and scientific research plan

**Owner-approved direction, 3 November 2026. Research-only design and prospective observations, not an executed scientific protocol.** This page operationalizes [ADR-0013](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0013-lived-autonomy-and-experimental-restraint.md) and the scientific method [ADR-0012](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0012-i2-scientific-development-methodology.md). The existing [P0-P5 integrated-behavior roadmap](INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md) remains the **sole task order**; [master issue #921](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/921) remains its live work tracker. Do not run new cohorts, tune scores, alter birth contracts or publish a scientific GO merely because this plan was accepted.

## Scientific purpose

Do not program an individual to survive by following the researcher's preferred actions. Establish experimentally whether, given a small body and a world with meaningful costs and opportunities, a locally born I2 identity can form fallible preferences from its **own experiences**, act on them under competing present needs, revise an ineffective strategy, and perhaps find a feasible alternative without a prescribed motor sequence. Successful adaptation, suboptimal persistence, abstention and death are all observable outcomes.

Three distinct claims must not collapse into one: **technical validity** (the implementation and causal boundaries work), **behavior observed** (a particular actually admitted choice with bodily effect occurred), and **scientific adaptation** (prospectively controlled, replicated, generalizable history-dependent change beyond innate response). No existing short local trial establishes the third.

## Minimal architectural responsibilities (one continuing I2 owner)

| Responsibility | What is permitted | Invariant / falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical ecology | Persistent small graph; local resources with physically committed stock, exhaustion/renewal; true path/energy costs and lawful external dynamics | Inaccessible resource consumed, magical replenish, free movement or global hidden-world knowledge invalidates the result |
| Body and innate equipment | Four physiological channels, current need pressures, survival reflexes, born temperament | A fixed reflex/trait is never presented as a learned experience; death and technical censor remain distinct |
| Perception | Time-local, site-local first-person sensory capture and effects of personally admitted action | Distant exogenous stock or future payoffs cannot leak to the subjective observer, including through change summaries |
| Experience and memory | Existing I2-owned history of actual before/after bodily effects, action/site/context, fallible need-specific evidence | No forged exposure, unchosen reward, future inference or I1 inherited memory; avoid duplicate stores |
| Motivation and action arbitration | Existing paid Competition integrates innate need, temperament, learned valuation, physical offers and current risk | Remembering a source, intention persistence or a last-resort hypothesis cannot force a motor action or override veto |
| Consequence and lifecycle | Fresh Action Control; atomic body/UC/physical movement; later subjective feedback; cold replay and terminal integrity | No action without admission/payment, non-atomic consequence, postmortem reset or fake independent M11 attestation |

**No new module is required by this diagram.** Use the current I2 single SQLite state owner and existing decision path. If an integrated behavior is missing, inspect an actual chosen trace and source of values before inventing a planner, special-case tie bonus or second owner.

## Small world, consequential lives

Prefer a modest 3-5-site persistent ecology rather than a large procedural environment: water, food and possibly rest/shelter are physically located, locally perceivable, depletable or renewable through independent world laws. Their effects are generally stable enough to learn, but not promised forever. Routes consume bodily resources and UC; hazards and ambiguous choices can occur. The individual may discover opportunities in an order not controlled by the researcher. Experimenters may prospectively configure laws and initial worlds, **never inject those private laws into the individual's perceptions**.

Design worlds in which a strategy might cease to work (observed stock exhaustion, repeated nonrestoration, inaccessible path or competing physiological pressure) and a **physically possible** alternative might exist. Whether the individual perceives or chooses that alternative is the measurement, not an acceptance assumption. If it never encounters the necessary resource, record **NO_EXPOSURE**; if the alternative was impossible, record **NO_FEASIBLE_ALTERNATIVE**, not an inability to learn. Do not silently provide the answer as a hand-authored rescue action.

## Scientific questions and prospective falsifiers

These are **candidate** study questions mapped to existing H1/H2/H3 and P3/P4, not new numbered proof gates. Each concrete execution requires its own fixed world/birth design, primary outcomes, adequate duration, independent controls and exact-source protocol in the owning issue.

### A. Personal experience changes a real decision

**Question:** Given equivalent initial world laws and opportunity to act, does an actually experienced need-specific benefit or cost alter later paid physical choice beyond innate competition alone?

- Observe actual resource exposure, body before/after, source of evidence, offered candidates, chosen/rejected action, UC and later bodily outcome, not only score differences.
- Compare independently initialized learning ON/OFF with the same innate reflex, temperament schedule, available world laws and external events. Record package ablations when switches also affect plan viability or several pathways.
- **Falsifier/limit:** identical actions despite claimed memory relevance, no exposure, unequal physical opportunity, a choice determined entirely by innate priors, or benefit that does not translate into a later action. The outcome may legitimately be null.

### B. One life, several strategies (principal integrated milestone)

**Question:** After personally experiencing a useful resource, then a genuine loss of benefit or access, can an individual **itself** revise pursuit and attempt a distinct physically feasible strategy under continued needs?

- Preserve an uninterrupted locally born identity and recurrent needs: initial discoveries, subsequent independent resource state, actual changed sensory evidence, decision competition, potential exploration, action admission and bodily effects.
- Do not script the individual's discovery sequence, force a switch, reward a hidden destination or assume that the moment of depletion is cognitively known. An innate emergency or survival reflex may contribute but is analyzed separately from acquired memory.
- Primary observations: exposure count and evidence quality, persistence after observed failure, paid attempts at alternatives, changed use of familiar sites, recovery or continued deficit, lost UC/energy, death and censoring. Secondary outcomes: survival time and efficiency, without requiring positive survival.
- **Falsifiers and negative controls:** repeated ineffective pursuit despite a personally available alternative and comparable current needs; abandonment before the individual has any evidence of failure (cannot be credited to learned reappraisal); changed route caused only by external availability; no feasible alternative; prior NO_EXPOSURE. Failure to switch can be a valid negative observation, not an invitation to patch toward a scripted success.

### C. Individually acquired behavioral divergence (secondary exploratory question)

**Question:** Can two I2 individuals with identical architectural rules develop different, causally explainable preferences after different physically genuine experiences?

- Compare matched birth traits and world laws with prospectively controlled *exposure opportunity*, plus equal-history deterministic controls and a separate innate-temperament contrast.
- Later behavior must be interpreted with current body state, observed offers, chance/exogenous changes, selection effects and accumulated history visible to the researcher. Never fake personal memories or infer a pure memory effect when present bodily or accessible conditions differ.
- Look for both **history-sensitive differences** and regularities across comparable histories. No anthropomorphic claim of personality or consciousness follows.
- **Null limits:** divergence explained solely by temperament or resource access, difference only in internal score with no action, or noncomparable bodily need.

### D. Held-out ecological generalization

Retest unchanged learned-value and action mechanisms on prospectively reserved alternative maps, competing need schedules, depletion patterns, safe/unsafe routes and physically observable environmental changes. Keep source-locked original cohorts unchanged. New tuning after seeing a held-out failure requires a newly frozen independent follow-up; do not relabel the tuned old test as validation. A short favorable test is mechanism evidence, never general natural learning.

## Interpretation: architectural defect or individual mistake?

Use the **actual predecision subjective evidence**, valid offers, current body, candidate valuations, admitted motor effects and audit record to classify:

| Classification | Appropriate response |
| --- | --- |
| **ARCHITECTURAL / PHYSICAL INVALIDITY:** hidden-world leakage, physically impossible offer, incorrect body transition, unsafe admission, free UC, broken memory provenance or replay | Correct the causal bug; mark affected scientific observations invalid until independently rerun |
| **INTEGRATION GAP:** experience is valid and feasible actions are offered but the decision sequence cannot maintain a useful objective or execute a learned revisit | Record the negative experiment; isolate competing explanations and test a minimal general mechanism, not a case-specific rescue |
| **LEGITIMATE INDIVIDUAL ERROR:** poor decision is allowed by partial evidence, needs, temperament and valid competition | Preserve it; do not automatically "fix" the individual merely because the researcher preferred another choice |
| **NO_EXPOSURE / NO_FEASIBLE_ALTERNATIVE:** the target was never encountered or no admissible effective option existed | Report what was unidentifiable; do not claim success or failure of learning |
| **TECHNICAL CENSOR / DEATH:** the test bound ended or a true terminal condition occurred | Label separately; never splice or revive lives to manufacture longitudinal evidence |

## Integration with the existing P0-P5 sequence

- **P0 / honesty and observability:** accurately document current code/gates and first-person constraints, including any [remote water-restock evidence leak (#945)](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/945). Fix validity defects before treating traces as causal science.
- **P1/P2 / actual navigation and personally experienced place value:** preserve both negative and positive source-locked return/drink outcomes. Confirm that remembered-site relevance causes *admitted* choices and genuine bodily consequences, not simply a return-score change.
- **P3 / ecological feasibility and decision coherence [#925](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/925):** physically locate water and food, apply genuine depletion/renewal and cost, and test collisions between needs. Diagnose choice ambiguity first under existing [#949](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/949) before proposing an exploratory tie mechanism. A discovered source must not be guaranteed by the experimenter.
- **P4 / longitudinal falsification [#906](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/906):** first preregister sustained, independently born ON/OFF and negative worlds for question B, with explicit exposure opportunities, actual alternative feasibility, birth-locked parameters, survival/death/censor, UC, repeat-use cost and source-locked traces. Then, if warranted, test question C and held-out D. Do not infer a long life from a 64/96/128-tick technical limit.
- **P5 / integrity and comparisons:** preserve generation independence, single ownership, real terminal/provenance tests and physical parity without inventing independently attested M11 or cross-generation GO.
- **Later:** sleep/dream consolidation [#902](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/902) remains deferred until integrated natural behavior and matched controls create a relevant question.

These are refinements of existing work, not additional execution gates or parallel tracks. The first experiment under this plan should reduce uncertainty about an observed blocker, not optimize a desired outcome.

## Handoff and stopping condition

Follow the [single I2 scientific development method](SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT_METHOD.md) for per-experiment predeclaration, exact SHA, controls, targeted software checks, independent lives, negative/positive results, held-out study and convergence review. When the unchanged architecture cannot explain behavior without a growing list of exceptional scores, explicitly report **DRIFT DETECTED**, evidence and a cheaper general falsifier to the owner.

**What counts as progress:** increased causal knowledge about how an individual uses its personal life, including why it failed, and discriminating evidence that survives realistic controls. Not higher PR count, perfect behavior, forced safety success or a green CI badge.

No file here authorizes automatic running of cohorts, activation, external actuation, a new cognitive module or published scientific GO.
