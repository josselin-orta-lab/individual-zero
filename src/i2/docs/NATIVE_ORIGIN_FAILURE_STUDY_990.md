## Precise frozen experiment

Only the six cells already preregistered in #990 are allowed: two declared passage-injury worlds (0 and -12) times three independent born arms (learning OFF/factor OFF; learning ON/factor OFF; learning ON/factor ON). Body E78/F46/W44/I94, declared temperament (0,.5,.3,0,0), 128 paid steps-or-death, 1000 UC. Ordinary food at camp restores 0 with stock 1 and replenishment every 8; second food at passage restores 20 but may injure integrity, stock 1 and replenishment every 8. Water at camp restores 35, stock 2 and replenishment every 10. Other birth flags are all OFF, including innate origin novelty. No scripted first move, forced feeding, hidden source payoffs, reset after death, new score parameters or demographic search.

**Scientific falsifier:** factor ON/OFF motor divergence **after two actually paid, nonrestoring food attempts under the same prior chosen physical history**, not a changed internal score alone. The readout distinguishes `DIVERGENCE_AFTER_MATCHED_FAILURE_WITH_FACTOR_CREDIT` (a contemporaneously signed v23 evidence suffix **on the action that was actually admitted**) from `DIVERGENCE_AFTER_MATCHED_FAILURE_NO_FACTOR_CREDIT` (including a divergence where v23 merely tagged an unchosen candidate); **neither label alone proves causal learning or improved survival**. First motor divergence before sufficient failures or after mismatched prior body/site/affordances remains separately classified. Identical motor sequences with differing body states, offered actions or physical site trajectories are marked `NO_MOTOR_DIVERGENCE_BUT_PHYSICS_UNMATCHED`, rather than presented as matched lives. Additionally the #990 *origin* hypothesis requires the **two most recent actually committed meals before divergence** to have taken place at `camp`, with unmet food need at each meal and no observed food restoration. Failed meals at `passage` can satisfy the general v23 runtime rule but are **not** evidence for this new predeclared origin-failure question; classify `DIVERGENCE_WITHOUT_TWO_FAILED_ORIGIN_MEALS`. Equal prior motors are insufficient if local prior offers differed, even when bodily trajectories are otherwise equal. These are readout validity checks only; no changes are made to how the individual perceives or chooses. Matched histories now also require parity of the predecision first-person claim context: its `goal_ref`, `subjective_evidence_refs`, and `contradicted_semantics`. Birth-specific life IDs, capture IDs, and eventual score or outcome differences are deliberately **not** compared as common evidence. If prior or current subjective contexts differ, report `DIVERGENCE_WITH_UNMATCHED_HISTORY` even where body, motor, site and offers match. This is a conservative attribution rule, not proof that the subjective observations were independent or complete. Alternative entry without consumption and food gain with integrity damage must remain distinct. Nulls, non-exposure, harmful decisions, genuine biological death and technical right censor are retained. The completed per-life readout additionally emits **nonexclusive, independently observable** labels `NO_DOUBLE_FAILURE_EXPOSURE`, `NO_ADMISSIBLE_ALTERNATIVE`, `NO_SCORE_ELIGIBILITY`, `FACTOR_INACTIVE`, `VISIT_WITHOUT_MEAL`, and `NO_SAFE_RECOVERY`. Their interpretation is guarded: no score-eligibility statement is allowed when the preregistered double origin-food failure and a novel physically offered passage move were never both present. Even when both exposures occurred, `NO_SCORE_ELIGIBILITY` is used only if every such observed window is a **measured numeric score rejection** (`NONPOSITIVE_SCORE` or `SCORE_GAP_GT_FOUR`), with none blocked by urgent reflex, missing paid frame, or another non-score gate. Mixed windows and reflex-only cases report the actual tick/reason pairs without claiming measured score ineligibility. Tagged v23 final scores have **unobservable preboost values**, so the readout lists tagged windows separately instead of imputing original score gaps. `NO_SAFE_RECOVERY` currently means no observed passage meal both increased food and avoided integrity loss, **not** that the life died or lacked any safe drinking/rest opportunity. Descriptive categories can coexist and do not assert evolutionary fitness. In particular, `FACTOR_INACTIVE` means **zero recorded v23 factor tags anywhere in the completed life** of a factor-enabled birth, not merely zero tags in #990's origin-failure opportunity window. The report separately records `factor_tag_ticks_whole_life`, `factor_tag_ticks_after_exposure`, and `factor_chosen_motor_credit_ticks_whole_life`. A v23 tag following personally failed food at `passage`, rather than origin `camp`, is still a real factor tag for lifetime accounting, but **not** evidence for the preregistered origin-failure primary contrast. A chosen-motor factor-credit flag without a tagged signed frame is rejected; factor-off births cannot have v23 tags. This distinction changes only the offline observer, never an individual's motor behavior. This is targeted prospective mechanism identification, not independent held-out adaptation or general H3/G3/M11 GO.

## Frozen cross-world premeal negative control

The preregistered #990 question also requires **no effect of the unknown passage food injury before consumption**. The offline runner now emits `crossworld_premeal_negative_controls` for all three otherwise identical arms in the zero-injury and -12-injury worlds, comparing committed motors, before/after body, local sites, offered moves and first-person claim context.

- `PREMEAL_CROSSWORLD_MISMATCH`: paid motor, predecision evidence or non-meal body differs before any passage food consumption. This is a potentially invalid matched-world contrast, not evidence of successful anticipation.
- `MATCHED_THROUGH_FIRST_PASSAGE_MEAL`: predecision state and **the first passage meal action** match. The post-meal body is deliberately not compared because the hidden injury can first affect physiology at this boundary.
- `MATCHED_WITHOUT_PASSAGE_MEAL`: no passage meal in either completed life, and the observed trajectories remain matched. This is not evidence of successful alternative feeding.
- `PREMEAL_DIFFERENT_LIFE_LENGTHS`: an otherwise matched observed prefix ends before a passage meal in one world. This is a censor/length distinction, not proof of learning.

This is a **researcher-only readout** and adds no organism knowledge, environment oracle, motor authority, born flags or score adjustment. Differences found retrospectively do not justify retuning the frozen worlds or repeating #990 in search of a favorable result.

## Explicit command (only after #989 integration and reviewed protocol)

```sh
python tools/i2_p4_origin_failure_study_990.py \
  --execute-protocol-990 \
  --expected-head FULL_40_CHARACTER_REVIEWED_GIT_SHA \
  --output /outside/the/repository/unique-i2-p4-990
```

The runner rejects an omitted explicit flag, an unmatched or dirty source checkout **including untracked files** (`git status --porcelain --untracked-files=all`), an existing output directory, and output within the repository. This is a repository-level source reproducibility guard; it does not authenticate the interpreter, packages, environment or independently establish M11. It creates **one** new directory, never automatically retries, and retains all original native SQLite journal files as well as separately cold-audited, SHA-256-receipted archival copies. For completed lives it writes researcher-only full signed per-tick trace, diagnostic output, receipt, per-life status and cohort comparison. It retains originals after study completion. The per-tick trace includes genuine body before/after, observed local offers, committed movement/consumption, final scores with source evidence, terminal and signed admission reference. The trace does **not** infer unchosen consequences or expose hidden future world information to any individual.

Before publishing a completed per-life readout, the runner rechecks the archived file's SHA-256 against its custody receipt and compares that receipt's exact source/archive paths, revision, horizon, death/censor flag, remaining UC, final site and body against the independent native audit. It checks every archived receipt again immediately before publishing a `COMPLETED` cohort. These checks detect ordinary replaced/misattributed files, but are not a cryptographic external M11 witness and do not guarantee protection against concurrent malicious replacement. A study error writes `status.json` as ABORTED (when possible) and **preserves partial originals** for forensic analysis. Such aborted lives must not be counted as completed observations or resumed covertly as if uninterrupted. The output directory may contain sensitive local experimental artifacts; operator custody and external preservation remain separate requirements. The report's archive hash does not authenticate anti-rollback or external witness M11.
