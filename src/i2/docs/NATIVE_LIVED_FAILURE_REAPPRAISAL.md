# Native I2 lived-failure reappraisal: bounded research prototype

Accepted engineering direction (2 November 2026), ADR-0009 / issue #888.

Previously, the integrated I2 paid policy could keep preferring a previously
chosen action when it had twice failed to improve a vital reserve in danger.
A scalar total-needs outcome did not express whether the threatened reserve
itself was restored. This increment addresses that narrow, falsifiable
behavior without creating another owner or copying I1 cognition.

The one existing native owner reads only its own previously committed
before/after body evidence. If food, water, or integrity is at or below 10,
identify the most depleted vital reserve. Two prior *nonrestoring* attempts
at the same currently accessible action lower that action's subjective
score to at most zero. One attempt does not suffice. The result is a
reconsideration hypothesis, not a claim that the world has permanently
changed or the action is logically impossible.

The innate reflex remains in both the learning and no-learning arm. Only the
learned evidence reappraisal is disabled in the no-learning arm. Ordinary
paid Competition and fresh safety/admission still decide whether any
alternative can be attempted. The alternative may also fail or kill the
individual; no unseen payoff is predicted and no success is guaranteed.
The signal and source are reproduced from the birth and lived journal by
the existing cold audit, without new mutable state, world oracle, UC owner
or motor bypass.

Focused tests: critically dehydrated inert-water life, a matched no-learning
control with the same innate reflex, restorative-water negative control,
noncritical-water negative control and past-only evidence chronology.

This is software demonstration, **not** scientifically established
natural adaptive survival. Limiting factors include a single physical
effect per cognitive capture, static source effects and no location/fatigue
parity with I1. See ADR-0009 and development issue #888.
