## Outcomes and scientific stop rules

For every committed cycle the test reads the real admitted action or NO_ACTION, reconstructed actual site, truly locally offered food/water, previous site exposure, real meals including actual per-channel food gain and integrity injury, actual first harmful meal, food need at a safe-site offer, later truly chosen safer-site meals, original paid candidate scores/evidence provenance, all four reserve minima/final, physical death versus 128-step technical right-censor, actual UC consumed and independent cold audit of the SAME SQLite owner.

All three pairwise comparisons within a hidden world report the **first motor divergence** only, with identical prior actual motor sequence, site and pre-decision body. Subsequent histories are mediated and cannot be treated as a second controlled one-step comparison. Between counterbalanced hidden worlds, physically different body effects must first arise at **genuine chosen food contact**, not from a remote injury oracle. The v22 trace tag `:lived-whole-body-site-need-relevance` identifies real paid evidence when an eligible positive tied movement occurs; a tag's absence is `NO_PAID_TIE_EXPOSURE`, not proof the individual never learned anything.

Classify NO_PERSONAL_HARM when no actual injurious meal was consumed; NO_SAFE_EXPOSURE when an alternate site was never locally offered; NO_HUNGER_AT_SAFE_OFFER when food need is not active; and NO_CHOSEN_SAFE_MEAL when an alternative was reachable/offered but never consumed. A move toward safer food alone is insufficient. Repeated damaging eating, opportunity cost, starvation, biological death and absence of difference are genuine negative outcomes. A single longer-lived or less injured individual is not proof of ecological superiority when others remain censored or more hungry.

**No scientific threshold is conditioned on a positive outcome.** No retuning the body/food/water/injury/innate conditions from readout, default activation, new site bonus, I1 import, new owner or repeated CI until a positive case appears. Even a favorable result in this fixed graph would be only limited held-out **physical-law** exploratory support, NOT independent topology generalization or H3/G3/M11.
