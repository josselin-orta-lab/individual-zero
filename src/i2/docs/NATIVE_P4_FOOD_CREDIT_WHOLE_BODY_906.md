## Frozen 2x2, no new cognitive mechanism

Four independently born paid research-only native v20 SQLite individuals, all `learning=True`, `origin_novelty_tie=True`, `contextual_movement_credit=True`, with fixed `evidence_tie_exploration=False`, `place_return_tie=False`, `purpose_followthrough=False`, `sleep_consolidation=False`. Two matched birth arms in each of two physical worlds vary **only** the already available immutable `target_food_credit_enabled=False/True`, representing general observed whole-body G2 relief versus target-food-only success/failure replacement. The two worlds vary hidden physically applied food injury `berry_integrity_delta=0/-12`.

All four share initial `Body(85,35,35,100)`, `BirthTemperament(0,.5,.3,0,0)`, camp -> passage -> source physical graph, food only at source and water only at camp, food/water initial stock 1/2 and both restock every eight actual committed ticks, genuine food +35 and water +40 when available, UC 950, and maximum 96 naturally committed ticks. No action script, rescue, clock-dependent score, new owner, reward shaping or identity reset. Physical effects remain hidden until admission.

**Primary measurement:** first actual same-world food-credit ON/OFF motor difference with identical preceding real actions and bodily state. Record personally consumed meals before that divergence, exact paid food score/evidence, competing move/rest scores and admission; distinguish prior experienced food effects from movement/innate variation. For hidden harmless/harmful laws compare only through the first physically chosen food action and its first actual injury, never impute an oracle or future knowledge.

**Secondary measurement:** all genuine movement, current site, local offers, actual meals including zero or negligible benefit, injury per meal, repeated harmful choices, bodily minima/final energy-food-water-integrity, real death tick or 96-step right censor, real UC charges and independent complete cold replay. Target-specific food successes cannot alone certify benefit for the entire body; generic net whole-body relief is also a heuristic, not established utility. All null, worsening, abstaining and dying outcomes stay in the report.
