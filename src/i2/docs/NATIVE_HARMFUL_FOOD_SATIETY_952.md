## Why this is a distinct hypothesis

#951 Python 3.12 source-locked six independently born 64-tick outcomes show
physically local food *is eaten*: at camp 1 OFF / 4 ON meals, and passage
2 OFF / 5 ON meals; source 0 OFF / 0 ON, never reached. The ON package sometimes
eats at near-saturation, receiving 1.4 or 0.0 net food while
the born food's `berry_integrity_delta=-8` still applies. For example
passage-ON tick 17 has **100 food before, 100 after**. The ON package's
terminal-free but right-censored integrity ends 66.2 (vs 84 OFF) in passage.
A local food offer is visible, but its future integrity effect is not an
oracle. This establishes a potential *personal consequence reappraisal*
problem, not automatic proof that a saturated meal is globally wrong.

## Frozen four-cell experiment

A 2x2, **four independent physically born** native paid lives, each
at most 64 committed physical ticks, food at `passage`, water at `camp`.
Cross learning OFF/ON with born food integrity change **-8.0 / 0.0**.
Every birth has body (85,35,35,100), temperament (0,.5,.3,0,0),
real water gain +40 and food gain +35, site-local stocks food=1 and
water=2, exogenous food and water refill every 8 committed ticks, UC=850,
no sleep consolidation and no evidence-tie-exploration. Learning ON carries
the already existing optional P2 package; learning OFF switches that package
OFF. This is **not** a one-component-learning causality test.

The only intended physical difference between harmful and harmless arms is
`berry_integrity_delta`; the source test explicitly checks all other
born physics match. No changing action scores, temperament, initial body,
site, first-person evidence quality, admission, UC cost or physical effect
after seeing the result. No scripted motor or paid historical reuse.
Independent cold replay and source-locked schema/tick audit are mandatory.

## Predeclared readout and falsifiers

For each independent life, report actual paid motor list, sites, all actually
observed food offers, real `manger` ticks, before/after food and integrity,
zero-gain or less-than-5-food-gain meals, `AMBIGUOUS` ticks, water/food/
integrity trajectories, UC spent, terminal vs right-censored and cold audit.
No claim of expected positive behavioral divergence. An unchanged pattern
despite repeated harmful consequences, no meal exposure or no beneficial
integrity difference is an admissible negative outcome.

If harm affects choices, the exact prior lived harm and paid arbitration
must be inspected before attributing it to experience instead of a
mechanically different immediate body condition. If food is near saturation,
an actually chosen meal with nil food gain should not be relabeled as an
adaptive success. If multiple food actions are used despite saturation,
quantify their adverse bodily tradeoff without imposing a rule forbidding
food.

This is a context-follow-up motivated by #951, not a hold-out validation.
P4 longitudinal natural learning and survival advantage, active living G3,
H3, independent M11 and generalized autonomy remain **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.
