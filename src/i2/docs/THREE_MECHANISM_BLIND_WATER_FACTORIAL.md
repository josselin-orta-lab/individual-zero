# Source-declared I2 three-mechanism factorial

This independent bounded observational experiment holds physical water
restoration (+6) **unknown until experienced**, and uses the exact existing
hidden adverse/restored berry schedules. It crosses learning on/off,
deadline policy on/off, and emergency reflex on/off (eight variants),
within two berry worlds, initial energy 6/40 and finite water stock 1/2:
64 fresh paid SQLite lives, 20 physical ticks and initial UC 60 each.

The suite reports all chosen actions, actual UC, water depletion, emergency
priority activations, death ticks and tick-20 censored observations.
For each learning toggle it computes the difference-in-differences
in the **censored-at-tick-20** binary outcome attributable to the
deadline/reflex interaction within a fixed world, stock and energy.
Such numerical interactions do **not** establish general statistical
synergy or independently prove the necessity of any mechanism. All
counterexamples, deaths and non-divergent arms remain in the output.

An additional negative-oracle check compares identical-genesis subjects
in two hidden berry worlds, asserting matching choice/score/cost while
the actual public body and personally experienced histories still match.
Once histories separate, paired-choice conclusions cease to apply.

No priors, thresholds, motors, cognitive modules, world payoffs, previous
cohort fixtures, I1, M11 status, active registry or production engine
are changed. Do not choose a successful subset after inspecting outcomes.
