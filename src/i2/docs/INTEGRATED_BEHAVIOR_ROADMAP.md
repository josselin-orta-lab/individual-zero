## The one scientific milestone

Within **one continuing born SQLite research identity**, an I2 individual must physically encounter at least two distinct sites, **personally experience** a body's need-specific consequence at one, later experience the return of a related need, and **voluntarily choose and pay for** a route back to the remembered site. Compare independent learning-ON/OFF lives with physically identical initial conditions and alternatives. Preserve actual refusals, failed returns, dead ends, risks, costs, death and horizon censoring. A site score without a motor return is insufficient.

**Latest evidence checkpoint (4 November 2026, main `6c9157371be0034b90f9fa617d061f721ba4a080`):** P1's three-site graph and paid movement are implemented; P2 supports lived site outcomes and an observed voluntary return, **but the initial return did not necessarily produce a new drink or survival benefit**. P3 has actual site-bound food/water, depletion, independent replenishment and localized evidence, though this is not a complete durable ecology. P4 [eight-life holdout](NATIVE_FOOD_CREDIT_HOLDOUT_963.md) had one matched severe-harm motor divergence at tick 17 but no death advantage; three other matched world pairs did not diverge over 96 right-censored ticks. The [six-life 128-step panel](NATIVE_COMPETING_SURVIVAL_967.md) instead recorded **six genuine starvation deaths at tick 61, zero food offers, no ON/OFF motor divergence and a physically contacted water-quality reversal at tick 49** (source-locked [PR #968](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/968), CI PASS on both Python versions). This is a major **NO_EXPOSURE / competing-needs limitation**, not evidence that credit learning has failed or that it improved survival. I2 `engine.json` still has no entrypoints and declares `NOT_REGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTABLE`.

**Latest post-P4 engineering change ([#969](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/969) / [#970](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/970), main `6c9157371be0034b90f9fa617d061f721ba4a080`):** a default-OFF v20 *innate* origin-novelty tie discriminator enables one unforced, paid first move to an unvisited source in a learning-OFF pair, followed by two meals but measured bodily injury. This addresses a specific real passage-movement tie and **must not be confused with learned strategy revision**; the six #968 food-starvation lives had a different no-exposure cause and remain frozen negative evidence. Further work must study the existing physical opportunities under personally acquired evidence, not promote this one-point comparator as autonomous intelligence.

## Methodology checkpoints (ADR-0012, no new workstream)

[ADR-0012](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0012-i2-scientific-development-methodology.md) sets the decision; [SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT_METHOD.md](SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT_METHOD.md) is the single agent procedure. These checkpoints refine the existing P0-P5 sequence; they are **not** new gates for isolated prototyping or separate issues.

- **After P2 behavioral observations:** retain frozen return/no-drink and later return/second-drink results as different source-locked trials. Challenge the unchanged integrated choice mechanism under alternate site and need configurations before adding another cognitive score offset. If a physical affordance is missing, build that minimal substrate under P3 without calling the absence a learning failure.
- **During P3:** make competing food/water needs and depletion/renewal physically encounterable over one continuous identity. Report lack of food/site contact as NO_EXPOSURE. Avoid tuning the world after observing the motor sequence or treating a bundle of birth flags as one isolated learning mechanism.
- **Before and during P4:** preregister independent births, matched physics, learning/no-learning and adverse/negative controls, explicit bodily/cost/death endpoints, and **held-out** environments not used for tuning. Include a real opportunity for abandonment of an ineffective strategy and a physically feasible alternative/last resort, without forcing its choice. Do not equate a 24/64/128-step technical horizon with full ecological life.
- **At each meaningful milestone and before P4:** perform a small convergence review of redundant cognitive flags, extra owners, score sources and stale navigation text. Retire or consolidate only on evidence; preserve old genesis contracts, immutable negative reports and exact-source claims. Safety and validity fixes may proceed without a separate generalization campaign.
- **Verdict:** distinguish software PASS, observed personally motivated paid action with measured body outcome, and independently supported general adaptation. Green CI or one favorable life is not scientific GO.


## Scientific and architectural intent (ADR-0013)

The owner has accepted [ADR-0013](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0013-lived-autonomy-and-experimental-restraint.md): build conditions under which a fallible individual develops behavior through lived consequences, **not** a scripted survival policy. The [embodied-autonomy architecture/science plan](EMBODIED_AUTONOMY_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) specifies existing physical/cognitive boundaries and a **prospective P3/P4 integrated milestone**: one continuous identity may learn an initially helpful strategy, personally encounter its ineffectiveness, then voluntarily persist, reconsider, explore or attempt a physically feasible alternative/last resort. No successful switch is mandated. Separately study controlled personal-history divergence, distinguishing learned effects from innate traits and world access. Use the existing method [ADR-0012](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0012-i2-scientific-development-methodology.md); this is a refinement of P3/P4, **not** another roadmap, authorization or engine gate.


## Strict invariants

1. **Single I2 owner and generation autonomy.** The already existing native SQLite state owner contains physical position, body, temperament, UC, motor/admission, remembered consequences and terminal. No second world/memory database, no I1 code or lived memory inheritance, no backward-compatibility owner.
2. **Physical truth, first-person chronology.** Only actually accessible, visited sites and committed before/after body effects may become known. Never expose full experimenter graph, hidden resource payoff, unchosen consequences or synthetic future reward. Path and search cost real energy/UC.
3. **Need versus learned site value.** Relevance of a remembered location may change with the *current* thirst/hunger/fatigue even though its past observation does not. A site is not guaranteed stocked because it once helped. No time-only invisible revaluation or resampling bonus (ADR-0010).
4. **Competition remains owner of choices.** No `go_to_water` branch, forced exploration, prescribed visit or motor bypass to make a test pass. Born reflex, temperament, current body, learned evidence, physical accessibility, paid competition and fresh Action Control all matter.
5. **No scientific self-promotion.** Local research births are not registered I2, independent M11 or living motor authority. Technical CI proves only software under exact SHA. Report controls and repeated adverse behavior, real UC, `NO_ACTION`, deaths, technical censoring, missing site access and cold replay.
6. **DRIFT DETECTED** if the work adds novel tie rules, synthetic G2 copies, wrappers or heavy CI but cannot make real same-life, need-motivated spatial return more falsifiable. Prefer one coherent small PR with narrow tests and an explicit negative control.
