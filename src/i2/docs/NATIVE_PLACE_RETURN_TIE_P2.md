# P2 experimental remembered-place arbitration: tie-only, research birth

## Scope and provenance
Source-locked prospective hypothesis in [#924](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/924),
under ADR-0010/0011. This is a **born-locked optional v13 research mechanism**,
OFF by default and valid only in the P1 spatial world. It never directly
moves an individual or assumes that a resource is still stocked.

Before each paid Competition, the one owner's committed physical journal
yields actual water/food outcome evidence **at physically reached sites**.
Its signed mean observed body-reserve effect under a relevant past need
is weighted by the individual's **current** physiological need. Only for
a positive top-scoring tie, a single adjacent move that leads to a
uniquely most positively remembered site may receive **+1** to break
that tie. Unvisited, currently irrelevant, failed or equally favorable
sites receive no override. No reward or route preference is embedded in
site names, and the original paid Competition and admission remain in
charge. The one-point conversion is a **researcher-declared engineering
hypothesis**, not validated natural place learning.

The exact born flag is in the same SQLite genesis digest, and the
same scoring, evidence suffix and action admission are cold-replayed.
No second owner, I1 runtime, free UC, scripted travel, perfect map,
time-based resource regeneration prediction or external M11 proof.

## P2 exit gate remains open
A later source-locked study must show a **same-life personally
experienced restoration at a specific place, departure, recurrent need,
voluntary physically paid return**, against learning OFF, no contact,
ineffective site and depleted site controls. The current pure
unit readouts do **not** establish adaptive action or a live engine.
If the individual remains blocked at the passage junction, preserve
that negative and do not retune arbitrary priors to fake a return.
