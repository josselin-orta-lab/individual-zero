# I2 P2 staging: attributable personally lived effects at visited sites

**3 November 2026; P2 [#924](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/924); depends on validated P1 #928.**

This *first narrow P2 source slice* is a pure, independently testable
**read-only projection of the same born physical journal**, NOT a new
SQLite owner, autonomous site policy, resource-rich world or proof of
an individual's learned return.

The extractor walks the actually committed, ordered physical decisions.
It reconstructs the individual's site from previously admitted adjacent
moves. Only a subsequent actually executed `boire`, `boire_decouverte`,
or `manger` at that physical site can yield a site-conditioned
water/food outcome, and only while the corresponding need was unmet
at the beginning of the action. Other actions, saturated reserves,
never-visited sites, future source quality, and mere time add nothing.
Negative bodily outcomes are retained without being rewritten by an
unrelated later success.

A later increment must bind a truly site-local consumable offer,
first-person experienced effect and current need to the native
Competition, replay the same owner, and demonstrate a **voluntary
paid return** with a matched no-learning counterexample. Do not
promote this extraction-only substep to P2 complete. No I1 runtime,
motor script, guessed site reward, automatic retry, external M11
or active I2 authority.
