# I2: scientific development method (research-only)

**Operational companion to [ADR-0012](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0012-i2-scientific-development-methodology.md).** Read the [current P0-P5 roadmap](INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md), [I2 status](STATUS.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [ADR-0010](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0010-stable-world-evidence-based-association.md) and current original experiment sources. This page is a procedure for development agents, **never a behavioral script or knowledge available to the born individual**. One canonical roadmap, one method; no duplicate research authority.

## North-star question

Can an I2 individual use **only its personally experienced history and present bodily state** to adapt its own admitted choices when its first strategy stops working, including a physically feasible last resort, while competing needs, depletion, costs and uncertainty remain real? Study action and consequent bodily state, not just internal scores, recommendations or software PASS.

Do not demand an advantageous outcome from every life. A legitimate failed or terminal life may identify a critical cognitive or environmental limitation. The technical maximum step count is a censoring boundary, not evidence that an ecological lifespan has been covered.

## Development and experiment loop

1. **Diagnose the gap.** Inspect actual main/head SHAs, current issue/PR dependencies, negative/positive source-locked traces and first-person evidence at the decision point. State the shortest unresolved causal link rather than enumerating desirable modules.
2. **Freeze a falsifiable question before behavior code or experimental runs.** Specify hypothesis (H1/H2/H3 or a direct dependency), physical initial world and birth parameters, personally accessible observations, expected discriminating behavior, primary bodily/cost outcomes, alternative explanations, no-learning/negative controls, technical terminal/horizon, and what would count as NO_EXPOSURE or null. Record it in the owning issue/protocol, not a competing roadmap.
3. **Implement the smallest I2-owned change.** Reuse the existing single SQLite owner, current body, born temperament, paid Competition, Action Control and cold audit. Do not import I1 mechanisms, create an extra cognitive/world owner, script a successful sequence, leak hidden world state or manufacture a future reward. Distinguish a pure physical fixture probe from an actual chosen motor action.
4. **Validate technical correctness cheaply first.** Run focused tests on the exact candidate source, plus required boundary checks. Use full Python 3.11/3.12 I2 CI only when affected source/contracts require it. A documentation-only method edit requires static navigation checks, not a research cohort. Never describe CI as scientific replication.
5. **Observe full unscripted trajectories.** Record every admitted action, offered/unavailable candidate, refusals, NO_ACTION, position, actual before/after body reserve, need pressure, resource stock/exposure, subjective causal evidence, UC, terminal/death, right censor and independent cold replay. Compare separately born, physically matched learning-on/off and adverse/ineffective/depleted controls. If the learning switch also changes multiple experimental flags, call this a **package ablation**, not an isolated causal contribution.
6. **Interpret before repair.** Classify: TECHNICAL_PASS/FAIL, BEHAVIOR_OBSERVED/NOT_OBSERVED, NO_EXPOSURE, UNIDENTIFIABLE, and GENERAL_SCIENCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED or a properly supported narrower result. Distinguish a successful route from actually beneficial consumption, a failed strategy from never having encountered the alternative, and biological death from a technical test horizon. Preserve first negative and subsequent positive studies under their original immutable source.
7. **Generalize before adding cognitive complexity.** After a meaningful behavioral milestone, keep the tested mechanism unchanged while challenging it in independently born and prospectively specified **held-out worlds**. Vary only justified environmental dimensions, e.g. relative water/food sites, repeated physiological need, resource depletion/renewal, costs/risks, competing needs or observable environmental change. Include null and adverse outcomes. Do not tune against the held-out set and then present it as independent confirmation; create a new frozen cohort if a revision is warranted.
8. **Decide and converge.** Conclude retain / simplify / revise / defer / stop, with a concrete evidence-based reason and next falsifier. At a meaningful P2/P3 milestone and before P4, review accumulated birth flags, experiment-only tie rules, owners, candidate-value sources and handoff documents. Prefer removing redundant current pathways *only with explicit independent birth/version decisions*; old lives and historical evidence remain untouched.

## Claim ladder and stop rules

| Level | Legitimate claim | Insufficient evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | Tests, source hash, internal admission/replay and bounded physical invariants PASS | A green CI proves neither learning nor ecological success |
| Behavioral | An actually chosen paid action follows personally available evidence and produces measured physical consequences in a specific frozen trial | A score shift, synthetic advice or externally scripted action alone |
| Scientific | Replicated, prospectively controlled, meaningfully held-out tests support a clearly defined adaptive hypothesis, including adverse results and uncertainty | One short positive trajectory, duplicate deterministic interpreter runs or multiple uncontrolled changed flags |

**Stop and report DRIFT DETECTED** when a change adds only a bespoke bonus, parallel owner, redundant fixture, repeated heavy CI or administrative gate without improving a falsifiable integrated observation. Report: observed blocker, evidence/source SHA, cheaper falsifier, expected negative and recommended redirection. An exception is allowed for an actual safety, repository integrity or scientific-validity defect, or for the minimal physical substrate required by a planned test; document the exception rather than disguising it as a positive cognition result.

A **NO_EXPOSURE** trajectory means the relevant food/site/action was never naturally encountered; it cannot prove a learning failure or successful refusal. If the alternative cannot physically be reached or offered, state that identifiability limit. Biological terminal and horizon-censored lives must never be resumed as though continuous across an invalid boundary. Short lives can inform mechanism diagnosis without judging an individual's entire adaptive potential.

## Handoff for the next development agent

**Before change:** issue/dependency; exact main and head SHA; scientific question; personally observable gap; minimal code/physical intervention; frozen birth/world; primary action and body endpoints; no-learning/negative comparison; falsifier; intended focused tests and safety invariants.

**After change:** issue and exact PR/head SHA; files; checks PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN; actual chosen action trace and evidence boundary; bodily outcomes, UC, NO_ACTION, adverse/no-exposure and terminal/censor; cold audit; separate technical, behavioral and scientific verdicts; held-out/generalization status; whether mechanism converges or remains experimental; next blocker/stop decision.

For live work selection use [master #921](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/921) and the [global research coordinator](../../../docs/engineering/RESEARCH_COORDINATOR.md). The [I2 test plan](TEST_PLAN.md) is a technical testing reference, not a second method or scientific result store. This method does **not** authorize active engine registration, external actuation, authenticated M11, G3/G4-G6 promotion or new large campaigns merely to satisfy a checklist.
