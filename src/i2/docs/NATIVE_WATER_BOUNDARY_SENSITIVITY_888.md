# I2 water-threshold sensitivity: predeclared protocol

Date: 2026-11-03. Preparatory research branch, stacked after PR #905.
Related: issue #888, PRs #904 and #905. Dream research stays deferred (#902).

Purpose: Test whether an eight-tick terminal contrast is a fragile arithmetic
threshold rather than broad natural adaptive survival. Twelve independent
born I2 SQLite lives cross initial water (2.4, 3.0, 4.0), personal learning
on/off and hidden site outcome (empty 0, restorative 30). Hold energy 80,
food 60, integrity 100, five temperament traits (0, 0.5, 0.3, 0, 0),
4 ineffective old-water units, 0 food, 1000 UC, sleep consolidation OFF,
one genuinely physical search site available to ALL from birth.

Run until real terminal OR the existing eight-tick native horizon. Do NOT
fabricate an eighth action after an earlier death. Cold-audit events,
chronology, actual explorations and discoveries, paid UC, body and terminal
status. A new water source is usable only after an actual prior discovery,
and its physical benefit requires a separately admitted drink. In the
empty world no novel water action may be executed. No-learning is NOT an
exploration veto: the native innate policy may legitimately explore.

Physics-only predictions fixed BEFORE CI: empty worlds with water=2.4
die by or before tick seven; empty worlds with water=4.0 do not die from
dehydration before the tick-eight limit; initial water=3.0 is an arithmetic
boundary, not evidence of general adaptive survival. Declared score:
terminal fraction by world/learning/water and time to terminal, plus action,
discovery and water provenance, not a one-case general survival percentage.

If the score proves sensitive to birth water, retain that negative finding.
No I1 runtime dependency, no extra owner or simulator, no independent
M11/registration, and no inference of robust survival or biological dreaming.
