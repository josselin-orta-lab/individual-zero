# Native I2: a continuous, born-bounded research life

Research owner decision: issue #906 (2026-11-03). This increment is a
**lifecycle prerequisite**, not evidence of natural long-term learning.

## Scope

- `max_steps` is an **immutable birth parameter** within 1..128.
  Previously existing 8-step cohort tests still use the default 8.
- The *same* I2 SQLite owner carries every physical event, cognition,
  body change, first-person observation, UC debit, chosen-outcome history,
  and discovery across the entire life. A longer life does **not**
  concatenate rebirths, reset memory, mint UC, or grant external motor power.
- The born lifetime is included in the new, versioned genesis digest
  (`i2-native-paid-local-life-v4-born-lifetime`). Cold audit rejects
  mutations, events past the born bound, and any post-terminal transition.
  No migration or compatibility requirement for obsolete research lives.
- A terminal body still stops immediately regardless of remaining steps.
  An exhausted cognitive budget does not manufacture free cognition.
  Existing single-site discovery, finite food and water resources,
  fatigue limitations, and current simplified physiology remain intact.

## Engineering acceptance tests

- A single identity survives and cold-replays **20** successive paid
  physical decisions, beyond the former 8-step research cap, with the
  same birth traits/body and monotonically nonincreasing paid UC.
- A bound of 20 stops actions after exactly 20 commits, even without
  physical death; the former default 8 is unchanged.
- A tampered birth limit is rejected by the native immutable-genesis
  hash. Invalid limits (including booleans, floats, zero and >128)
  are rejected before new individual creation.
- No learning advantage, long-term generalization, sleep benefit,
  adaptive death avoidance or independently witnessed M11 is inferred.

## Next scientific dependency

The world itself must sustain repeated real opportunities, changes and
renewals without oracle leakage. A 20-step alive individual with finite
resources is a **technical long-life pilot**, not the scientific long
environment of #906. Next, add minimal exogenous, repeatable external
world events to the *same* SQLite history with explicit physical causality
and next-capture observation, including controls that prevent regeneration
from inventing personal success or reward.

Do not reopen dream-like replay or complicate owner architecture (#902).
