# I2 blind-water learning ablation: source-declared exploratory cohort

Compare 32 fresh independent paid SQLite lives, paired by identical 20-tick
food physics (adverse/restoration), initial energy (6/40), finite initially
blind restorative-water stock (1/2), and policy (deadline/composed). The
only within-pair intervention is the existing `learning` toggle. Every
motor action uses ordinary paid Competition/Action Control and owner
audit. No world parameters were selected based on results.

The regression prints all chosen actions, costs, water consumption,
terminal and censored outcomes, and the first action divergence between
learned and fixed-valuation owners. At that first split, public body,
affordances, physical time, UC and all prior actions must match.
Changes *after* the divergence are not matched-state causal evidence.
Prior observed food or water might both contribute to the learning
effect; do not attribute the difference solely to water learning
unless those sources are separately isolated.

This is a deterministic toy-world falsifier, not 32 independent ecological
samples, not a claim of general natural learning or indefinite survival.
Keep failures, never retune initial conditions after inspecting the CI
trace. Prior source-locked studies, I1, M11 and engine activation are
untouched.
