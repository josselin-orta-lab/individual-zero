# I2 P4: recurring hunger with two physically independent food sources

**Prospectively preregistered before code**: [#906](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/906#issuecomment-6633181437), following the completed [#976](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/976) (merged baseline `325a063656c059e2555a2ad42e067f130b8337d7`). Prior #976's four real lives were 96-step censored; learning-ON visited both sources but no second-source meal occurred. Food never fell below the hunger setpoint of 70 again after the initially satiating +35 meals; **the behavioral question of food re-exposure was underpowered**, not invalid. This new study is exploratory and the old null remains untouched.

## Immutable physical/birth contrast

Four new independent native v21 SQLite births, each `Body(85,55,35,100)`, `BirthTemperament(0,.5,.3,0,0)`, camp start and existing camp-passage-source adjacency, 128 committed maximum (or actual death), UC 1000. Camp water stock 2/refill 8/+40. One food site in passage and one in source, each independent stock 1/refill 8, both restore only **+12 food** per chosen meal so present food pressure may recur naturally. One physical world makes passage injury -12, source injury 0; the counterbalanced other makes source injury -12, passage injury 0. Actual environmental injury remains private until a paid food contact.

Paired learning OFF vs ON within each harm world; both have identical innate origin novelty enabled; target-specific food credit, contextual movement credit, personally remembered-site tie, purpose carryover, sleep consolidation and additional action tie-exploration OFF in ALL cells. No new reward, physics implementation, scripted movement, unearned observations or registered engine activation.

## What counts as meaningful evidence

`tests/i2/test_native_p4_recurrent_two_food_906.py` records actual chosen and declined actions, each reached site and genuine local food offer, before/after four reserves and food integrity injury for each consumed meal, first personal injury, truly consumed safer food **after** injury, local safe-food offers at current hunger below the setpoint, paid scores at first learning contrast with matched preceding motor and pre-body, UC, true death vs 128-step censor and independent full cold audit. It is a **test of what happens**, not a test that the individual must switch.

A safe-site movement alone is NOT safer-food consumption. A future harmless source is not subjectively known until personally exposed; a safe offer can be local with full food reserve and thus not motivate eating. An ON/OFF difference that precedes injury is not harm-sensitive learning. If all contacts remain with harmful passage despite hunger and actual safe offers, label repeated damaging strategy rather than training success. NO_HARM_EXPOSURE, NO_SAFE_FOOD_OFFER, NO_RECURRENT_HUNGER_AT_SAFE_OFFER and NO_FEASIBLE_ALTERNATIVE are separate reasons for nonidentifiability. Both genuinely terminal and 128-step right-censored bodies are valid results.

Do **not** tune this birth after output, rerun to hunt for a successful case, add food-quality knowledge or adjust the competition to force the second source. A single chosen safe meal following harm is at most a narrowly controlled exploratory behavior. General learned survival, independent M11 and active G3/H3 remain NOT_ESTABLISHED. If it fails under adequate recurrent food pressure, first inspect whether context-free `manger` outcomes fail to retain the accompanying site-specific integrity cost before considering a general origin-conditioned multidimensional memory hypothesis.
