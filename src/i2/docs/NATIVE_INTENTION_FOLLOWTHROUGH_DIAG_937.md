# P2: actual semantic intention continuity after personally learned place return

Read-only diagnostic for [#937](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/937)
following [#934](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/934) and frozen
[#933 results](NATIVE_PLACE_RETURN_RESULTS_924.md). No new
mechanism or intended behavior change, and no scientific GO.

## Audit question

The I2 cognitive fixture's `IntentionContinuity` stores a
**single action semantic** and a generic goal, not an independently
represented bodily purpose and place-use completion condition.
In native `step`, an initial `open` stores the locally available
`boire` semantic with `ttl_steps=64`; later cycles use `observe`.
The native physical capture step increases by eight per actual
tick, so this finite intention may **expire before** physiological
thirst recurs and before a site-motivated return.

The post-hoc diagnostic reads **the actual committed**
`owner_snapshot`, `intention_kind`, captures, Competition
values, paid actions and local places in a new birth with the
original exact useful-water ON parameters. It reports expiry,
suspension, presence/absence of the semantic intention and
post-return local drinking scores. The output is a **trace**,
not a planned or commanded motor sequence.

## Decision boundary

A semantic intention existing at a step does not establish it
causally controls Competition. An expired intention cannot
reasonably be described as an active persistent bodily plan.
Consequently an eventual improvement must specify a general
*need purpose* with personally observed origin and explicit
satisfaction/contradiction/priority-change boundaries, and be
paid, revision-bound and subject to the same Competition and
fresh Action Control. It may not automatically open `boire`
at camp, forbid departure or claim a hidden remaining resource
will work.

**Next:** decide a minimal bounded purpose-state contract on
the evidence of source-locked trace and run prospective
negative ON/OFF controls before changing actual action values.
The frozen #933/+40/0 baseline and the #934 no-drinking
outcome may never be rewritten or tuned retroactively.
