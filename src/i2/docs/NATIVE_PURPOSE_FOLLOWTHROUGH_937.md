# I2 P2: one-capture purpose handoff after a paid remembered return

**Research-only born experiment (3 November 2026).** Under
[#937](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/937), [#934](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/934),
ADR-0010/0011; does not certify active I2, independent M11 or G3 science.

New native v14 `purpose_followthrough=True` is **OFF by default** and
requires spatial learning plus the already opt-in experienced-place tie.
No previous native identities are migrated; legacy research behavior
is retained when disabled.

At the capture immediately following a **paid and admitted** move
whose prior **actual cognitive evidence** was
`:personally-observed-reachable-place`, reconstruct the arrival
site from the same physical journal. A continuing bodily purpose is
eligible only when this very individual previously observed a
**net positive** bodily effect from a consumed resource **at that site**,
the corresponding need is presently active, and that exact action is
now physically available. The prior chosen move's **already paid
Competition score** may be carried into that local action, but never
invented or supplemented with a fixed bonus; other options retain
their scores and fresh Action Control can veto. No command to drink
or automatic future payoff is issued. It expires after **one capture**
unless a new actually paid memory-grounded arrival supplies fresh
evidence. Local stock, failed sources and satisfied needs always
override. Both live advice and cold audit recalculate provenance
from the same SQLite cognitive and physical event sequence.

This is a deliberately tiny **source-grounded engineering hypothesis**,
not proof of general action planning or optimality. One committed move
value may remain below another rival; such a negative must not be tuned
post hoc. A future extended goal representation must be bounded,
revisable, and use the existing owner rather than a shadow intent state.

The new no-script research cohort compares purpose handoff OFF/ON
against actually useful +40 and ineffective +0 water. It reports
actual paid motor trajectories, bodily restoration, UC, scored
handoff provenance and cold replay. It does **not** assert a positive
chosen drink. The first four-life 24-tick trial #933 and diagnostic
#936/#938 are immutable source-locked references.
