# Native I2: abandon an ineffective intention from lived body evidence

In the existing native I2 SQLite paid cycle, after two personally observed
nonrestorations of a currently critical reserve, the first-person capture
can change its goal to `goal:physical-last-resort`. The existing
`IntentionContinuity.observe` then records `goal_changed` and retires the
old intention. This does NOT pretend the unsuccessful physical action has
disappeared: it remains in accessible semantics and paid Competition may
still choose it if evidence justifies doing so.

The mechanism uses the same lived-action sufficiency test as the native
nonrestoration score adjustment. No hidden world phase, second memory,
free cognition, or direct motor override. Disabled-learning control retains
innate body reflexes but has no evidence-derived goal change. A positive
restoration remains a negative control. Full cold audit reconstructs the
claim and owner transition from original birth and earlier body records.

This is bounded G2 software behavior, not proof of general impossibility
recognition, world instability inference, natural survival or M11 continuity.
The world is still a simplified nonliving local prototype. ADR-0009 / #888.
