# I2: independent native research architecture

**Verified baseline: main `8ff35c119057645f03eec7224037f38cd69216ea` (3 November 2026).** I2 remains a **proposal with no active registry or executable engine entrypoint**, `NOT_REGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTABLE`, as defined by [engine.json](../engine.json). There **is**, however, an I2-owned [native local research runtime](../runtime/native_paid_cycle.py) that can independently initialize a research identity and perform paid, internally admitted, SQLite-committed physical actions, including a modeled terminal condition. A local simulated research birth is **not** an authenticated living I2 engine or real-world actuation.

Start with [current status](STATUS.md), [owner-approved P0-P5 roadmap](INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md) and [ADR-0011](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0011-i2-integrated-spatial-life-priorities.md). Older synthetic v5 research fixtures remain relevant but cannot override actual native code.

## Native causal path and owners

1. [NativePaidI2.create](../runtime/native_paid_cycle.py) binds one independently initialized local SQLite identity to immutable body, temperament, world/resource parameters, cognition flags, lifetime maximum and UC. No I1 runtime, I1 inherited memory or obsolete owner.
2. [Native physical body](../modules/native_body_contract.py) tracks energy, food, water and integrity; [innate needs](../modules/native_physiological_needs.py) and [born temperament](../modules/native_birth_temperament.py) contribute to real-time pressures. Local offer availability is based on actual chosen/depleted resources.
3. A current [first-person claim](../runtime/native_paid_cycle.py) uses committed body evidence and personally encountered outcomes only, not experimenter-hidden source quality or future gains. [Unified I2 cognitive cycle](COGNITIVE_CONTROL_UNIFIED.md) pays UC for competition, intention and safety/admission.
4. Fresh internal motor admission and a selected physical outcome are committed in the **same** owner SQLite transaction via [physical journal](../runtime/native_physiology_journal.py). Vetoes/abstention/technical censor and death restrictions are explicit.
5. The *later* subjective capture can use observed target-need effects. The associative rule is cumulative (ADR-0010); optional born-locked tie exploration is a researcher-authored comparator, not established natural curiosity. `NativePaidI2.inspect(path).audit()` verifies local replay, **not independent M11**.

## Missing shortest-path behavior

The native world has **no traversable coordinates, site identities with experienced value, path-dependent movement or autonomous return**. `collecter` represents a single local discovery possibility and `boire_decouverte` may use a discovered quantity; this is not place memory. Implement one minimal single-owner spatial world [P1 #923](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/923), real experienced site effects conditioned by current bodily needs [P2 #924](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/924), renewable multi-need resources [P3 #925](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/925), then lived longitudinal contrasts [P4 #906](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/906). See the [detailed roadmap](INTEGRATED_BEHAVIOR_ROADMAP.md).

The I1 geographic exploration and site-value path is **scientific inspiration only**, not a reusable I2 runtime or memory owner. All choices remain paid, individually driven and checked. ADR-0010 forbids treating the passage of time as proof that a hidden resource changed; places may become attractive under recurrent needs without their historical effect changing.

## Limits, evidence, safety

[96-tick reports](NATIVE_RECURRENT_NEED_RESULTS_906.md), [24-tick negative source contrasts](NATIVE_STABLE_RESOURCE_RESULTS_906.md) and [12-tick useful/useless exploration](NATIVE_STABLE_NOVELTY_RESULTS_906.md) show specific internal real motor effects and null outcomes. None proves generic natural learning or durable survival. Native 128 ticks is a **technical bound**, not a life-science result.

No I2 engine registration, public birth authority, real hardware actuation, independent monotone M11 anti-rollback attestation or scientific G3/G4-G6 GO exists. Physical comparability with I1 [#888](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/888) and trust [#926](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/926) must be evidenced separately.
