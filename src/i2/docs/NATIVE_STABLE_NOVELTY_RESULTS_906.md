In the useful ON cell the actual paid sequence was
`boire, boire, collecter, boire_decouverte`, then eight chosen

additional spent UC**. In the useless ON cell the sequence was
`boire, boire, collecter, boire`, then eight `dormir`; the
collection found nothing. Both useless cells ended water 30.2,
not an invented gain. That does **not** prove the failed search was
free of other opportunity costs.

## Scientific boundary

The evidence shows a narrow *mechanism-mediated* behavior:
previously witnessed failure plus born exploration, at an otherwise
unresolved positive tie, can lead to a voluntarily admitted
exploratory choice and, **if** a stable real resource is present,
a subsequently paid restoration. It does not show forecasting
the hidden site's effect, generic curiosity, ecological viability,
superior expected survival over many worlds, learning under gradual
change, or independent M11 authentication.

The tie mechanism is an explicitly researcher-designed unit bonus;
its positive short-horizon case cannot be treated as a naturally
discovered law of decision-making. The original #916 negative
24-tick baseline remains unchanged.
