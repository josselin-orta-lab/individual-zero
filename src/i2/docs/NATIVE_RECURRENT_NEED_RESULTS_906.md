## Observed physical lives, exact frozen source

All four genuinely distinct local SQLite birth identities completed
96 paid committed ticks, with a successful cold replay and no terminal.
All were technically **HORIZON_CENSORED**; their survival through the
technical horizon proves neither viability beyond it nor general learning.

| World / cognition | First post-tick-12 drink | Chosen ineffective drinks after tick 12 | First recurrent critical thirst (<=10 after early recovery) | Last-resort goal observed | Final water /100 | UC spent |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Stable +10 / innate-only | Tick 32 | 0 | Never | No | 60.6 | 401 |
| Stable +10 / learning ON | Tick 13 | 0 | Never | No | 70.6 | 396 |
| Degrades to 0 / innate-only | Tick 32 | 42 | Never | No | 30.6 | 434 |
| Degrades to 0 / learning ON | Tick 13 | 42 | Never | No | 30.6 | 434 |

Each individual started at water=9 (critical) and physically restored
hydration with its first self-chosen drink at tick 1. None returned
to <=10 while observed. In the degraded worlds, every physically admitted
drink after the tick-12 change gave **zero** water gain, not hypothetical
reward. The learning arm drank degraded water at ticks 13, 15, ... 95;
the innate-only arm first encountered this at tick 32 and consumed
many further ineffective drinks. Forty-two post-change failures were
independently observed in each arm; all recorded actions were paid.

In both world pairs, the first cross-world physical difference occurred
only when a real post-change drink was chosen, at tick 13 for learning ON,
tick 32 for innate-only. Their committed physical prefixes before that
were identical (12 and 31 ticks respectively). **No hidden source
quality leaked into pre-action decisions.** The stable and degraded
learning arms differ in actions/UC through actual later physical effects,
but the degraded learning arm does not show fewer failures than the
degraded innate-only control. Both finish with 30.6 water and 434 UC
spent. In particular, the results do **not** demonstrate successful
abandonment of a degraded source due to personal experience.

## Why the proposed critical last resort did not activate

The current native policy only declares a personally nonrestoring
action as critical for goal revision while the relevant bodily reserve
is <=10 and at least two chosen, nonrestoring outcomes are available.
This protocol achieved repeated personal failures but **not recurrent
critical thirst**: water stayed above 10 until the technical horizon
despite declining from the final successful pre-change hydration.
Thus the condition for the critical last-resort branch was not met.
This is not evidence that critical-body abandonment fails when entered.

Importantly, the source remained a physical offer even when its effect
was 0. Repeated action under noncritical need highlights a narrower,
falsifiable design question: does existing past-only chosen-outcome
valuation make a sampled ineffective action less attractive than an
actually available alternative, *without* waiting for physiological
emergency? Do not conflate a subjective score difference with a chosen
action or claim a reliable learned advantage based solely on final water.
The one-time `collecter` search site was also consumed early in both
arms (one occurrence each), preventing an assumption that an untried
physical last resort necessarily remained accessible later.

## Next bounded, falsifiable intervention

1. Analyze paid `boire` opinions and the exact fresh competing offers
   on the **first** and later post-reversal captures, using the already
   replayable event journal. Record action-specific chosen failure
   attribution, actual choices, costs and continued availability.
2. Predeclare a **separate** small focused precritical contrast,
   controlling innate drives and motor opportunities, which asks
   whether two real personal nonrestorations cause voluntary avoidance
   or repeated ineffective consumption while water is still >10.
   Preserve a negative finding if innate regulation remains dominant;
   do not tune a score or impose a controller-forced alternative simply
   to make the experiment pass.
3. Separately investigate the critical-return/last-resort question in
   a new immutable born world with enough *real physiological time*
   and physical alternatives, not by retrospectively extending,
   resurrecting, or altering these four lives. Freeze the new physical
   protocol before observing a result. If an alternative has already
   disappeared, distinguish physical unavailability from cognitive
   refusal.
4. Keep finite current-capture refs (native genesis v7), the bounded
   existing chosen-result window and the full cold-auditable journal
   distinct. No second owner, I1 runtime fallback, external motor
   activation, dream replay (#902) or independent M11 claim.

**Scientific verdict:** evidence of paid, personally observed long-life
physical consequences and first-person chronological provenance, but
natural adaptive learning / superior survival / critical last-resort
adaptation all remain **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.
