## Original question, not redefined after looking at outcomes

The original [Stage-2 launch charter](STAGE2_LAUNCH.md) and [development plan](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) ask whether the individual can **choose whether one further real computation is worth its expected gain in its own body-defined distress D after paying its energy/vigilance cost**, without losing native action authority. The research slices are 2.1 optional memory replay, 2.2 optional Competition cycles and 2.3 optional simulation depth. `n_base` and `h_max` are potential ceilings, not instructed amounts of thinking. An ability to execute a formula, a cheaper budget or a score perturbation alone does not answer this question.

Three distinct scientific targets must not be swapped: (1) local native-action mediation under a specific paid information treatment; (2) total paired-policy effect on D and bodily costs, including naturally altered Competition cycles, survival and inactivity; (3) independent ecological generalization. Existing material does **not** jointly identify these targets.

## Two adversarial closure claims tested under the same rule

**Claim A: “E3 0/2 motors implies all adaptive thought is useless.” Rejected.** E3 consists of two fixed artificial informed histories, not independent born/assigned lives; the action purchase was not autonomously selected, no alternative factual World action was executed and no net bodily endpoint was measured. This falsifies its **own frozen screening gate**, not the original general question.

**Claim B: “B1 +8/96 implies adaptive thought succeeds.” Rejected.** B1 is DEVELOPMENT; physically and statistically independent sampling was not established, the 5-point non-inferiority margin was illustrative and not justified prospectively, cost in vigilance can worsen, and the full-policy contrast does not identify value of an individually purchased computation.

**Symmetric decision rule:** neither a small negative software screen nor a small favorable exploratory survival contrast can bypass a prospective estimand, independent unit, reproducible paid-body receipt, adequate inference, and scope match. Null/adverse data remain visible.

## RP-A1 decision (versioned internal triage)

- `CLOSE_BOUNDED`: admissible **only for the already frozen E1/E3 candidate-specific software gates**; not admissible as scientific completion of Stage 2.
- `DISTINCT_SOURCE_REQUIRED`: a **conditional research re-entry requirement**, not an automatically nominated new candidate. No admissible additional first-person action-relevant source is established by these studies. The exposed “past error” fixtures must not be retuned into a success claim.
- `HOLD_NO_VERDICT`: **chosen for the original general Stage-2 question**. Retain `HOLD_NO_VERDICT_FOR_GENERAL_STAGE2` as internal diagnostic and `NOT_ISSUED` as the canonical scientific verdict.

The closure logic is deterministic on documented evidence: if original estimand/scope and independence/power fail, no general positive or negative inference; if a source is only a software screen, close only the named screen; otherwise hold. The decision remains open to substantive **new, prospective** evidence, not to another internal rephrasing. No reviewer selection, signature or due date is required to enter the documentary `HOLD`.
