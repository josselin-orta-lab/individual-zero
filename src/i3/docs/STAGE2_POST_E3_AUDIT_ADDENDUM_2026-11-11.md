## F. Operational definition of Stage 2 completed

Four separately checked conditions:

1. **Technical:** reference/genome/body/motor preserved; each of replay, Competition-cycle and simulated-depth spending has a versioned, bounded, physically modeled decision mechanism **or a predeclared valid negative falsifier of its specific intended capability**. No free metareasoner, externally imposed success action, hidden World fact, unbounded search or unpaid host operation described as native cognition.
2. **Experimental:** relevant prospective controlled exposure and all original receipts, or an independently reviewed well-scoped demonstration that another experiment would not answer the intended question. Source locks, a true paid-discard control, actual action/outcome chronology, all-deaths/no-exposure, calibrated tariffs and genuine unit independence are mandatory for organism-value claims.
3. **Scientific:** accepted scoped answer to **when personally funded computation is worth the individual's own downstream physiological consequences**. Positive must clear meaningful effect, confidence and generalization; negative must truly refute the bounded proposed capability, not simply observe 0/2 synthetic motors. Infeasible/inconclusive is reportable but **is not a demonstrated scientific answer** to the broad Stage-2 hypothesis.
4. **Transition:** a signed/verifiable decision states the retained reference, results and valid scope, limits, exact provenance, what is postponed, no stale permission and a distinct Stage-3 gate. Issue closure alone cannot substitute.

## G. What can wait and why

Defer Stage 3 environmental change/latent causes/factorization, Stage 4 successor features and delayed credit, Stage 5 topology/F1, Stage 6 multi-family evolutionary generalization, mass lineage campaigns, a Competition rewrite, a new additive exploration/survival bonus and further projection-precision tuning unless a prospective decision-changing defect is identified. These are legitimate long-term questions for a self-determining, survival-dependent individual, but adding them now obscures the causal Stage-2 question and increases compute without identifying the value of thinking.

## H. Architectural recommendation and explicit final answer

**Preserve the existing architecture; make only a narrow optional and source-justified intervention if a real new causal information pathway survives the prior no-World gates.** Keep BodyLaws/CognitiveBudget, the first-person learning path, native Competition/Action Control and inherited generic dispositions. There is no source-backed necessity for a wholesale rewrite or replacing the individual with an oracle, fixed rescue script or externally rewarded decision policy.

**Shortest rigorous and least costly path to Stage-2 closure:** freeze the complete post-E3 negative-evidence dossier, request **one independent scoped methodological/futility ruling first**, and accept an honestly bounded negative closure *only if* it truly answers the original Stage-2 hypothesis within an explicitly defensible scope. Otherwise stop running specimens while the key causal mechanism is unidentified. Reopen only for **one independently justified, genuinely additional, personally acquired and fully costed information-to-native-action mechanism** that prospectively passes a new non-World falsifier; then one source-locked, physically audited paired life study, followed by confirmation **only** if a scientifically credible and affordable independent design exists. If this route cannot pass, record a negative candidate or NO_VERDICT rather than manufacturing a positive or calling the broad question answered.

This preserves emergent autonomy because neither the laboratory nor a protocol chooses the organism's motor action: the individual's own bodily stakes, prior experience, finite resources and native Competition remain responsible for its decisions, including its mistakes.
