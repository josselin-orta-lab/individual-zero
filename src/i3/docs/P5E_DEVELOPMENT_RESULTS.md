## A useful negative outcome

**Without scored curiosity, no individual collected or carried food in this new 12-life development sample.** Accordingly the original eating competition mechanism is **UNOBSERVABLE in that arm**, rather than being a repeat of the P5d cohort where 11 foods were collected and none eaten. This is an important ecology/genome-history dependence. Do not condition on post-intervention food acquisition and then call the resulting normal-only meal competition a treatment effect.

In the normal arm, **five** of the twelve lives carried food, yielding **342** paid and scored direct-meal opportunities. Three ecological families were represented among these five lives. Two genotypes and the same known development worlds are not independent populations.

## What remains unresolved

The P5e diagnostic separates raw-score dominance, threshold competition, control admission and actual motor execution **within existing lived decisions**. In this cohort the rarity of eating is primarily associated with competing options receiving higher raw scores during normal food-carrying steps; **no mechanism attribution is possible for masked lives that never carried food**. Separately, acquired resource absence, physical travel, incomplete memory of value, uncertain food harms, and need-weighted curiosity remain competing explanations. There is no common-body counterfactual across paths after the first different choice and no independent ecological/genetic inference.

**Stop:** no tuning based on these exposed development outcomes and no rerun to hunt for favorable examples. For independent scientific claims, freeze new genetic populations, genuinely novel ecology mechanisms, fresh validation indices >=120, horizon, endpoints and analysis **before** exposure.
