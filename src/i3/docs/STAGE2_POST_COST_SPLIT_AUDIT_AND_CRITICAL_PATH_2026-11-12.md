## C. Priority gaps, rival hypotheses and what they mean

**C1 / model + causal identification / P0.** A recorded error or a changed score is not necessarily usable extra information. Native TD memory lacks action keys; an old action-effects model already predicts some of the purported novelty. Rival explanations: (a) no new action-relevant information exists; (b) the wrong target was stored; (c) severe forecast tails and uncertainty miscalibration; (d) the native scorer is insensitive to that source in fixed contexts; (e) a delayed effect rather than an immediate motor difference. Reject an extra predictor unless it identifies genuinely incremental information, prospective forecast merit and either a new legal motor or a preannounced **native computation-budget choice** on unseen engineering states.

**C2 / identifiability of the treatment / P0.** Equal requested optional read tariff **does not imply equal total paid cognition** once its output changes variable-duration native Competition; 3/4 first-split observations prove a relevant concrete failure of that simplification. In a natural *total-effect* experiment, this downstream spending and the resulting future body change are genuine **mediators** and must be INCLUDED, not artificially refunded. In a *controlled direct information-to-motor* experiment, equal all-in work is a different intervention and must first be shown feasible without changing native authority. These two estimands must not be combined.

**C3 / cost accounting / P0.** Body model bills predefined work blocks; CPU copies, sorts, HMAC, forecast training/reads, error storage, memory allocation, next-learning opportunity and food/sleep conversion may differ from the tariff. Independent calibration of hardware energy may be impractical; at minimum prospectively reconcile operator counts, E/V model bill, variable native cycles, actual World settlement and lost opportunities. Never relabel modeled joules as physical CPU joules.

**C4 / experimental inference / P0.** Only a few historical genetic populations and a few F0 mechanisms. Seeds from F0 are not genuinely new physical/ecological family replicates. [Inference power audit](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/INFERENCE_POWER_AUDIT.md) demonstrates the 7/8 population sign rule can coexist with a **negative 15.42 percentage-point mean**. The B1 conditional one-sided lower bound **-0.04325165** uses unapproved retrospective iid/margin assumptions: it cannot license a 5-point survival non-inferiority claim. Death, terminal, censor, failed funding and nonexposure must stay all-assigned.

**C5 / scientific status drift / P0.** Numerous deterministic diagnostics and PRs do not fill the missing first-person acquired-information -> truly chosen next computation/action -> fully settled net organism outcome chain. Repeated rescoring of these four familiar DEV trajectories now risks selecting favorable hypotheses after exposure. Declare **DRIFT DETECTED** if another downstream no-World accounting study is proposed without a frozen novel discrimination or independent methods answer.

**C6 / engine release / separate P1.** [#1331](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1331) and [#1339](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1339) on I3 native identity/durable M11/anti-rollback belong to technical runtime promotion, not evidence that Stage 2 cognition succeeded.

## E. Experimental program, controls and decision rule

**E0 / immediately, zero births.** Independent methods audit on evidence already frozen. Reproduce source-locked aggregate checks READ ONLY only where receipt validity is in dispute; distinguish deterministic tests from new ecological observations and keep first CI failures. Explicitly decide whether E3's 0/2 and the replay B1/80/81 contrasts can answer any *originally stated* narrowly scoped hypothesis. No new candidate merely to turn a non-verdict green.

**E1 / conditional, zero scientific births.** One **prospective** genuinely distinct personal-information mechanism. Preannounce source, original observer-vs-native storage integrity, exact quotation and actual payment, two modes **VISIBLE** vs **PAID_DISCARD** with the same exogenous operators executed, prospective prequential quality vs predeclared cheap baseline, at least one nontrivial native motor **or endogenous compute purchase/stop** discrimination not imposed by laboratory. Observe all natural Competition cycles rather than asserting their equality. Controls: information absent, same estimated mean and scrambled action labels, null action, low-E and low-V, death, source held out, token renaming, fixed and random computation, unchanged P1.3. No F0 fixture hunting after first observed failure.

**E2 / conditional, newly authorized 48–96 DEVELOPMENT lives illustrative.** Freeze an **intent-to-treat total policy effect** on the individual's own model-defined distress D, with survival/inactivity and five-channel post-paid-learning receipts, including actual voluntary computation, variable paid Competition, energy/vigilance, food/sleep opportunity and any later action difference as mediators. Compare with a relevant treatment at the same *exogenous* source-read work: if visibility is the intervention, compare VISIBLE against genuinely computed and fully paid DISCARD; if self-selected computational **quantity** is the intervention, compare ADAPTIVE against frozen FIXED and RANDOM-count. These are separate estimands and costed panels, not interchangeable arms. Include all initially assigned units, even if the mechanism is never purchased, the source never acquired or death occurs first. A detached alternate motor is not a factual counterpart.

**E3 / conditional confirmation.** Specify independent genetic/world population unit, previously unseen *mechanistic* ecological family (not just F0 seed changes), within/between-family inference, one primary D endpoint and separately justified survival non-inferiority and inactivity checks, multiplicity, missingness/death/terminal/censor, planned sample size and confidence/decision bounds, original immutable G/R, max births and stop rule before any new source exposure. Validation and reserve must be distinct from development/selection. If no viable powered study, do not spend; state INFEASIBLE/NO_VERDICT. A negative conclusion can close a **predeclared, sufficiently powered scope**; one more negative synthetic screen cannot close all possible cognition.

## F. Cheapest defensible critical path

**Default path:** N0 -> N1 -> N7, **zero new scientific lives**. It may yield a bounded candidate negative or a justified HOLD, but **only** an independent finding that the *original Stage-2 question and tested scope match* can support full scientific Stage-2 negative closure.

**One conditional attempt if N1 identifies a genuinely new source:** N2 (zero scientific births) -> N3 -> N4 (zero scientific births) -> N5 (one small prospective pilot) -> N6 *only if warranted and powered* -> N7. **Why this is shortest:** all essentials of native bodily agency and paid learning already exist, and 24 new commits have advanced instrumentation without proving marginal information utility. The remaining decisive unknown is not another head or more tests, but a causally identified and individually affordable choice to compute that truly affects the organism, evaluated on independent exposure.

- [ ] **Scientific**: positive generalizable value-of-computation claim passes an independently reviewed prospective design, **or** a negative result genuinely refutes an original, explicitly bounded Stage-2 hypothesis with sufficient discrimination/power. - [ ] **Successor**: signed/scoped decision, exact source/status, complete null and adverse inventory, P1.3 retention/promotion rationale, downstream unknowns for Stage 3 and separate I3 runtime release status; issues/PRs adjusted only when these claims are genuinely supported.

**Point d'arrêt:** no perfect individual, no forced survival policy and no indefinite refinement loop. Once an appropriately targeted, independently reviewed test answers the bounded original scientific question positively or negatively, stop Stage 2 and document the boundary. If no discriminating hypothesis or feasible test exists, **stop expenditure without falsely closing scientific Stage 2**.

## H. Architecture and explicit final answer

**Choose incremental conservation, not a rewrite.** Keep native P1.3 Mind, BodyLaws, World, Action Control, genetics and personal outcome learning intact. If and only if independent methods review finds a genuinely distinct extra first-person information source, introduce **one** isolated, fully paid, generic information handoff at the native consequence-estimation boundary, without an external motor policy. Do not use the already inspected E3 fixtures or known error tails to tune a new behavior. Variable Competition is a real native physiological mediator: preserve and measure it instead of manufacturing fixed costs.
