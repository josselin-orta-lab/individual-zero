then recompute a complete final decision from the cognition-depleted body:

- relevant -> one complete Candidate-F final geometry;

That integration intentionally overpays because Stage-A scores are not reused.
The next bounded question is therefore lived and physical:

**does relevance gating contract unnecessary Candidate-F activation without
recreating Candidate-G's funding collapse or becoming too expensive itself?**
