## Primary prospectively declared outcome: alive at step 400

Four modes with exactly the same initial genome, world, body, life seed,
physiological rules and nominal v2 cognition price schedule:
`full`, `blind_action_predictions`, `action_agnostic_prior`,
and `blind_sleep_forecast`. Every condition executes and pays its
actual learning and retains its personal learned effects. The
intervention changes only how specific predictions are **read
during decision**, not whether action effects are learned at all.

| Genetic population (72 lives/mode) | Full | Blind action predictions | Personal action-agnostic prior | Blind sleep forecast |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 19 | **0** | **0** | 21 |
| 1 | 31 | **0** | **0** | 23 |
| 2 | 23 | **0** | **0** | 21 |
| 3 | 25 | **0** | **0** | 25 |
| **Overall (descriptive)** | **98/288** | **0/288** | **0/288** | **90/288** |

There are 190, 288, 288 and 198 observed physical deaths,
respectively. The 1152 lives are *dependent* because they share
genomes and environments. The statistical independent unit is
**n=4** original genetic populations.

The two action-specific prediction interventions yield the **same
population-level survival contrasts**, `full - intervention`:
**[+19, +31, +23, +25]** survivors per 72, mean **+24.5**.
Exploratory two-sided Student-t interval (df=3):
**[+16.54,+32.46] lives per population** (approximately
[+22.98,+45.08] percentage points), with uncertain n=4 assumptions.
Exact two-sided sign-flip reference **p=0.125** and
exploratory Holm adjustment across the three contrasts **p=0.375**.
With only four genetic populations, even identical signs cannot give
an exact two-sided p below 0.125, so this is **not conventionally
confirmatory**. Neither the aggregate 98-life gap nor the positive
t-interval overrides that limitation.

For `blind_sleep_forecast`, population-level contrasts
**[-2,+8,+2,0]**, mean +2 lives/72, exploratory interval
**[-4.87,+8.87]**, exact p=0.75 and adjusted p=0.75.
No coherent survival benefit of retaining only the learned
sleep-restoration readout is established in these sampled worlds.
This does not refute sleep, replay, consolidation, or its contribution
in different environments.

## Mechanism: genuinely funded scores BEFORE first action divergence

The 12-step prospective observation window retains the **same named
candidate's genuinely paid score**, its base projected return and
curiosity/risk bonus, and the five-channel body at decision onset.
A candidate without paid simulation has no fabricated score.
The paired comparison counts only common options under the same
pre-decision body, up to the first different physical action/parameter.
This avoids comparing a later already-changed body but does **not**
match hidden memories, knowledge, RNG or the full perceived context.

| Paired readout vs full (288 pairs) | Common-option score difference before first changed action | Different base projected return | At least one action change within first 12 steps |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Blind action predictions | **288/288** | **288/288** | 166/288 |
| Action-agnostic prior | **285/288** | **79/288** | 183/288 |
| Blind sleep forecast | 44/288 | 44/288 | 22/288 |

Earliest shared-option score difference: **step 2** for both
action-oriented interventions, **step 3** for sleep forecast
blinding. Earliest different physical action: **step 2** for
action-oriented interventions and **step 4** for sleep blinding.
These are minima across potentially **different** pairs, not one
demonstrated unbroken causal sequence.

A particularly important falsifier: under the action-agnostic prior,
the scored candidate differs in 285 pairs but its **base projected
return** differs in only 79. Thus much of its early scoring difference
cannot be assigned to the projected-value term alone. Uncertainty,
curiosity/risk bonus, and the separately retained first-person
contexts can affect score. Future claims must analyze these
mechanisms separately, not describe this as a pure change in reward
prediction. For zero-delta action blindness, both base projected
returns and full scores change across all 288 pairs.

| Admitted actions | 62,172 | 12,772 | 11,538 | 64,888 |
| Modeled paid cognitive energy | 43,361.4456 | 5,402.3026 | 4,808.8220 | 45,657.8072 |
| Modeled paid cognitive vigilance | 52,607.784 | 7,713.226 | 6,881.548 | 55,636.168 |
| Censored alive at horizon | **98** | **0** | **0** | **90** |

Identical initial budgets and tariffs **do not imply identical
realized cognitive charges**. Loss of action-specific prediction
readouts changes physical decisions; bodies later become more
inactive, and substantially less cognition is requested, executed
and paid over their shorter lives. The large survival difference is
a consequence of the entire readout intervention and its downstream
decision/body/economy feedback, **not an isolated information-only
effect with equal realized computation**. There are no free
predictions, refunds, postmortem replays or synthetic work invoices.

The action-agnostic prior does preserve a personally observed global
body-change mean. Its total mortality shows that **this aggregate
personal information did not substitute for action-specific learned
consequence readouts** in these ecologies. It does not tell us whether
a different uninformed but structurally functional policy could
survive; a different comparator would need its own preregistration.

## Boundaries and next falsifiable experiment

P4a's inconclusive genetic-selection generalization remains unchanged.
P4b/P5a findings that storing/using learned effects matters in
their own held-out world families are supported by a different,
independent prospectively allocated P5b world slice; these
observations are internally consistent but **reuse the same four
genetic source populations and ecological generator family**, not
large independent genetic/ecological replication.

The supported narrow interpretation is that I3's **action-specific
learned consequence readout** is strongly associated with
sustained persistence under these embodied v2 laws, while simply
retaining the model or exposing a generic global body-change scale
does not preserve normal behavior. This does not prove optimal
intelligence, stable acquired individuality, information-only
causality, or consciousness.

Next research steps, subject to separate preregistration:
(1) disentangle base predicted return, uncertainty-weighted curiosity,
risk veto and option-admissibility, without learning from hidden
oracle world states; (2) explicitly test energetic mediation with
causally valid new controls, never by fictitious matched bills;
(3) obtain more independently generated genetic populations and
new ecological mechanisms; (4) separately test **same genome,
different lived histories → identical novel challenge in a surviving
continuous individual**, rather than merely different memory hashes.

**No scientific GO.** I1 remains active, I3 remains an
unregistered research prototype and the P4 reserve stays sealed.
