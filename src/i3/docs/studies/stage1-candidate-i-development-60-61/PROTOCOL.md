comes from the Candidate-I architecture or from omega.

omega=0 ablation.

native-omega effect must be retained as evidence rather than tuned away.

Candidate-I continues beyond this DEVELOPMENT pilot only if **all J1-J7**

### J1: both first-contact final routes are exercised

Across native Candidate-I lives, at least one completed first-contact decision

- `P1.3_BASE_FINAL`.

### J2: native omega is behaviorally expressed under a common state

At least one matched native-vs-omega0 block must have a **common-state
first-contact divergence**.

For each block, scan native Candidate-I and its same-genome omega=0 ablation
from step 1.

Let `t*` be the earliest step at which any of these differs:

- final option;
- final action;
- final action parameter;
- Candidate-I final route;
- final decision funding status.

A block counts for J2 only if:

1. every earlier step has identical action, parameter, terminal state and
   post-step physical body;
2. at `t*`, pre-decision physical body, personal position and resource
   capture fields are identical;
3. the decision at `t*` is an unsupported first-contact Candidate-I decision;
4. native receipts say genome omega was used;
5. omega0 receipts say the explicit zero override was used;
6. at least one declared final-decision field above differs.

Score-only differences that never alter route, funding or motor output do not
satisfy J2.

If no block satisfies J2, native omega has no demonstrated lived behavioral
expression on this panel and J2 fails. Do not widen omega or change fixtures.
