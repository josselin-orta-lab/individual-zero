## Primary prespecified comparison: censored survival at step 400

There are **288 lives per learning mode** (72 per independent population).
Normal and all three interventions use exactly the same initial
genotypes, world snapshots, life seeds, horizon, v2 bodily physics and
nominal cognitive work prices. Actual cognitive spending and activity
are consequences of each treatment.

| Population | Normal /72 | Discard effects /72 | Discard TD values /72 | Discard replay /72 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 17 | **0** | 15 | 16 |
| 1 | 36 | **0** | 39 | 41 |
| 2 | 21 | **0** | 19 | 18 |
| 3 | 38 | **0** | 44 | 44 |
| **Overall (descriptive)** | **112 /288** | **0 /288** | **117 /288** | **119 /288** |

**Discarding persistent learned action-effect statistics** is
associated with mortality in all 288 tested lives and all four
independent genetic populations. Normal-minus-discard-effects paired
population differences are **+17, +36, +21, +38** survivors per 72.
Mean **+28** survivors/population (38.89 percentage points), exploratory
95% t interval **[+11.21,+44.79]** survivors/population. Exact
two-sided sign-flip reference p **0.125**, exploratory Holm-adjusted
across three comparisons **0.375**.

**Discarding durable TD values**: population differences normal minus
ablation **+2, −3, +2, −6**, mean **−1.25** survivors/72, exploratory
95% t interval **[−7.53,+5.03]**. Exact sign-flip p **0.625**;
Holm-adjusted **1**. No coherent evidence of benefit to retaining
these particular learned value coefficients in this screen.

**Discarding incremental replay retention**: population differences
**+1, −5, +3, −6**, mean **−1.75** survivors/72, exploratory
95% t interval **[−8.79,+5.29]**. Exact sign-flip p **0.5**;
Holm-adjusted **1**. There is no coherent positive contribution
established for retaining replay's incremental TD changes.

**Critical limitation:** four independent genetic populations yield
a minimum attainable exact two-sided sign-flip p of 0.125, regardless
of how unanimous the signs are. The intervals rely on fragile n=4
t assumptions. This is an *exploratory mechanistic contrast*, not a
confirmatory p<0.05 result. The four populations reuse the earlier
P3 origins, even though P5a uses fresh worlds. Do not call all 1152
individual lives independent population replications.

## Learning and sleep manipulations genuinely execute

The `discard_effects` and `discard_values` interventions compute and
pay the ordinary full learning pipeline, then restore **only** their
declared learned component. `discard_replay` executes replay on a
detached copy and discards its incremental TD retention, while
keeping the normal base update, sensory observations, spatial maps and
orientation. In all cases actual replay work is charged; there are
no fictitious postmortem invoices.

| Per-mode total (288 lives) | Normal | Discard effects | Discard values | Discard replay |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Executed and paid replays | 391,323 | 31,401 | 421,792 | 422,400 |
| Lives with at least one replay | 274 | 267 | 274 | 274 |

The replay ablation is **not** a no-exposure test. However its result
pertains specifically to *incremental replay TD retention*, not sleep
as a whole. Normal base learning stays active; the experiment does not
evaluate generalized memory consolidation or sleep deprivation.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Physical lived steps | 80,209 | 54,252 | 79,828 | 79,790 |
| Paid modeled energy for cognition | 45,747.782 | 6,568.554 | 48,480.219 | 48,762.295 |
| Paid modeled vigilance for cognition | 55,652.890 | 9,510.972 | 58,375.160 | 58,777.170 |
| Inactive physical steps | 15,880 | 39,293 | 11,842 | 11,313 |
| Inactivity fraction of actual lived steps | 19.80% | **72.43%** | 14.83% | 14.18% |
| Censored alive at horizon | 112 | 0 | 117 | 119 |

The no-effects model becomes markedly more inactive and pays far
less cognitive energy **because it acts/learns and lives differently**.
That is evidence of a large behavioral and energetic cascade when
lasting action-effect statistics are unavailable, **not** proof that
the stored information alone has a survival advantage under identical
realized cognitive bills. The `normal` and `discard_effects`
conditions are equivalent in initial opportunity and per-unit prices,
not in their observed work vectors or time at risk.

Even if `discard_values` and `discard_replay` show slightly more
censored lives overall, that does NOT prove these mechanisms are harmful:
the sample is small and the population signs mixed.

## Scientific interpretation and next decision

The strongest narrow conclusion is that, in this I3 architecture and
its three P2 ecologies, **retaining a model of action consequences is
much more strongly associated with sustained active persistence**
than retention of the specific TD-value or incremental sleep-replay
components tested here. It remains possible that effect statistics
also serve as a structural control signal for action generation, not
simply as an extra predictor. The experiment does not distinguish
these pathways or establish broad ecological generalization.

Next interventions require a distinct preregistration:
(1) analyze behavior and physical inactivity as mediators without
conditioning away the primary treatment; (2) test a minimally
informative effects baseline or targeted effect-model restoration
without injecting privileged world facts, so a broken action-selection
pipeline is distinguishable from a weaker but functional learner;
(3) assess replay/value retention under longer, more independent
life histories and new ecology families; (4) independently test
same-genome lived histories under a *common post-history challenge*
rather than inferring individuality from memory hashes.
