### Exploratory secondary TD residual, not proper predictive utility

In **19/24** cases, both detached Minds chose exactly the same next real action and the observed transition was nonterminal. There, and only there, a bootstrapped TD residual was evaluated on both copies against the **single realized transition**. The sum of P residuals was **0.2543946581**, versus **0.2434861212** for R. These sums are not a normalized per-experience benefit, calibrated predicted outcome loss, independent cross-validation or proper predictive score. The TD target itself depends on the model's own bootstrap, and learned values/experience selection differ; therefore neither numerical sign is evidence that R predicts better. No outcome was invented for an action that P might hypothetically have chosen but was not executed.

## Interpretation, falsification, next direction

1. **Local values vs decisions:** earlier first-realstate study found 17/24 assigned nonzero learned-value differences at same pre-replay state and count. This new distinct panel finds 20/24 first different replay subsets but only **1/24** changed next admitted action. Learned-value divergence does not imply near-term behavior change. Neither result should be pooled as an iid-confirmatory effect estimate.
2. **No future oracle:** neither detached alternative executes World, so future survival and true counterfactual physical consequences of the alternative action are unobserved. Same-transition bootstrapped TD residual cannot substitute for an independent proper forecasting loss.
3. **Source limits:** same known stable/depletion/pulse F0 ecological generator, HMAC deterministic unbalanced source allocation but **no independent attestation of iid ecology or external scientific sign-off**, no registered loss margin, no held-out validation/selection/reserve or ecological transfer proof.
