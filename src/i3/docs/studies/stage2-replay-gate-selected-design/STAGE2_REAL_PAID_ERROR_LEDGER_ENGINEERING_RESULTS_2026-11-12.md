## What this changes, and what it does not

**Achieved:** one complete **native, bodily funded** technical chain for personally observed forecast errors: original native decision, additional truly computed and World-debited forecast, actual World motor, native paid learning, additional World-debited error calculation and storage, followed by a next native decision from its changed actual body. Both costs demonstrably influence actual World accounting. The two detached retain/discard arms remain behaviorally equal because no policy reads the externally retained history. This closes the narrower engineering **physical-debit execution gap** in #1351.

**Not achieved:** the external error ledger is not native durable first-person Mind storage, its persistence across actual reboot/M11 is not proved, the individual does not decide to buy the extra forecast/storage, and the old fully paid native predictor may already be computing overlapping information. A contrast in outcomes or action efficacy is impossible in these arms by construction: information is withheld from both controllers. No autonomous metareasoning, native action mediation, survival improvement, generalization or Stage 2.2/2.3 GO can be inferred from a pass. Although the two arms have identical modeled-work tickets and predictions, the extra erase overhead prevents claiming exact complete host-CPU parity, and model-price coefficients remain uncalibrated against hardware joules.
