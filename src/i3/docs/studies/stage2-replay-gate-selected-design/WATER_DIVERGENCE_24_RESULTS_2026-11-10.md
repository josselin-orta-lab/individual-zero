## Prespecified first-replay mechanistic finding

| Complete finite-panel observation | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Original matched assigned units | **24/24** |
| Pairs with a first difference in replay-memory selection within common observed steps | **18/24** |
| Pairs where that first difference occurred at identical comprehensive pre-replay learned state and identical positive funded count | **18/24** |
| Pairs without observed different replay selections in their common life window | **6/24** |
| Pairs where the first *personal water-belief* difference occurred strictly **after** first replay-content difference | **16/24** |
| Pairs where the first *decision/action* difference occurred strictly **after** first replay-content difference | **16/24** |
| Mechanistically unobserved / hidden World interventions | **0**, no added organism cognition |

The prespecified **primary** quantity is therefore **18/24**. The six null or right-censored common windows are included; no convenient replacement or retroactive reclassification of their outcome. In the 18 content-divergent pairs, first changed selected memory identity occurred at observed steps 24–178, with 9th/10th sorted values 67/70. After this first content difference, **16** pairs later diverged in their personally held water-belief history and their decisions. The remaining two must not be portrayed as water-belief or behavioral success.

Family-wise observations: stable 7/10 first changed memory identity with equal replay state, of which 6 later changed water beliefs and decisions; depletion **6/8**, with **5** later changing water beliefs and decisions; pulse **5/6**, with **5** later changing water beliefs and decisions. These are small conditional counts, not estimates of transferable causal mediation.

Observed first-positive-water-uptake status across all 24 pairs: **15 pairs recorded at least one positive drinking transaction in both policies**, and **9 recorded none in either**. No pair had such an event in only one arm under this source. This does **not** imply equal water acquisition timing or guarantee subsequent survival.

## What this does and does not establish

This observation validates that changing memory-content selection can occur at equal **replay-relevant** learned state and prepaid count and can precede differences in personally learned water beliefs and decisions. The temporal ordering is consistent with a possible memory-learning route to behavioral change, **not** a randomized controlled mediation estimate, because all later bodily state, exposure, cognition expenditure and environment encounter can diverge. No water acquisition or death mechanism has been established from the eight previously selected survivor-only discordances; the current study retained every assigned unit before analyzing any outcome.

The known stable/depletion/pulse F0 ecology generator and internally generated HMAC seeds do not constitute independently attested iid ecological sampling. The new study is a bounded *exploratory DEVELOPMENT* only: no scientific superiority, noninferiority, independent model validation, lineage evolution, reserve or Stage 2.2 active registration. Nothing in these observations justifies forcing water-seeking behavior, changing the innate needs, or tuning TD priorities to match desired outcomes.

**Decision:** preserve all positive, null and adverse observations unaltered; **CLOSED_NO_RETRY** source 510000–510023, disable its execution workflow, and independently check every original gzip/reduction receipt after archival. Further mechanism identification should involve a new separately predeclared perturbation of replay content under exact physical costs, not retrospective reanimation of any individual.
