## Mechanistic comparison and actual result

The earlier [#1345 cost-settlement study](STAGE2_ACTUAL_LEARNING_SETTLEMENT_RESULTS_2026-11-12.md) **fixed** the laboratory action `attendre`. Its two energy/vigilance boundary cases could each pay the optional extra readout yet could no longer afford native outcome learning; that was a true native World/body accounting result on the specified fixed action. This new study instead permits the original `Mind.decide` to choose its own motor once, then requests additional work externally, computes and **discards** the result, and executes the *already chosen* motor with real World debit, followed by native paid learning.

| energy_boundary (E=0.56,V=80) | `dormir` | Actually funded, computed, discarded | PAID_LEARNING | PAID_LEARNING | **No** lost learning; differs from fixed `attendre` result |

| optional_denied (E=0.49,V=80) | `dormir` | Actually funded, computed, discarded | PAID_LEARNING | PAID_LEARNING | Historical **case label** 'optional_denied' only applied to a prior *fixed action/work* fixture; **not** a denial in the native decision study |

**Complete finite result**: 4/4 cases with native `dormir`; 4/4 optional requests were physically model-fundable; 4/4 extra readouts actually computed then discarded; 0/4 optional denied; 0/4 no native motor; **0/4 lost native outcome-learning opportunities**; 0/4 pairwise selected-motor differences. No case selected/changed in response to the readout: the additional computation occurs **after** the native choice, and its result never enters the Mind or the controller. These outcomes are **not** rates in an ecological sampling distribution. The apparent contradiction with the forced-wait study is an explicit difference of experimental interventions, not evidence that either test was incorrectly executed.

Native `Mind.decide` chose and funded its original computation without experimental override. Each arm's pre-optional work, option and motor matched its paired arm exactly; the laboratory's additional request was only allowed through existing `CognitiveBudget` and motor-reserve checks. The original `World.step` debited actual cognitive work in both native body currencies; on nonterminal outcomes, the original `Mind.prepare_learning` allocated learning and `World.pay_cognition` preceded `Mind.learn_from_outcome`. Immediate post-World and post-paid-learning five-channel bodies were retained in full job receipts. Tests also verify no unfunded head, no new policy, no counterfactual chosen motor, no postmortem learning and no source/RNG modification by the discarded readout.

## Limits and decision

1. **Positive technical result:** the original native action, not a designer-forced action, materially changes the *experimental context* for an optional cost. It exposes how fixed-action opportunity-cost examples can be overextended. The native controller selected `dormir`, which can replenish energy and vigilance; native learning remained affordable in all four fixed cases. This is a narrow finite mechanism observation, not an identified causal comparison of alternative self-chosen actions.
2. **No new Stage 2.1 adaptive thinking evidence:** the individual did **not** select the optional computation, it received none of its information, and the study contains no paid VISIBLE/PAID_DISCARD contrast that could influence future action. A successful native choice and full bodily accounting do not imply the mind has discovered when to think.
3. **No physical CPU tariff calibration:** costs are native modeled BodyLaws energy/vigilance per declared CognitiveWork, not measured joules. The outcome is not proof that all host operations and opportunity costs have been fully priced.
4. **No scientific World/cohort:** eight deliberate 1x1 engineering steps are deterministic unit-like tests, not population samples or survival experiments; no newly admissible scientific World source or independent ecology.
5. **Stop:** preserve all four results, including no-learning-loss observations. Do not retune the same cases to force a non-sleep decision and do not reopen E1/E3. Stage 2 stays 2.1 `NOT_ISSUED`; C01 remains `HOLD_NO_VERDICT_FOR_GENERAL_STAGE2`. The next *scientifically relevant* technical design would need to capture independently justified **first-person action-relevant information** and compare truly equal executed and paid VISIBLE vs PAID_DISCARD **before** a later native choice, with rigorous costing, no action override and prospective source/authorization review. This experiment does not supply that missing source.
