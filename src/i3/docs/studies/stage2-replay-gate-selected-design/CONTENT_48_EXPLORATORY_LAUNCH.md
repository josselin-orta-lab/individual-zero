## Question and hypotheses

Does replaying personally highest-TD-error memories produce higher **horizon-400 full-life survival** than replaying independently random memory identities when **both use the identical stakes-based funded GATE replay-count rule**? Primary assigned-pair finite estimand: `(priority-only survivors - random-content-only survivors)/48`. Death at exactly 400 counts as death, not survivor. The hypothesis permits positive, zero or negative effects with no favorable early stopping.

Secondary outcomes: all four pair discordance categories, all assigned-life lived steps, actual energy and vigilance physically paid per assigned life, actual paid replay counts, the number of random-content opportunities whose selected subset differed from TD priority and adverse results in **every** stable/depletion/pulse family. Report both total and family-specific results, including missing family samples if the unbalanced original draw produces any. Do not drop any adverse unit or alter its source.

**Causal scope:** the random-content intervention changes the selected memory identities at a given identical state and admitted replay-count decision. Later closed-loop state/buffer/sleep/count trajectories may diverge because learning changes, therefore this is a **full-policy** matched counterfactual contrast, NOT a causal controlled-direct effect at realized fixed replay count. The physical replay tariff and innate survival system remain unchanged; no artificial survival rule or genetic preference is imposed.
