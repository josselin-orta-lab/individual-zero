## Scientific problem and two separate estimands

The original [paid-residual readout](STAGE2_PAID_RESIDUAL_UNCERTAINTY_NATIVE_DECISION_RESULTS_2026-11-12.md) and [first native cost split](STAGE2_FIRST_NATIVE_COST_SPLIT_DECOMPOSITION_RESULTS_2026-11-12.md) showed 0/4 clean native motor divergences and 3/4 bodily differences caused by **different native Competition cycles with the same motor**, plus one no-split unit. Identical exogenous paid source scans do NOT guarantee equal subsequent endogenous cognitive spending.

- **Natural intention-to-treat total policy effect**: compare initially matched assigned genome/World/seed units with an equally executed-and-funded optional information source, VISIBLE vs PAID_DISCARD; leave native Competition, subsequent motor, paid learning, physiology and information purchase free to diverge. Downstream cost is a real mediator, not a parity error. This is the appropriate conditional future bodily experiment; the four old technical units are not such a population.
- **Controlled direct information-to-motor effect**: separate hypothetical intervention requiring equal *all-in* paid native work at the motor boundary. No reimbursements, free extra cycles, forced motor or cloned World future can be used to fabricate this equality.
- **Autonomous optional compute purchase** is NOT demonstrated: the past private-error scan was laboratory-requested. Native variable Competition duration alone is not informed choice to buy another computation.
