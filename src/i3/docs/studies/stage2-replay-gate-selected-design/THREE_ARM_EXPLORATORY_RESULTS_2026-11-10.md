## 3. The control's nominal/eligible/physically paid distinction

The paid RANDOM arm averaged **502.28125 nominally scheduled** replay slots and **501.875 actually eligible and physically paid** replays per assigned life. Across all 64 lives, the aggregate count is **32,146 nominal vs 32,120 eligible and 32,120 paid**, with 26 nominally scheduled replays unfulfilled. The frozen count target `5532/867` is from the previously completed DEVELOPMENT 80/81 pilot, NOT a guaranteed actual match to the new GATE life trajectories.

RANDOM paid **501.875** replays/assigned life while GATE paid **515.140625**; this is **not actual replay-count equality**, even though both modes respected physical funding and used the same base Mind/World per matched unit. This means that the GATE−RANDOM contrast is a full-policy effect, not an isolated causal comparison of which replays were selected with all other costs matched. The three-way transport is functional; the causal mechanism remains unidentified.

This sampling assumption is **not independently attested**, and there is **no approved biological loss margin**.
