## Scientific significance and next development gate

This confirms a *model-level accounting pitfall*: the final
post-rest E/V values do not fully reveal whether cognition consumed
resources that were transferred into food, **which is vital** in
i3's present physiology. Together with previous #1298/1299, optional
cognition can displace future learning ability **or** consume
food by recovery coupling, even though an action remains executable.
Neither fact alone demonstrates i3 can predict those opportunity
costs, nor does it establish a learned value-of-computation
mechanism or general survival improvement.

**Important:** A synthetic `zero_food` terminal is NOT a sampled
mortality event or survival statistic. A deterministic
threshold demonstration is not an independent ecological study,
there is no calibrated CPU-to-Body pricing, and the prospective
readout is still a nonproducing external laboratory proposal.
Actual first-person future targets must remain phase aligned:
`POST_PAID_LEARNING` primary, `IMMEDIATE_POST_WORLD` secondary;
no historic outcome is reinterpreted.

For any future metacognitive proposal, require
(i) a truly pre-budgeted optional computation, (ii) total
two-currency and subsequent sleep/repair food consequences,
(iii) true post-learning first-person target and a proper
forecast-loss comparison, (iv) no imposed motor choice or
priority between thinking/learning, (v) independent prospective
ecological source/authorization before born lives. Start
with **software-only learned-cost opportunity boundaries** rather
than implementing a hard-coded `always preserve food` veto.
