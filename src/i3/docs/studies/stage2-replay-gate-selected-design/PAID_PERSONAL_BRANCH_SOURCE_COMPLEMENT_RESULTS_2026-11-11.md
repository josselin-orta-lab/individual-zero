## Architectural decision and scientific stop

**New actionable architectural finding:** a bounded, personally action-keyed empirical information substrate already exists with explicit **modeled** energy/vigilance storage and read prices. There is no engineering justification to create another action-labelled memory store or another TD replay-distress predictor simply to obtain a Stage-2 input. Under a future genuinely born and paid original, this storage could supply information about the *distribution* of personally experienced outcomes that is absent from the currently used mean-only `EffectsModel`. But the present histories are synthetic and this availability has **not** been shown to cause a real action change or improve net survival.

**Why this does not reopen Stage 1:** ADR-0018 closed Stage 1 negatively and forbids a new candidate to replace the inherited curiosity terms without its own accepted scope. This E2 check does not reuse Candidate F/G/H/I/J as a policy, invent an information bonus, use an external oracle or alter the existing P1.3 reference. A personal empirical spread is not automatically an intrinsic value-of-information reward. Existing paid `PersonalOutcomes.construct` and `paid_personal_information` are experimental arithmetic kernels, not certified native choice policies or accepted generalizations.

**Smallest next E3 gate, conditional:** select **one** exact previously implemented, action-indexed empirical support query and a general first-person consequence forecast transformation, preregister its required **two-currency work ceiling**, then perform a bounded no-World **equal-real-compute VISIBLE vs PAID_DISCARD** test of its arrival in *native* `Mind.decide` scoring and admissible motor. Do not preassign a preferred motor or tune world fixtures for success. If the unchanged full native decision and control do not use it, report an **E3 mechanism NO_GO** and stop this source candidate. If the structural gate passes, actual bodily use and net viability still require independent source-index-scoped prospective World authorization, true post-paid learning and proper control/power, not earlier old-born archives.
