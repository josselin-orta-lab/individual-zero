## Falsifier and fixed sample, prior to first World

The previous physically billed compact result froze *one* forecast at the
individual's seventh step in each of three matched engineering sources.
A one-step fixed technical target cannot establish prediction sustained
across an evolving life. Ask whether the same compact, directly
self-experienced predictor can repeatedly train and forecast on **new,
factual next first-person outcomes**, with native equal-cost funding and
no effect on the already chosen actions.

**Freeze exactly** new engineering seeds `950041,950042,950043`,
`genome.midpoint()`, `dev_open_field()`, horizon 80, reference P1.3.
For each seed execute three **cost-matched** compact observers:
TD-priority memory, genuine life/step-seeded HMAC-random memory, and
three real training updates whose effects are deliberately **discarded**.
Separately run one **unpriced reference** life solely for the *first*
precharge action match. These are **12 technical engineering lives**
in one known deterministic ecology, not 12 independent G0 scientific
organisms or proof of generalization.

## Frozen longitudinal intervention

At the **first twelve eligible decisions** in each paid arm
(`len(actual Mind.buffer)>=6`; first 12 by chronological eligibility,
not first 12 successful outcomes), after living `Mind.decide` returns
and before the actual next `World.step`:

1. Retain the same actual living chosen `action, param` and native
   `CognitiveBudget`. The external observer may read only the actual
   first-person current body, inherited distress parameters and
   previously lived transition memories. Never inspect the next World
   outcome or hidden environment.
2. For `n=len(actual buffer)` and fixed `k=3`, **preauthorize**
   `work_envelope(n,k)` from the same native budget, protecting remaining
   energy for the chosen motor command. On failure log
   `NO_FUNDED_FORECAST` and perform **zero** compact head computation.
   A refused slot still consumes one of the 12 chronological
   opportunities; never retry it after viewing the outcome.
3. On successful authorization only, construct the compact immutable
   personal snapshot **fresh from the current personally learned
   history** and run both priority and random index selectors.
   Execute exactly three true direct-distress training updates on the
   selected past memories in **every paid mode**; the discarded control
   forecasts from its *pretraining* state while still computing and
   paying for those updates. No persistent compact weights carry
   between slots: this examines repeated **refits on evolving personal
   histories**, not a novel persistent online learner.
4. SHA-seal prediction, personal memory-source identity, actual
   precharge body/choice, training identity and full prepaid work before
   `World.step`. Return enlarged real `budget.work` to unchanged
   `run_life`, where actual `World.step` debits **both energy and
   vigilance** before physical consequences. Verify that full native
   work and paid/no-unpaid receipts match the frozen ticket.
5. Score only that *next actual first-person* returned body/death flag.
   Proper score is squared loss against existing `-Mind.distress`
   of actual after-body or terminal `-d_max`. No body hallucination,
   future outcomes in training, causal counterfactual action,
   policy readout or new innate reward. Log all negative, favorable,
   tied, refusal, terminal and horizon-censored slots.

If World terminates before twelve eligible opportunities, retain the
missing slots as explicit `HORIZON_OR_TERMINAL_CENSOR`; do not add
new lives or resample favorable sources. If a slot was eligible but
unfunded, count it as a **refusal**, not a scored success or zero loss.
Report source-level and step-level counts and unique observed targets.

## Explicit software gates and descriptive estimands

On all three fixed engineering sources:
- Every genuinely paid event has a preoutcome SHA and an actual next
  World return, with native dual-currency payment; every refused
  attempt has no head creation/learning or added bill.
- The three equally billed modes preserve **exact full** `trace_head`,
  `evidence_head`, `steps_lived`, `terminal`, `death_causes`,
  `actions`, `final_body` and `cognitive_accounting`.
  Only the first attempt's precharge motor choice may be compared to
  the cost-free reference; later divergent actions due to distinct
  bodily budgets are not information-only evidence.
- Print every assigned step and its status, actual target, per-arm loss,
  paid energy/vigilance. Report each arm's aggregate loss *over matched
  observed windows only*, also report **all assigned/null** counts,
  per-seed favorable/adverse/ties and the number of distinct actual
  outcome targets. Avoid cherry-picking only low-loss windows.
- Failure cases: attempt without eligible history; too little
  energy/vigilance or insufficient motor reserve; tampered prediction,
  mismatched action or native bill, unsupported mode; observed terminal
  penalty; pending forecast at report time; observer hook restoration
  on exception. No changes to `modules/`, `runtime/life.py`,
  `World`, P1.3, laws, genome or policy.
