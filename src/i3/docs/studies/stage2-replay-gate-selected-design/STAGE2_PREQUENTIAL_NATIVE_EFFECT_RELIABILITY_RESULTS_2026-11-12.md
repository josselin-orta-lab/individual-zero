## Actual causal ordering checked

At each real first-person decision, the witness records the original chosen action, its native learning-key, current body and the **already learned** `EffectsModel.predict(key)` five-channel mean with native count-proxy uncertainty. The record is SHA-256 sealed **before** `World.step` sees the outcome. The original World executes the chosen motor and debits real **model** cognitive energy/vigilance. When reference outcome learning is actually funded, `World.pay_cognition` debits the body **before** `Mind.learn_from_outcome` can modify its effects model; the witness scores its frozen mean against factual `POST_PAID_LEARNING` body delta at this boundary. Null/unfunded/terminal branches are included without invented forecasts or outcomes. The observer never returns a value to `Mind.decide`, controls, budget or learning.

The entire original `run_life` output is compared against an **unobserved baseline** from the same fixed World/seed, including full trace SHA, evidence SHA, steps, final body, terminal, actions and cumulative cognitive receipts. All four baseline comparisons passed. The observer's reads and SHA operations are **external, unpaid laboratory work**; this study does not show that the individual can afford or select this additional assessment.

The four technical lives all ended **right-censored at horizon 80** (not dead). Each had 80 actual paid learning updates and 0 native null decisions in the frozen grid, so **320/320** actual preoutcome forecasts were scored and **320/320** factual paid post-learning bodies accounted for. The unobserved baseline life outputs remained identical to observed lives; hence the measurement has not changed policy or action. None of these 320 steps are independent experimental units for ecological inference.

**Narrow interpretation:** the first uses of previously unobserved exact-key actions show much larger average squared error than later uses in these finite trajectories. It is consistent with experience improving the native mean forecast; it is **not** an independently identified causal effect of learning because action, physiological state, early/late time, ecology, and missingness differ between unseen and seen conditions. Nor is the 1/sqrt(1+n_exact) count proxy calibrated by this study to a probability or confidence interval. Within the same count category, errors differ across trajectories and actions; a simple count is not an empirically validated estimate of the value of additional computation.

## Distinct from E3 and remaining hard stop

The prior E3 studied empirical nonmean history of an action and found **one score-only change, zero native motor changes** in source-frozen synthetic contexts. It remains `CLOSED_NEGATIVE`. This prequential diagnostic does **not** retest the E3 artificial body/seed, does not add a new candidate controller, and does not claim a different information-source mechanism that can change an action. It supplies an instrument to establish **proper forecast fidelity on actually observed subsequent bodies** without TD bootstrap as target. An instrument is not itself a new useful computation chosen by the individual.

**Next logical gate:** before any Stage-2 positive inference, specify **one** information update that is not already in the action-selection input; demonstrate its marginal predictive reliability prospectively and its ability to change a *later native motor* under a genuinely equal executed-and-paid `VISIBLE/PAID_DISCARD` comparison. To claim organism benefit, real post-paid-learning bodily differences, full host/opportunity cost and independent ecology/power/index authority are additionally required. Without such a distinct source, stop candidate proliferation. Stage 2 remains `2.1 NOT_ISSUED`; #1062/#1276/#1283 remain open; I3 development reference, I1 active registered engine, no Stage 2.2/2.3 GO.
