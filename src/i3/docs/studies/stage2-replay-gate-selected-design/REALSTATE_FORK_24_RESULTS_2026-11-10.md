2. **Statistics:** no independently attested iid World/ecology source, environmental sampling frame, approved loss margin, preregistered powered survival sample or external methodological sign-off. This finite exploratory source must not be pooled post hoc with the consumed 48-content or earlier 24-water cohorts as confirmatory evidence.
