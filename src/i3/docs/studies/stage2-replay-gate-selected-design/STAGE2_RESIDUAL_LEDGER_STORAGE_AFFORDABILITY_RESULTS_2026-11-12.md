## Interpretation and scientific stop

**Supported:** on these 320 already observed post-learning bodies, with this fixed hypothetical native-model tariff, adding a single residual-history acquisition/update ticket passes an isolated budget admission check. The strict software mechanism can refuse unpayable work *before performing it* in the two low-resource negative controls. The original archive and genuine past-only native errors remain unchanged, with full source/receipt reproducibility.
