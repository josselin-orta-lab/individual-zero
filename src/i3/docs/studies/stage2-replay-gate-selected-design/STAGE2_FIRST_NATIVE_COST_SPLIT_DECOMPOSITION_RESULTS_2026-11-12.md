## Conclusion, gate and next bounded choice

**Supported:** in these three original matched-first-split cases, the first body difference can be fully explained by the native model's additional decision cycles without any motor difference. The same optional error-read computation can therefore create **different total native cognition costs** once its output enters a real native scorer, even if the immediate motor is unchanged. Equal optional read tariff is *not* equal all-in mind cost.

**Not supported:** an endogenous decision-to-buy-computation; improved predictive utility or future actions; causal survival or physiological benefit beyond arithmetic settlement of these specific sources; calibration to physical host CPU joules; independent population generalization; Stage-2 promotion. The original #1353 `STOP_NATIVE_COST_CONFOUND` remains a proper scientific stop, not an artifact to retroactively reclassify as efficacy.

**Recommended next, without opening scientific sources:** design and review one prospectively fixed **natural total-effect** estimand that **includes** information-induced native Competition cost, paid storage, future learning and body effects as real mediators. It must not artificially equalize downstream costs by giving free cycles, changing Mind decisions, or cherry-picking only identical-world futures.
