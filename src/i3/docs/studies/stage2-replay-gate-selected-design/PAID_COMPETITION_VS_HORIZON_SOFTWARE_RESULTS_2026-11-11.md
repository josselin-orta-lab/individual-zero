One shared synthetic source was instantiated from the native i3-genome-v4 midpoint and native I3 Mind, life seed 211, with exactly two manually supplied **unpaid** action-effect histories (wait / sleep). Three body states were fixed beforehand. All evaluations used native simulate, native projected intrinsic distress, actual CognitiveBudget purchases and two-currency model-cost settlement; no source prior history was represented as real paid acquisition. The two eight-step plans were deliberately synthetic sequences, not actual native generated candidate sets. The cost of a longer simulation includes the native self-projection work as well as modeled steps. None of the 18 forecasts was an observed motor command or true World outcome.

In all nine fixed-score Competition records, the evidence vector stayed unchanged throughout the tested cycle counts. This supports the limited source claim that paying another **Competition cycle** does not acquire an additional observation or simulate another option. It does **not** prove extra cycles can never influence action, goal persistence, inertia or eventual survival: those remain separate tested/unobserved mechanisms.

## Scientific consequence and stopping decision

**Positive narrow software observation:** native simulation depth can change subjective forecast values on the three frozen states.

**Negative narrow decision-relevance observation:** these altered values changed **none** of the raw top-ranked options among the frozen two-plan comparisons, so this fixture gives no basis to claim that the extra simulated steps were *worth* their personal bodily cost. Do **not** infer that a paid longer horizon is beneficial, or tune the fixed fixtures to manufacture a favorable rank flip.

**Cost-aware Stage 2 priority:** preserve existing P1.3 without source modification. Before proposing a new adaptive-horizon controller or any scientific born-life experiment, require one prospectively frozen, **native option-set / actual Mind.decide** mechanism check that a *truly payable* additional simulation can alter the genuine legal choice **after** it is scored through existing Competition/Action Control. If that gate is still non-discriminating, stop this candidate; do not add a complexity patch, a free evaluator or another replay head. Any subsequent estimate of net value must include meta-evaluation work and downstream learning/sleep/food effects and must be tested against equally paid random/discard controls. Later Stage 2.2/2.3 requires its own explicit scoped scientific decision, exact-source cohort authorization and independent ecological design; this result alone is not that decision.

**Preservation:** original 80/81, B1, 64 three-arm, 24/48 original life studies and their source hashes remain untouched and permanently CLOSED_NO_RETRY. Active manifest fresh_lived_worlds_allowed=false is unchanged. This result changes **no** native Mind, World, BodyLaws, genome, sensor, motor, Competition, selection, registry or cohort permissions.
