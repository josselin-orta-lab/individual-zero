## 3. Why this is the minimum-cost next decision, not an indefinite improvement list

**Selected trajectory: stop and publish the scoped negative evidence now.** No additional E3 synthetic bodies, seeds, action spreads, hidden world probes, more candidate replay/distress heads, changed curiosity/risk bonuses, fixed motor scripts, or replay of closed 80/81/B1/64/content-48/next-24 sources. A positive or negative result is not made more scientific by running the same source-locked artificial grid until a favorable threshold crossing occurs. Read-only existing-source audits are already sufficient for the archive question.

**Only one conditional future reopening is defensible** and its trigger is objective: an independently justified **new** source of additional, genuinely personal and payable **action-relevant** computation that has a *prospectively frozen no-World natural-choice distinction* not obtainable by tuning E3's source; alternatively an independent methods review that legitimately authorizes a bounded negative scientific closeout **of the originally defined Stage-2 scope** based on the existing original/falsifier record, with no false generalized-survival claims. A third replay head is not a source. Until this threshold, do **not** implement a new 2.2/2.3 computation controller merely to manufacture a candidate.

If the first trigger occurs, the smallest remaining critical path is: (1) **one** frozen general information-plus-compute rule, no fixed motor, with a true same-operator paid-discard control; (2) **one** exact-source software native-action discrimination showing the information can actually affect a choice while preserving Competition/Action Control; (3) complete actual E/V plus lost future learning and model host overhead accounting, independent prospective ecological unit/power/margin review and separate source-index-scoped birth authority; (4) one sealed G/R non-evidentiary rehearsal; (5) **one** newly authorized, preassigned, all-life and adverse-inclusive developmental paired panel before any justified confirmatory test. Stop if steps 1 or 2 fail: **zero new scientific lives**. Keep the biological study and validation/reserve closed otherwise. No fixed sample size or arbitrary survival NI threshold is approved by this memo.

**Estimated incremental work from this checkpoint:** stop/disposition documentation plus a source-custody index (less than one agent-day, zero lives, tiny CI/doc-review cost). If a genuinely novel source is presented later, costed mechanism specification/test ~2-4 agent-days software, transport/methods work ~2-4 agent-days with **zero scientific births until independently admitted**; original [roadmap's illustrative 24-48 matched-development-unit panel](../../STAGE2_COMPLETION_AUDIT_AND_ROADMAP_2026-11-11.md) is *not* a valid positive-generalization estimate or authorization. Independent survival non-inferiority could cost orders of magnitude more; [power review](CONFIRMATORY_SURVIVAL_POWER_STUDY.md) gives **7,198 hypothetical independent pairs** for its unapproved conservative 5pp case. Do not broaden an old lift or waive costs to meet a budget.
