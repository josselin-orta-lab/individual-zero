- Forecasts must be sealed before each actual observed outcome; source Mind/history/RNG and action/parameter are never modified by the observer; all trained indices are unique pre-existing memories. No predictions when no paid count or no eligible history.
- The `discard` arm executes three real training updates and pays the **same** model costs; its forecast is the no-learning control.
- Exactly one priced training/forecast opportunity per assigned engineering branch (or explicit null). No post-result retuning, dropped adverse arm, rewriting of memory priorities, changes to `Mind.decide`, `World` or the genome. Never use the resulting error sign to claim that memory replay improves survival.
- The tariff is intentionally provisional. It prices declared categories, not true byte-copy CPU time, independent inference overhead or future deployment performance. Therefore engineering PASS cannot certify complete cognitive-cost mediation or activate adaptive replay selection. The recorded work must never be presented as the intrinsic fee for a production predictor.
- All six matched engineering executions, absence of surprise World births, non-observation, any funding rejection and all forecast losses are preserved without selecting favorable seeds.
