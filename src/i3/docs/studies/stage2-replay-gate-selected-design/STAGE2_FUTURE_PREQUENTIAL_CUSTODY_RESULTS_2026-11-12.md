## What the software now permits and forbids

The pure `FuturePrequentialCustody` accepts one fixed source SHA-256 and a finite, **all-assigned immutable identity list**. An observer must `freeze(unit, step, baseline, candidate)` before supplying `settle(unit, digest, actual_delta)`. A pending five-channel prediction is bound to its exact previous internal receipt head and cannot be revised through mutation of the supplied forecast. The target is a genuine *later-supplied* five-channel effect delta for a **conditional mean proper squared-error loss**. This process-local call order is **not external proof** that a future integrator really timestamped or saw the factual World outcome only later.

The program refuses incorrect digests, repeated or skipped step numbers, nonfinite or invalid five-channel inputs, duplicate/missing assignments, changes after close, settling without a prior forecast, and premature aggregate reporting. It stores **one** pending event per unit; a death or censor before a subsequent outcome preserves the frozen prediction as **unscored**, never fills in a zero or calls it a favorable prediction. Units that cannot provide information or whose model cannot afford it remain in the all-assigned denominator and count separately. The report averages event scores **within each unit** before computing a purely descriptive mean across evaluable units; correlated steps do not inflate the number of independent units.
