# Stage 2.1: mechanism-first, fixed-newborn-G0 paired design (candidate v3)

**Date:** 9 November 2026. **Status: DRAFT / SOFTWARE ONLY / NO SCIENTIFIC WORLD OPENING.**

This is a separate, deliberately narrower proposal following [the v2 inference review](PROTOCOL_REVISION_V2.md), [the exact-power audit](INFERENCE_POWER_AUDIT.md), and the complete sealed-[originals bridge](SEALED_SELECTION_BRIDGE.md). It **does not overwrite** the earlier selected-pipeline proposal, its frozen DEVELOPMENT 80/81 evidence, or any historical study. Reference P1.3 funded-v2 is unchanged; I3 is unregistered.

## Scientific decision: isolate replay computation before selection

The selected-pipeline candidate tried to answer simultaneously whether GATE saves cognition, preserves survival **after separate genetic selection**, and generalizes. The original eight-population sign gate targets a directional median; it cannot certify an acceptable mean survival loss. The subsequent conservative mean bound is too imprecise with eight population units and does not by itself prove that no other justified design could work.

**Candidate v3 changes the primary question:**

> For deterministically chosen *newborn* G0 genomes, on exactly the same F0 world and life seeds, how do the experienced bodily outcomes differ when the agent uses P1.3 FIXED, physically funded GATE, or physically funded RANDOM-EQUAL-MEAN replay selection?

This tests a **same-genome, same-world policy contrast**. Separate genome selection is deliberately **not** tested; success cannot establish evolutionary advantage, evolved individual fitness or generalization to new ecological mechanisms. Crucially, the control RANDOM pays the gate's own audit and forecast, so comparing GATE and RANDOM helps distinguish context-sensitive replay choice from simple reduction in count, subject to actual funding and behavior divergence.

## Exact proposed panel and budget

Use the already proposed but **unopened** F0 validation metadata slots (indices 120–167, world seeds 30120–30167), *only if future independent collision review accepts them*. For each of eight population identifiers take the **first five generation-zero founder birth seeds** defined by `F0.founder_seed(population, slot=0..4)`. Seed choice is deterministic **before outcomes**; no selection-ranking receipts, no adapted founders, no learned-state inheritance and no use of old development 80/81 genomes.

Each of the 48 world slots contains 5 predefined newborn genomes × 3 matched policies (FIXED_G0, GATE_G0, RANDOM_G0) = **720 proposed lives**, or at most **288,000 horizon steps**. The earlier selected-pipeline plan requires 3,840 selection lives plus 1,200 validation lives = 5,040 lives and 2,016,000 steps. Thus v3 is **4,320 fewer lives (85.7% less nominal capacity)**. This is a *planned compute ceiling*, not a price quote, an executed study, or a replacement for preregistration.

The 720 lives share founders and world slots, forming 240 distinct matched (population, world, founder) blocks. These are **not 720 independent inferential units**. The same three known ecological families (stable, depletion, pulse) are repeated; no new-family transfer is tested. Independent G0 seed labels do not by themselves establish statistically independent sampled populations.

## Transparent response variables (none can produce an automatic GO)

All 720 complete reduced receipts are required with exactly one cell per block and policy; no partial survivor-only arithmetic. Compare GATE-minus-FIXED and GATE-minus-RANDOM within each matched block, then report:

- **Lived steps to death or horizon 400.** This is the truncated/restricted lived duration, not an uncensored future lifespan estimate; a death at step 400 is **death**, not survival at horizon.
- **Survival at horizon 400**, reported as a separate binary contrast, with complete denominators.
- **Actual paid energy and vigilance per assigned life** and inactive steps per assigned life. This avoids calling a treatment efficient solely because earlier death reduced exposed steps. Separate per-lived-step summaries may remain diagnostics but cannot replace per-assigned-life and duration.
- **Family- and population-stratified contrasts**, including an adverse or catastrophic family and the raw denominator. The equally population-weighted mean here is strictly a *finite-panel descriptive* contrast, not a confidence bound over unseen populations.

`i3.lab.stage2_mechanism_first_design` is a **pure proposed layout/readout**, with no access to World, Mind, registry, admission tickets or original data. It checks the proposed 720 exact cells and fails closed on duplicates, missing/foreign IDs, negative or nonfinite paid costs, illegal terminal state, and unpaired world/life seeds. The module cannot itself authenticate originals or verify physical phase-work accounting, so even a full 720-cell readout must be connected to a separately verified, authorized sealed G/R transport before scientific use.

### Non-inferiority and decision rules still unresolved

No arbitrary 5% survival margin is adopted here. A scientist must choose *before exposure* whether the aim is a bounded **finite-panel description**, a confirmatory conditional effect in a sampled F0 population, or a generalizable average effect; the estimand determines the sampling unit, inference, margin, sample-size calculation and allowable claims. If a non-inferiority claim is sought, specify the accepted survival loss in percentage points **and** lived steps, the appropriate confidence-bound method and empirically plausible dependence structure. The 80/81 DEVELOPMENT pilot suggests adverse heterogeneity, but it is not a statistically independent basis to promise 720-life power. No individual genomes may be re-picked after looking at response.

As drafted, **v3 is a research option, not a replacement authorized protocol.** It can be compared to (A) the 5,040-life selected pipeline, which targets an architecture-plus-selection effect; or (B) stopping Stage 2.1 as inconclusive. Both scientific selection/validation permissions and old P2 index/transport collision rules remain locked, and the previously consumed DEVELOPMENT 80/81 authorization is not reusable.
