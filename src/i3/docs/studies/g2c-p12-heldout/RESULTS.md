# I3 G2c: P1.2 versus P1.1 on held-out Worlds (result)

**Preregistered decision: `REJECT_P1_2_KEEP_P1_1`.** Criterion B
(non-inferiority on general Worlds) fails by one life. One execution on
5 November 2026 ([protocol](../../G2C_P12_HELDOUT_PREREG.md),
commit `d45809d`). Data: [p11.json](p11.json), [p12.json](p12.json),
[g2-regression-p12.json](g2-regression-p12.json),

## Interpretation

P1.2 substantially improves strategy revision. Under selection its best
version exceeds both P1.1 and the reference searcher. Yet for randomly
sampled generation-zero genomes it performs slightly worse on general
conditions (six lives per hundred; not significant either way). The two
changes, revisable beliefs and ray-based exploration, were tested together.
This protocol cannot identify which one incurs the general loss.

## Consequences

- The reference remains **P1.1**; P1.2 code remains on
  `i3/p1.2-variant`.
- Laboratory tools (World-family generator, probes and G2c executor)
  remain available.
- Hypotheses generated by these results require **prospective** testing
  with newly reserved seeds >= 1,000,100, never a retrospective G2c rescue:
  (1) revisable beliefs alone improve revision without general loss;
  (2) architectures must be judged under selection, prospectively declared,
  rather than with arbitrary random genomes.
