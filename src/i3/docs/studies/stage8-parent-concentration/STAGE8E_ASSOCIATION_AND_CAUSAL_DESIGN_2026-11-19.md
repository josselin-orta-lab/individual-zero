# Stage8-E : exploratory locus associations and future causal design

**Current state: DESIGN + PURE SOFTWARE ONLY. No original-gzip Stage8-E run yet.** This document is not a positive genetic verdict. Stage8-C's registered S−M positive mean +8.675998 steps, 22+/10−, remains the only source-locked primary result; Stage8-D's overall diversity Pearson r≈−0.05955 is descriptive and not a gene mechanism.

## Step 1: archival locus-by-locus discovery, read-only

The new `stage8e_gene_associations_readonly.py` first invokes the existing original-level Stage8-D auditor, which in turn rechecks **32,256 pre-existing Stage8-C gzip original lives**, 4,608 inherited mutations, the pre-verdict independent Stage8-C report and both 16-population native panels. It then reads **only** the already authenticated `SELECT_G1.json` and `SELECT_G2.json` offspring genotypes. Each v4 gene is normalized to its fixed bounds, and exactly **24 offspring** per arm and population are aggregated into ONE population-level mean. No survivor-only filtering.

All declared genes, including `theta.*`, `p.*`, `w.*`, `gamma1..3`, `alpha`, `beta_u`, `lambda_r`, `n_base`, `h_max`, `m_max`, `belief_rate`, temperaments and `omega`, are evaluated. For each gene: G1 and G2 population-level mean S−M allele shift, G2 population SD, descriptive Pearson correlation of G2 gene shift against G2 S−M step difference, leave-one-population-out correlation range and sign stability, and descriptive differences between the ten adverse and 22 favorable populations. The tool preserves **all 32 populations** and **ALL genes** in its outputs; it publishes an absolute-correlation ranking for **hypothesis generation only**.

The **unit of analysis is population (n=32)**, not 24 genomes, 276 pairs, 144 life replicas or the 32,256 original bodies. Across-gene multiple comparisons, correlated genes, a phenotype-selected ranking, a possible selection collider and shared ancestry **invalidate confirmatory interpretation**; therefore there are **no gene p-values**, false-discovery claims or causal claims from this phase. Strong correlation is not proof of selection at that locus or a biological mediation mechanism. Results must be archived regardless of sign and are unusable for relabelling Stage8-C.

## Step 2: new prospectively falsifiable causal locus intervention (PROPOSAL ONLY)

Only **after the archival rankings are sealed**, nominate a **single** mechanistically interpretable, independently editable v4 locus (not `gamma1/2/3`, whose ordered constraints couple to other genes), record the exact gene and *predicted direction* based on evidence available then, and freeze the SHA/source, population count, alpha, effect-size target, null and negative controls **before any new seed or World exposure**. The nominee could also be **NONE** if the descriptive signal is weak or unstable.

A possible causal intervention is a **paired reciprocal G2 allele swap**, not a rule imposed on minds:

- In NEW independent G0/G1 S and M lineages, select native G2 offspring under the existing persistent-rank S and exactly multiplicity-matched M procedures. All parents and mutations are sealed before G2.
- For each G2 population and slot, keep `S_native`, `M_native` genomes as sham controls and create `S_swap`, `M_swap` offspring that exchange **only the preselected gene value** between the matched slots of S and M. All other declared genes and body/cognition configuration are identical to the corresponding sham sibling. `genome.validate` must succeed *for every planned swap* before any birth; any incompatibility is **TECHNICAL_NO_VERDICT**, never exclusion of a disadvantaged genome. No memory inheritance and no forced action.
- Expose all four G2 arms to **new, preregistered and paired** `stable/depletion/pulse` Worlds reserved BEFORE the first G0 birth, using the same intended physical opportunities, unchanged 400-step horizon and paid P1.3 funded-v2 cognition. Use distinct but precommitted life seeds for each individual identity and source-bound SHA receipts. Never tune which ecology supplies a favorable signal.
- Freeze a **single difference-in-differences test** per population: `T[p]=(S_native−M_native)_p−(S_swap−M_swap)_p` in mean G2 steps, and a predeclared *one-sided or two-sided* criterion based on the nominated directional mechanism. A positive `T` for a gene predicted to contribute to the S−M advantage is consistent with gene-dependent modulation; zero/negative falsifies this directional prediction **within the experimental power and World family**. Report native vs swap S−M, gene dosage, mortality and all 32 population contrasts even when unfavorable. This tests the **causal effect of that bounded genetic edit**, not proof of a specific natural molecular pathway or general evolutionary mechanism.
- Controls: equal births/World opportunities/horizon, no arbitrary motor choice, sham genome SHA identity, unchanged noncandidate loci and funds, equal G2 arm mutation protocol, sealed parent multiplicities S/M, blinded independent analysis on heldout science. Outcomes may be affected by epistasis, genotype-value constraints and other loci. A separate candidate or alternate direction needs a **different, prospectively justified** study, not a retry.

This is **not yet preregistered or source-admitted**, has no named locus, no computed sample-size/power or fixed alpha and has **no new born Worlds or technical rehearsals**. A full new test requires a separate immutable native generator, two source-exact complete 3.11/3.12 engineering repetitions before an independently seeded scientific cohort, new preexposure custody, read-only original-gzip reproduction, and strict no-retry. Owner ADR-0022 permits such research after those controls; it does not turn this design into a completed experiment. I1 active, I3 unregistered reference, Stage2.1 `NOT_ISSUED/HOLD_NO_VERDICT`.
