**Status: `SUPPLIED_TRACE_TWO_CURRENCY_CHAIN_MATCH_NOT_ATTESTED` on an invented single-life fixture, NOT a scientific verdict.** This is a bounded correction to a named validity gap in [#1395](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1395): the current native-original custody/selection bridge (#1397/#1401) verifies sealed archive shape and end-of-life funding envelopes but does not recalculate **per-step** evidence and trace heads. ## Exact falsifiable question

Given one fully supplied I3-funded-v2 *claimed* native original with a 400-step (or shorter deceased) `trace`, do its recorded steps reproduce the native `run_life` SHA-256 trace chain, its per-step evidence chain and final terminal seal, while each reported decision and learning phase satisfies the model's **two physical cognitive currencies** and the entire original's accounting totals?

`src/i3/lab/stage6_light_trace_funding_preflight.py` independently uses the existing read-only custody envelope check and exactly follows the current native reference's computations without importing `i3.modules` or `i3.runtime`, constructing a World or invoking a Mind:

- Require the source/World/genome/funding native-v2 envelope and current **deny-all Stage6 method**; a missing or incomplete `trace` fails closed rather than claiming a funding proof.
- Reconstruct the `trace_head` from the initial native genome hash, World name, life seed and horizon, then every original `(t, action, param, option, body, pos, dead)` record with its canonical hashed field order. Check monotonic `t`, continuing body across steps, and last-step death causes.
- Recompute `evidence_head` from the life `manifest_id`, each step's native `record`, joined cognitive work, two-currency accounting and both funding phases, then the terminal accounting seal.
- For both `decision` and `learning`, recompute declared energy/vigilance bills using the initial native v2 BodyLaws cost rates and four work counts; compare cost receipts with body-after-debit and per-step work totals. Check the complete energy/vigilance sums, inactivity/replays, last rounded body and action counts against the native lifetime summary. No scores, survival contrasts, gene shifts or parent ranks are emitted.
- Fail on absent/altered/reordered steps, invalid genome, missing phase, unpaid cognition, altered phase/body, broken SHA chains, false final aggregate and misdeclared actions. A trace is not patched or regenerated by the auditor.

## What PASS **cannot** mean

The test fixture invents **one** source-shaped 400-step trace, with no `World`, genome birth, physics, real motor or experimental exposure. This is a check of **declared funding equations and cryptographic self-consistency**, not a replay of the World transition or proof that Mind chose a stated action. A malicious source could fabricate a new trace, regenerate its digest chains and pass. `trace.info` is **not part of native `trace_head` or `evidence_head` and is ignored for scientific proof**; the audit exposes this fact explicitly.

No trust in the supplied native source pin, first-execution time, source checkout, independent seed commitment, original archival custody, individual motor authority or interpopulation independence is established. The check runs on **one independently supplied trace**, not all 11,520 possible Stage6 lives, and does not qualify the unbuilt full-scale G/S/R transport. Before any living Stage6, the exact authorized source must expose full traces and have a separate **complete original-by-original archive audit** with independent off-host commitments and source-attestation, plus the owner decisions and rehearsal specified in #1395. This module itself has no launch, admission, archive editing, seed allocation or World code.
