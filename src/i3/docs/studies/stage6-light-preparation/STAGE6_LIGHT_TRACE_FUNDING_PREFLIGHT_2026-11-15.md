## Exact falsifiable question

Given one fully supplied I3-funded-v2 *claimed* native original with a 400-step (or shorter deceased) `trace`, do its recorded steps reproduce the native `run_life` SHA-256 trace chain, its per-step evidence chain and final terminal seal, while each reported decision and learning phase satisfies the model's **two physical cognitive currencies** and the entire original's accounting totals?

`src/i3/lab/stage6_light_trace_funding_preflight.py` independently uses the existing read-only custody envelope check and exactly follows the current native reference's computations without importing `i3.modules` or `i3.runtime`, constructing a World or invoking a Mind:

- Require the source/World/genome/funding native-v2 envelope, **complete `BodyLaws` v2 snapshot** and current deny-all Stage6 method; a missing or incomplete `trace` fails closed rather than claiming a funding proof.
- Reconstruct the `trace_head` from the initial native genome hash, World name, life seed and horizon, then every original `(t, action, param, option, body, pos, dead)` record with its canonical hashed field order. Check monotonic `t`, continuing body across steps, and last-step death causes.
- Recompute `evidence_head` from the life `manifest_id`, each step's native `record`, joined cognitive work, two-currency accounting and both funding phases, then the terminal accounting seal.
- For both `decision` and `learning`, recompute declared energy/vigilance bills using the complete native v2 BodyLaws snapshot and four work counts; compare cost receipts with body-after-debit and per-step work totals. Check the complete energy/vigilance sums, inactivity/replays, last rounded body and action counts against the native lifetime summary. No scores, survival contrasts, gene shifts or parent ranks are emitted.
- **Hardening after #1402:** for actions with no resource gains (`None`, `attendre`, `dormir`, `collecter`, `se_deplacer`), independently recompute the full five-channel *body* transition from the declared laws, decision-phase work, passive food/water decay, sleep/metabolism/repair, motor energy and fatal channel thresholds. Compare it to the learning-phase incoming body and death causes. Check the elementary geometry and positional continuity of all actions against native `World._step`: only cardinal adjacent movement changes location. For `manger` and `boire` the resource effects remain **UNREPLAYED** since stocks, inventory and scheduled effects are not independently reconstructed.
- Require complete, exact native v2 law keys with positive conversion denominators. Reject a law snapshot containing only cognitive charge rates, even if all hash chains can be recomputed. This directly closes a false-positive in the original #1402 test fixture.
- Fail on absent/altered/reordered steps, invalid genome, missing phase, unpaid cognition, altered phase/body, broken SHA chains, false final aggregate and misdeclared actions. A trace is not patched or regenerated by the auditor.

## What PASS **cannot** mean

The corrected test fixture invents **one** source-shaped *shorter deceased* trace with pure passive BodyLaws arithmetic, with no `World`, genome birth, motor or experimental exposure. The former self-consistent 400-step no-op fixture wrongly omitted physical water/food decay; it now serves as a **negative regression** and is rejected even when its accounting, manifest and SHA chains are internally self-consistent. Reference parity tests compare the independent audit equations with the actual pure native `i3.modules.body.transition` for multiple states and effect-free actions. The output reports exactly how many effect-free transitions were checked and how many resource-effect steps remain opaque. This is **not** a complete World/ecology replay nor proof that Mind chose the recorded action, and a malicious source can still fabricate a physically plausible trace and reseal it. `trace.info` is **not part of native `trace_head` or `evidence_head` and is ignored for scientific proof**; the audit exposes this fact explicitly.

No trust in the supplied native source pin, first-execution time, source checkout, independent seed commitment, original archival custody, individual motor authority or interpopulation independence is established. The check runs on **one independently supplied trace**, not all 11,520 possible Stage6 lives, and does not qualify the unbuilt full-scale G/S/R transport.
