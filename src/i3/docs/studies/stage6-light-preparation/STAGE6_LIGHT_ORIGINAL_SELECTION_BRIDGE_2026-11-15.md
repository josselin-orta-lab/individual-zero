## Falsifiable question and controls

Earlier [#1397](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/1397) checked gzip originals and their native-v2 envelope, [#1399](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/1399) checked caller-declared genome-v4 ancestry, and [#1400](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/pull/1400) checked invented persistence and parent draws. These independent positive flags **cannot** establish that the selection inputs came from the same sealed original lives as the claimed offspring. The question here is narrower: **given a fully supplied three-generation native-shaped archive and declared parents, can the offline auditor derive every persistence value from the original gzip bytes instead of accepting a caller's score table, then reject parent/genome claims that disagree?**

Falsifiers are any absent/modified/unsafe original; changed/rebound generation manifest; missing, duplicate, switched, mismatched or extra G0/G1 opportunity; genome mismatch between six contexts or with G0/G1/G2 genotype ancestry; missing G/S draw, seed repeat, selected-vs-uniform rule mismatch, wrong source generation manifest SHA, different parent/offspring digest; changed cognition funding/terminal record; or non-inert method. Invalid input fails closed without adjusting weights or filling missing lives. The random-parent branch does **not** read parent persistence for its choice.

## Mechanism and audit order

1. `stage6_light_original_selection_bridge.inspect_original_selection(...)` checks the unmodified deny-all method *before* opening data, then delegates the **full** original-byte, manifest, WorldSpec/source and funding-envelope audit to `stage6_light_native_custody_preflight.inspect_three_generation_shape`: 2,304 G0 unique + 4,608 G1 + 4,608 G2 = **11,520** supplied originals and 288 logically distinct claimed World opportunities. All roots must be distinct, regular directories, not symlinks. A newly exposed **per-generation manifest SHA-256** in the custody report lets the bridge insist that a later read sees the same manifest bytes.
2. Independently reopen, hash, decompress with size bound, and validate **all 6,912 G0 and G1 original gzip records** to extract only `(terminal == censored, steps_lived)`, six contexts per genotype. All original identities and full-v2 funding-envelope checks must still pass. During the comparison, no externally supplied persistence scores are read.
3. Derive the **1,920** genome digests from the three sealed generation manifests. The previously audited original bytes for G2 remain subject to the custody pass. Join those digests to the **384** declared G0 genomes and **1,536** declared native-v4 `mutate(scale=0.1)` G1/G2 genotypes via the existing #1399 standalone recomputation.
4. Recompute the **1,536** supplied parent draws. For `selected`, rank by `(number of censored lives desc, total lived steps desc, slot asc)` and apply the specified 24..1 weights; for `random_parent`, use uniform `randrange(24)` with no persistence read. Compare selected parent, source-generation manifest SHA, native parent genome, child genome and mutation chain. The drawing seeds are **supplied claims, not prospective authorisations**. Require a unique declared draw-seed integer per child.
5. Return only source-scope **structural counts** and explicit negative scientific/authorization flags, not any life results, gene contrasts, survival summary, ranking or experimental GO. Deny callable `run_life`, `open_world` and `authorize_science` endpoints.

Adversarial tests use small manufactured native-v2 gzip originals to reject corruption, unsafe filenames, substituted identity and unpaid vigilance, and a **fabricated full 11,520-record panel** to test end-to-end parsing and parent draws. Python 3.11/3.12 exact-source CI is required to adopt this patch. The fabricated 11,520 rows **are not 11,520 lives lived by I3**.

## Scientific, operational and custody limits

- **No independent historical authenticity**: a self-consistent archive, source pin, manifest SHA, draw table and mutation tree can all be generated after seeing an outcome. Neither this bridge nor a Git commit proves an independently witnessed first-execution timestamp, that a source ran, or that the user-provided pins came from an independent authority. A malicious author could choose a seed *after* choosing a desired parent. No actual S(g) receipt tied to a protected, prior seed commitment exists.
- **No real prospective population independence**: the 288 World slots and 384 founders are a fixed **symbolic plan**, not allocated scientific indices or attestations of ecological independence. The archive reader checks shape and declared paid-energy/vigilance totals, **not** full physical action-by-action transition/funding replay; it never reconstructs a living individual's subjective choice.
- **No online chronological G/S/R guarantee**: the native archives are inspected **after** creation. The bridge cannot prove G(g) closed before S(g) was computed, S(g) preceded the next generation, or that the files could not be replaced atomically by their owner. Concurrent modification is not a supported threat model: audit immutable snapshot copies with independently controlled custody for any later external usage.
- **Stop-rule remains**: method JSON and all World/cohort/engineering/scientific permissions are false/null. The bridge never accepts an owner authorisation argument and cannot issue one. A future living transport requires a new source-exact, authorized implementation. Any missing archive ends the audit as invalid input, not a death and not a license to retry.
