## Primary mechanistic endpoint

For each candidate life, preserve the runtime's paid frontier counters and
classify prospectively:

- `NO_WATER_EXPERIENCE_BOOTSTRAP_NOT_OBSERVED`: no positive water uptake and
  no actionable-water query;
- `WATER_EXPERIENCED_BUT_NO_ACTIONABLE_MODEL`: positive water uptake occurs
  but the candidate never obtains an actionable-water frontier belief;
- `ACTIONABLE_MODEL_BUT_ZERO_INFORMATION`: actionable-water belief occurs but
  no paid frontier option has information value > 1e-12;
- `POSITIVE_FRONTIER_INFORMATION_OBSERVED`: at least one paid frontier option
  has information value > 1e-12.

The **engineering continuation rule** is:

`at least one candidate life == POSITIVE_FRONTIER_INFORMATION_OBSERVED`.

Meeting this rule establishes only that the #1082 mechanism becomes operative
inside a fresh natural life. It does not establish survival non-inferiority,
revision superiority, food-risk information, multi-cell information, family-R
success, architecture promotion or scientific GO.

Failure is also informative:

- no water experience means bootstrap exposure was NOT_OBSERVED;
- water experience without actionable belief falsifies the current support
  bridge;
- actionable belief with zero information falsifies the current one-cell
  water continuation mechanism on this panel.

No category may be relabeled as success because a different arm survives.
