## Question

Did the frontier-water mechanism fail because the information calculation never
became available, because cognition became unaffordable, or because useful
information appears only after the first personally useful water experience?

## Answer

The third explanation is directly supported.

Candidate summary:

- 12 lives;
- 2668 paid frontier-belief queries;
- 680 actionable-water queries;
- 1360 frontier options scored;
- 121 positive-information options;
- 7 lives with positive information;
- 5 lives with no water experience and no positive information.

For every one of the seven positive-information lives:

`first_positive_information_step = first_positive_water_step + 1`.

There is no exception in the archived reductions.

This is exactly the transition expected from the #1080 conservative belief:
the structural probability of an unseen resource exists before experience, but
the bodily consequence of water is not actionably modelled until a positive
personal drink effect has been learned and retained in paid branch memory.

## Before bootstrap

The five no-positive-information lives all die and average 113.4 lived steps.
They contain zero water uptake.

Three of those lives show the most recognizable old curiosity-ablation pattern:

- stable-50-g901: 54 waits, 18 moves;
- stable-51-g901: 106 waits, 4 moves;
- pulse-50-g902: 68 waits, 2 moves.

Two others move more but still fail to encounter water before death.

The result therefore does not reduce to one deterministic "wait" rule. The
common property is absence of a personally supported water consequence, hence
absence of water-frontier information value.

## After bootstrap

The seven positive-information lives average 305.3 lived steps and include both
candidate survivors.

Positive information is therefore compatible with substantially longer lives,
but this is **selection on an endogenous exposure**, not evidence that the
information term caused the longer lifespan. Individuals must survive and find
water first to enter this group.

Five of seven still die. Three die from starvation because food information is
explicitly not implemented; two additional positive-information lives still die
from dehydration.

## Scientific interpretation

The scenario's principle remains intact: information should be valued through
predicted bodily consequences, not an external bonus.

The diagnostic exposes a necessary companion principle:

> A value-of-information mechanism cannot value the first observation of an
> unknown consequence unless the individual already has some generic belief
> over what that consequence might be.

The current #1080 rule intentionally refuses to invent such a beneficial or
harmful bodily prior. That made the experiment clean, and it now makes the
bootstrap limitation visible.

This does **not** justify choosing a favorable water prior after the fact.
50/51 are exposed and closed. Any new prior must be generic across action names,
tokens and worlds, prospectively frozen, and derived from first-person or
heritable generic structure rather than from these outcomes.

Stage 2 is not the remedy. The problem is the support of the belief distribution,
not the number of unique computations purchased.
