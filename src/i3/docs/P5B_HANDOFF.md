## First-pass P5b evidence, prospectively registered before exposure

[Protocol](P5B_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) and
[unchanged full original data, checksums, receipts and report](studies/p5b-paid-readout-heldout/RESULTS.md).
Exactly **1152 lives** from 48 unmodified frozen G2 genotypes,
24 wholly new validation worlds at indices 72–95, four original
genetic populations and four paid readout modes:

| Readout mode | Censored at step 400 /288 | Physical inactivity during lived steps |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Full learned action effects | 98 | 19.56% |
| Blind only simulated action-effect predictions | 0 | 75.65% |
| Personally learned but action-agnostic effect mean | 0 | 75.64% |
| Blind only learned sleep-restoration forecast | 90 | 15.52% |

Each individual actually computed, paid for and **retained**
its learned effect model, with intact sensory/motor orientation,
place histories, body physics and nominal work tariffs. This differs
fundamentally from the earlier P5a `discard_effects` intervention,
which removed stored effect updates entirely.

Paired n=4 genetic-population survival contrasts full versus either
action-readout-blind or action-agnostic mode are
**[+19,+31,+23,+25] survivors per 72**, exploratory mean +24.5,
95% Student-t interval [+16.54,+32.46] but **exact two-sided
sign-flip p=0.125** and exploratory three-comparison Holm p=0.375.
This remains an exploratory *within-this-substrate* mechanism
finding, **not** conventional confirmatory inference or broad
ecological/genetic generalization.

The sleep-only contrast is inconsistent
**[-2,+8,+2,0]**, exact p=0.75. It does not demonstrate a
universal absence of sleep/consolidation benefits.

## Action forecasts, scoring and the causal gap

In each of 288 prospectively paired `full` vs treatment lives,
the first 12 *prepaid* decision steps preserve scored option
identity, body state at the start of decision, base projected return,
curiosity/risk bonus and total score. At a matched body and before
a different physical action occurs, the same option had a different
total score in **288/288** action-blind pairs, **285/288**
action-agnostic pairs and **44/288** sleep-blind pairs.

Crucially, the *base projected return* differed in **288/288**
action-blind pairs but only **79/288** action-agnostic pairs.
Thus it would be an **incorrect interpretation** to ascribe the
285/288 differences of the agnostic control to the base forecast
alone: uncertainty bonuses, risks and possibly hidden cognitive
context contribute. A matched pre-decision body is not a
matched full mind state. The witness is observational and
cannot alone establish single-channel causality.

When action-specific predictions are masked, the model becomes
physically inactive for roughly three quarters of steps lived and
spends far less cumulative modeled energy on cognition. This
change arises during a divergent life; **initial nominal pricing
parity is not equal realized computational work**. An
information-only causal interpretation would require new
interventions, not a post-hoc adjustment of bills.

## Priority decision for continued development

**Preserve the existing functional prototype.**
The next question is *which component of using an action-specific
effect model changes behavior*:
- Separate the **base projected return** from **uncertainty-weighted
  curiosity, integrity risk and control admissibility**. A control
  should replace only one already paid readout contribution at
  a time without privileged information, new action proposals,
  unbilled computations or rescue heuristics.
- Observe the **first matched-state decision** before the agent's
  body/energy trajectory separates. Report measured available
  resources, action families offered, funded and selected, and
  when no-processing-budget states begin, without conditioning
  away downstream consequences in survival inference.
- Prospectively freeze source, extra truly independent genetic
  populations if seeking broader inference, untouched world
  indices **>=96**, ecology family, cognitive work price rates,
  primary and mechanistic endpoints, multiplicity and adverse
  outcomes. Do not tune or reopen P5b's 72–95, P5a's 48–71,
  P4b's 24–47, P4a's 0–23, selection worlds or the reserve.
- A *different* remaining research question is acquired
  individuality: an identical inherited genome acquires distinct
  first-person histories, then the **same surviving continuous
  individual** faces a prospectively identical novel challenge.
  Record stable acquired behavior and counterfactual self-relevant
  valuations, rather than inferring a self from unequal final
  memory hashes. No memory may pass between births.

I3's first-pass evidence now supports a bounded claim that
**reading consequences associated with specific actions is central
to sustained active behavior in the present architecture and
ecological sample**. It does not establish evolutionary
advantage of selected parents (P4a remained mixed), universal
optimal survival, autonomous selfhood, or subjective experience.

**No scientific GO and no engine activation.**

## Post-hoc readout-score decomposition (hypothesis generation only)

The [P5b original-only bonus/base audit](studies/p5b-paid-readout-heldout/POSTHOC_SCORE_TERMS.md)
subsequently decomposed the actually funded option score into its
base projected return and curiosity/risk bonus before the first
paired physical-action difference. Across 288 genome/world pairs
for the personal action-agnostic prior, **4,587** option comparisons
had a bonus-only scoring difference and **273/288 pairs** had at
least one such instance; only **650** option comparisons had a
base-only scoring difference. These are repeated candidate-level
observations, not 4,587 independent individuals, and the analysis
was **not preregistered**. It motivates testing bonus components
separately but cannot be used to revise P5b primary endpoints.
