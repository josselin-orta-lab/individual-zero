## Owner decision

**8 November 2026: imagined and perceived first contact share the same prior.**

An action the individual has never performed carries one inherited expectation,
whether the trial is imagined at an unseen cell or offered by a perceived
resource. Both use the same primitive,
`paid_recent_rms_zero_mean_effect_prior`, with the same genome ω and the same
branch memory:

- the imagined trial wraps it in the existing three-leaf target prior, because
  the resource itself is still uncertain;
- the perceived trial uses it directly, because the resource is perceived.

Consequence: at ω ≠ 0 this modifies Candidate-H's imagined probe, which froze
ω = 0. At ω = 0 Candidate-H is reproduced exactly.

## Question

With one inherited prior shared by imagined and perceived first contact, does
some ω > 0 make a first trial relevant where ω = 0 does not, while paying
everything and leaving every non-relevant decision exactly P1.3?

**Hypothesis.** ω gives first contact a generic, inheritable expectation that
can change a decision at current physical prices.

**Falsifier.** No ω > 0 makes the perceived first drink relevant in any frozen
state where ω = 0 does not (`NO_CAPACITY`).

## Declared expectations, before implementation

Recorded so that the outcome can contradict them. They are not acceptance
criteria and change no rule.

- **Composition of the hope.** On the frozen `effect_memory()`, the personal RMS
  scale is `s = (2.0915, 0.2598, 0.4000, 0.1155, 2.3094)` for energy, food,
  water, integrity and vigilance; the expected external effect is `ω × s`. The
  hope therefore mostly anticipates energy and vigilance relief. Its water part,
  `0.4 × ω`, is about two orders of magnitude below a real drink (+25 to +40).
- **Narrow capacity.** R1 is expected to hold for ω ≥ 0.5 in a few P80 states,
  where water or food is the low channel and energy and vigilance are
  comfortable; not where energy or vigilance is low, since sleep's known effect
  should dominate.
- **Need-agnostic trials.** The perceived drink is expected to become RELEVANT
  in some food-deficit states as well.
- **F108** is expected to behave like P80, more weakly (food hope `0.26 × ω`).
- **K80** is expected to gain a few RELEVANT states as ω rises, as in the ω
  software falsifier.

## Scope

- **First contact only.** Before any personal observation of the trial's action
  at the action level, where the effects model would fall back to `("*",)`.
- **Imagined path.** Candidate-H's first-contact probe with personal recurrence,
  unchanged except for the ω it passes.
- **Perceived path.** A single-action plan offered by a perceived resource
  (`boire` on perceived water, `manger:<token>` with a carried token).
- **Out of scope.** A new token whose action is personally known, travel plans
  ending in an unsupported action, imagined first contact without recurrence,
  and post-contact paths (Candidate-H post-contact stays as frozen in #1163).
- **Laboratory mechanism**, like Candidate-H: no runtime mind, no Competition, no
  motor authorization. Imagined and perceived trials are exercised on separate
  fixtures, so their effects are attributed separately. Their coexistence in
  one decision is left to a later integration gate.

## Mechanism

### Stage A, imagined

Candidate-H's frozen first-contact Stage A, with one difference:
`paid_water_personal_recurrence_value` passes the genome ω to its inner
one-future-opportunity kernel. Candidate-H's frozen relevance rule is
unchanged:

- `TRIAL` is in `target_node_best_choices`;
- trial information value > `1e-12`;
- trial advantage > `1e-12`.

### Stage A, perceived

From the fixture body, with a real `CognitiveBudget`, under the canonical
projector (`candidate_f`, `event_local_quadratic4`):

1. Quote one common ceiling covering the complete trial matrix and the four
   ordinary plans of #1161 (`wait`, `sleep`, `move`, `move_wait`).
2. Build the prior: `paid_recent_rms_zero_mean_effect_prior(branch_memory,
   budget, omega=<genome ω>)`.
3. Evaluate the trial matrix: the nested comparison of
   `paid_unknown_action_trial_value` (no trial, frozen, informed), with its
   prior source replaced by (2). Frozen continuations: for no trial, the empty
   plan; after the trial, the empty plan and one exact repeat of the trial key.
4. Score the four ordinary plans from the same forecast body.
5. Pay all actual work. The probe cannot authorize a motor command.

**Perceived relevance.** A state is RELEVANT only if the trial's informed value
exceeds, by more than `1e-12`, both the no-trial value and every ordinary plan
value. This is a decision-top effect under one score geometry, as #1163
requires. Information value is reported but not required, because ω is defined
to act on expectation.

### Stage B, both paths, as in Candidate-H

For a FALLBACK state:

1. Start a fresh phase from Stage A's actual `body_after_computation`.
2. Score under P1.3 `reference/chunk_end`, from one quoted common ceiling:
    - the four ordinary plans;
    - for the perceived path, also the perceived option with its unchanged
      effects-model forecast.
3. Pay every actual reference simulation. No Stage-A value is reused.

If Stage B cannot be funded, the state is `FALLBACK_UNFUNDED`. It is never
converted into a Candidate-F decision. A RELEVANT state executes no Stage B.

### Controls

- **Same-body reference control:** Stage B's scorer on a fresh budget from the
  same `body_after_computation`.
- **Pre-probe control:** Stage B's scorer from the original fixture body. For
  the perceived path, this is the valuation the 56/57 lives used.

## Arms

ω ∈ {−1, −0.5, 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1}, the grid of the ω software falsifier, applied
to both paths at once.

## What each verdict authorizes

- **CAPACITY_SHOWN.** An owner decision on a separately frozen slice of the
  unified Candidate-H runtime in which FALLBACK routes drop the curiosity and
  prudence addends and first contact uses the shared prior, compared with
  Candidate-H as is on fresh DEVELOPMENT worlds. If both remain viable, a
  prospectively frozen ADR-0015 selection gate follows, with ω free and an
  ω := 0 ablation at equal genomes. That gate must report the distribution of
  selected ω and its mass near the +1 bound. Saturation would mean that the
  prior's scale, not the gene, limits first contact. It must be reported as
  such, never answered by widening the bound.
- **NO_CAPACITY.** Record that ω cannot make perceived first contact pay through
  expectation, at current prices, with the personal RMS scale. K80 and F108
  remain informative. Return to the decision note: keep the P1.3 curiosity
  addend as an acknowledged designer term (option B), or take a new owner
  decision. Enlarging the prior scale or the ω range afterwards would be
  calibration and is excluded.
- **DEFECT.** Fix the implementation, re-freeze, and rerun on the same fixtures.

## Interpretation boundary

At first contact the individual acts on an inherited expectation, not on lived
evidence. A positive result supports a first-contact disposition carried by the
lineage and set by persistence, as ADR-0014 intends. It is not evidence that
the individual evaluates the unknown from its own experienced stakes, and must
not be reported as such.

## Sequencing

This slice runs at laboratory level. It does not touch the runtime frozen for
the 58/59 pilot and must not be merged into it. It changes only the prior's ω

Keep it minimal: its purpose is to reach the selection gate above, where the

## Known limits

- The RMS scale comes from recent generic steps. Its water part is small and
  its energy and vigilance parts dominate (see the declared expectations).
  `NO_CAPACITY` would be a legitimate result, not a reason to rescale.
- Imagined first contact without recurrence, the case of a newborn that has
  never seen a resource recur, stays outside Candidate-H and this slice. The
  56/57 audit suggests that exploration at birth, more than perceived first
  contact, limits survival. Extending the shared prior to that path is the
  expected next slice.
- Relevance gating removes policy drift, not cost. In #1162 fallback states the
  combined work (503.36 simulated steps) exceeds the probe alone (470.05). #1163
  found 68/80 post-contact states relevant, and the runtime pays a probe plus a
  complete final decision. Over a life, probe frequency is expected to dominate
  cost. Whether to run a probe at all is a value-of-computation question
  (Stage 2), which this slice does not address.
- The two paths keep different relevance rules: Candidate-H's frozen rule for
  the imagined trial, a decision-top rule for the perceived one. The prior is
  shared; the rules are not changed here.
- A strict raw winner is not a Competition outcome, and software states are not
  lives. Capacity is not survival, adaptation or individuality.
- The lifetime cost of activation is not assessed.
