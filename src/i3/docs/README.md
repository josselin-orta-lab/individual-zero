## Stage-0 software slices

[Eight-population F0 design and energy receipts](STAGE0_DESIGN.md) implement
deterministic planning, complete persistence-only candidate ranking and
population sign-test arithmetic, plus observer-side checks of actual phase
work, body debits and original life evidence chains. Complete declared pairs
are summarized by population; deaths and unequal lived time remain visible.
The full Competition observer is checked against observer-off software fixtures,
including RNG state. No new cognition or default observer is introduced.

The plan prepares identifiers for eight new candidate pools, not evolved
lineages; no selection/validation world is instantiated. It is not a
preregistration. [Revision contract and endpoint](STAGE0_REVISION.md) distinguish
local available absence, paid processing and positive uptake at another source;
no exposure, failure and death remain explicit. Next: bind this definition and
the bounded Stage-1 candidate to source/endpoint/margin/multiplicity locks and
study-specific cases, rather than expand the observer framework.
Lineage controls and the existing #1026/#1054 questions remain open.

[Paid information-value kernel](STAGE1_INFORMATION_VALUE.md), issue #1061:

## Stage-1 personal-outcome construction

`modules/personal_outcomes.py` connects a private bounded history of applied
bodily consequences to the existing kernel. Only the known own decision debit
is removed; recording, retrieval and construction are themselves paid. Latest
up to three exact-key outcomes, otherwise same-action experience, define an
explicit empirical approximation. No support remains unknown, not optimistic.
Same-key persistence and body-only signals are documented in the
[Stage-1 contract](STAGE1_INFORMATION_VALUE.md#personal-outcome-constructor).

This is not a calibrated posterior or spatial exploration model. Its original
no-support behavior is unchanged; the local prototype explicitly supplies its
own stationary-ignorance assumption rather than altering the constructor.
