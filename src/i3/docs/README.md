I3 is an independent **research proposal**, not a registered or activated engine. The [engine contract](../engine.json) declares no entrypoints, and [the registry](../../../config/engine-registry.json) continues to identify I1 as active. A green software test does not establish a scientific GO.

Read these in order, consulting the original git commit pinned in each preregistration:

2. [ADR-0014](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0014-i3-lignee-enjeu-de-soi.md): selection on individual persistence and self-relevant vital accounting.
3. [Specification](SPECIFICATION.md) and [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md): generation-owned physical and cognitive boundaries.
4. [Test plan](TEST_PLAN.md): software checks and evidence/cost limitations of executed P4a/P4b.
5. Original G2 [preregistration](G2_FIRST_FALSIFIER_PREREG.md) and [observations](studies/g2-first-falsifier/RESULTS.md).
6. Original G2b P1.1 [preregistration](G2B_P11_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) and [observations](studies/g2b-p11-heldout/RESULTS.md).
7. Original G2c P1.2 [preregistration](G2C_P12_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) and [negative result](studies/g2c-p12-heldout/RESULTS.md).

The non-adopted P1.2 implementation is preserved on branch `i3/p1.2-variant`. Do not retroactively change existing held-out gates, reuse their evaluation seeds for model selection, or describe an engineering PASS as scientific confirmation.

The first I3 lineage pilot is [prospectively preregistered](P3_PILOT_PREREG.md) and its
[complete mixed results, frozen G2 genomes and unaltered evidence](studies/p3-mutation-pilot/RESULTS.md)
are archived. Selection outcomes are **not** held-out validation and do not
prove an evolutionary advantage. P4a has since been [preregistered](P4A_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) and its
[inconclusive held-out result](studies/p4a-g2-heldout/RESULTS.md) archived.
P4b was separately [preregistered](P4B_LEARNING_PREREG.md), and its
[paid learning-retention/individual-history result](studies/p4b-paid-outcome-learning/RESULTS.md)
is archived without reopening P4a worlds. No scientific GO: the strong P4b
retention effect is limited to learned effects/value/replay and includes
differences in actual cognition and activity; spatial belief learning
remains active in both modes. Reserve worlds remain locked.

The existing focused tests are `python -m unittest discover -s tests/i3 -p 'test_*.py' -v`. Any future scientific study needs a prospective preregistration and newly reserved environments.

## P5a next bounded research intervention

[Selective paid-retention engineering contract](P5A_SELECTIVE_RETENTION_CONTRACT.md)
separates persistent effect statistics, TD value coefficients and sleep replay
without altering the default life or reopening any validation world. These
controls were followed by a separately [preregistered P5a mechanism screen](P5A_SCREEN_PREREG.md) whose [full original results](studies/p5a-paid-component-screen/RESULTS.md) are now archived. Across 1152 lives, retaining learned action effects made the largest difference: 112/288 normal survivors versus 0/288 without persistent effects. Removing TD values (117/288) or incremental replay retention (119/288) did not produce the same collapse; signs vary between populations. Four genetic populations, actual cognitive cost mediation, and exploratory multiplicity prevent a general scientific GO. See [P5a handoff](P5A_HANDOFF.md).
Future validation requires independent prospective preregistration, fresh
allocation, adequate population-level replication and actual paid-cost
mediation analysis. See [#1026](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1026).

## P5b decision readout diagnostic (development only)

[Readout-control contract](P5B_EFFECT_READOUT_CONTRACT.md) and
[reproducible first diagnostic findings](P5B_DEVELOPMENT_FINDINGS.md)
separate learned action predictions, learned sleep forecasts and
learned source-positivity gates without removing the genuinely paid
and retained effect model. The observer records offered/scored options,
rejections and processing-budget losses but does not influence decisions.
Across 12 matched engineering lives per condition, suppressing action
predictions increased inactivity from 109 to 841 of 1370 lived steps;
all conditions had 8/12 reach 120 steps. **Not a held-out scientific
study or evidence of generalized survival benefit.** Next design question:
which first-person uninformed effect interface is structurally sound?
See [#1035](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1035).

### P5b action-independent empirical comparator (development only)

The [action-agnostic personal-prior diagnostic](P5B_AGNOSTIC_PRIOR_FINDINGS.md)
adds a comparator that predicts the **same personally observed global
body-change mean** for every contemplated action, keeping the original
paid planning interface and first-person learned effects intact. Its
12 matched development lives reached the 120-step horizon in 8/12
cases like the normal group, but showed **894 inactive steps versus
109 normal**, with 891 no-processing-budget events. A generic learned
magnitude did not restore action-specific activity; this is **not**
a held-out scientific survival claim. P5b validation remains NOT_RUN.

### P5b matched decision chronology (development only)

[Post-life paired chronology](P5B_DIVERGENCE_CHRONOLOGY.md) now distinguishes
the first physical action, body state, actually paid decision work and
funding-status differences across 12 matched engineering lives per readout.
The action-agnostic prior first diverges in action at step 4 and its earliest
funding-status divergence is step 33 across the fixture; these are minima
across pairs, not a causal claim. **No P5b held-out study has run.**
