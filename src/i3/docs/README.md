## Latest: full exact-trace cache is funded but physically too expensive

Frozen accounting sensitivity on the #1146 80-state grid
([results](studies/stage1-separable-cache-accounting/RESULTS.md)) completes all
80 states under both an optimistic and conservative compute-price bound. The
funding classification is `CACHE_ACCOUNTING_ROBUSTLY_FUNDED`.

That does **not** make the candidate affordable enough to advance. Mean physical
bill is 7.759 energy / 3.532 vigilance even under the optimistic arm, versus
1.340 / 1.840 for Candidate-F. The conservative arm costs 17.184 / 12.957.
The frozen runtime-advance rule therefore fails.

The dominant cost is now identified: each fresh exact trace stores 1,035 paid
memory items, while only 7.6% of trace accesses are hits. Before adding another
cache, test the simpler exact optimization already latent in the model:
**per-step projection truncated at first predicted death**, with the absorbing
tail summed analytically. Only if that remains too costly should sparse exact
checkpoints be opened.

## Latest: exact separable projection cache passes its structural gate

Frozen-before-code characterization
([results](studies/stage1-separable-projection-cache/RESULTS.md)) finds that the
255-step per-step projection can be decomposed into exact channel-local traces
and reused inside one decision batch. Maximum pairwise decision bias is
**2.22e-15**, projection-equivalent gap **1.33e-15**, and final-body error
**0**. The frozen structural verdict is
`SEPARABLE_EXACT_CACHE_STRUCTURAL_GATE_PASS`.

Reuse is real: the 486-case grid records 6318 trace builds / 972 hits; the
nonlinear #1150 set 250 builds / 50 hits. This is **not yet an affordability
claim**. I3 has no frozen physical price for a channel-local distress sample,
so the next slice must freeze storage/build/read accounting and run the #1146
kernel under a real `CognitiveBudget`. No runtime mode, World or life is
authorized.

## Current handoff after the 7 November Stage-1 diagnostics

The original local-information prototype is now a **failed engineering
prototype, not the Stage-1 gate candidate**.

The first frozen 44/45 diagnostic exposed computational paralysis. Exact
duplicate-work corrections then reduced that bottleneck. A second, independently
frozen 46/47 diagnostic met its engineering continuation rule (22.49% candidate
inactivity and 12 positive water uptakes), but the candidate still had **0/12
survivors** versus reference 4/12. It slept on 69.25% of lived steps; all 8666
paid information scores had zero information increment. In stable/pulse, 400
funded decisions at sensed water <=20 offered no local drink option. See the
[cost-reduced results](studies/stage1-cost-reduced-development/RESULTS.md) and
[read-only audit](studies/stage1-cost-reduced-development/POSTHOC_INFORMATION_ZERO_AUDIT.md).

**DRIFT DETECTED:** do not keep tuning `local_information_v1`, add Stage-2
budget heuristics, force resources or rewrite Competition to rescue it.

The replacement path now preserves P1.3's repertoire and learned state:
[reference integration contract](STAGE1_REFERENCE_INTEGRATION.md).
Implemented software prerequisites are:

- fixed-capacity 18-record paid empirical branch memory, empty at birth;
- `reference_branch_memory_v1`, a storage-only P1.3 cost control whose decision
  path and replay allocation remain reference-identical before extra storage;
- paid exhaustive plan-level information kernels, including branch-specific
  plans that can appear only after an observable result;
- a conservative first-person frontier prior that reuses P1.3's existing
  Laplace resource rate and never invents actionable newborn effects;
- the opt-in `reference_frontier_information_v1` engineering probe, which
  preserves P1.3 repertoire/state/Competition/control and replaces old additive
  curiosity/relevance/prudence with **one-cell water-frontier information only**.

The fresh reference-preserving mechanism now has one complete development
result: [50/51 results](studies/stage1-frontier-water-development-50-51/RESULTS.md)
and [bootstrap audit](studies/stage1-frontier-water-development-50-51/POSTHOC_BOOTSTRAP_AUDIT.md).

Its engineering rule is met: 7/12 candidate lives exhibit positive frontier
information, 121 options score positive information, and in every such life the
first positive information score occurs exactly one step after first positive
water uptake. The mechanism is therefore genuinely online after relevant
personal experience.

But the candidate survives only 2/12 versus reference 3/12 and storage-only
6/12. Five candidate lives never experience water, never activate positive
information and all die. The candidate is usually funded, so this is not the
old computation-paralysis failure. It is a **bootstrap limitation**: before the
first useful water experience, the conservative no-oracle belief provides no
actionable bodily consequence for water discovery.

Do not tune 50/51, restore curiosity as a hidden bonus, invent favorable water
behind an unseen frontier, or jump to Stage 2.

The bootstrap is now decomposed in software, with no new lives:

- [zero-mean stake prior](STAGE1_BOOTSTRAP_PRIOR.md): two opposite unsupported
  bodily effects, p=.5/.5, exact zero expected external delta and no action or
  World input (#1088);
- exact branch-local simulation after one effect becomes observable, without
  mutating the real Mind or transferring the sample to other actions (#1089);
- nested unsupported-action trial value, explicitly separating
  `information_value` from `trial_advantage`; a synthetic test demonstrates
  positive information from the zero-mean prior (#1090);
- a symmetric first-person modality prior over no resource / water / token,
  extending P1.3's existing Laplace resource-rate principle without body utility
  or token identity (#1091).

**DRIFT DETECTED:** the source scenario explicitly budgets only 2-3 simulated
outcome branches for Stage 1. Do not naively compose modality 3 x effect 2 into
six leaves. The next design must keep the complete frontier/bootstrap
uncertainty within three outcome branches while preserving the distinction
between observing a resource and learning its effect.

Food-effect risk and multi-cell joint frontier outcomes remain NOT_IMPLEMENTED.
Three candidate deaths are starvation deaths, but seven are dehydration deaths,
so food information alone cannot explain the dominant failure.


The later bounded software work sharpens the diagnosis further. Water without a
represented future matching opportunity gives zero information (#1099), and
exact P1.3 explorer semantics select 0/80 epistemic water trials (#1101).
Carried-food information is genuine (#1102): a rich competing-needs audit moves
the meal's rank in 9/27 states (#1104). But after all ordinary alternatives and
the information meal share one complete cognition-depleted forecast body,
27/27 states are fundable, information remains positive in 9/27, and the raw
winner changes in **0/27** (#1105).

So Stage 1 is no longer blocked by information arithmetic or by a hidden
cost-parity ambiguity. It is blocked by P1.3's sparse representation of
**future matching opportunities**.

**Current handoff:** no more local water/food score patches. Before another live
Stage-1 candidate, test one minimal generic first-person future-opportunity
representation, empty at birth and learned from personal observations only.
This is an evidence-driven Stage-1 prerequisite, not the start of full Stage 3.
Stage 2 remains blocked.

P1.3 remains the reference. I3 remains unregistered. No validation or reserve
world has been opened.
