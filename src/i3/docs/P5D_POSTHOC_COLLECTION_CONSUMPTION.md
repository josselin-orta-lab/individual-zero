## Stock and consumption reconstructed from original first-person receipts

Each original step includes the first-person `capture_inventory` before action and the actually executed physical inventory delta for `collecter` (+1) or `manger` (-1). The independent custodian reconstructs balances for every life and rejects unaccounted inventory disappearance, missing/duplicate physical time, negative inventory, fabricated ingestion, unpaid cognitive phase or work after death. The last balance is therefore a **derived observer fact**, not a newly invented recorded terminal field.

- Scored curiosity **normal**: 10 collected tokens, 7 actually eaten, **3 left in the terminal inventory** of one censored life (development index 35, engineering genome seed 902).
- Curiosity bonus **discarded**: 11 collected tokens, **0 eaten**, all **11 left in terminal inventory** of two censored lives (index 35, seeds 901/902 with **6 and 5** tokens respectively). These lives did not die within the 120-step horizon.
- The normal arm contains seven matched FIFO collection-to-ingestion lags of **2, 53, 4, 2, 33, 2, 81** lived steps. FIFO matching is an accounting convention per token type, not a reconstruction of which physical item was eaten; nothing is charged or replayed.
- Curiosity masked produces **125 steps with nonempty carried inventory**, compared with **180** in normal, despite the absence of any physical eating. No counterfactual eating decision has been generated.

## Interpretation and explicit limits

The data falsify the simplifying narrative that absence of scored curiosity merely causes the individual to stop *issuing* commands, or that resource collection is equivalent to nourishing itself. The individuals still choose rest/wait, sometimes collect, and do not necessarily consume what they carry. **The data do not establish why they refrain from eating, nor that non-consumption caused dehydration or later death.** In the masked arm the three observed deaths are from water zero, and the two lives with retained food are censored survivors.

The 12 paired conditions diverged in physical actions at step 1 (10 pairs) or step 2 (2 pairs). In these short pre-divergence prefixes, no genuinely paid resource option was shared at the same pre-decision body, so there is **no directly observed paired same-option score effect**. Do not compare post-treatment bodily states as if identical or call these two G0 seeds independent genetic populations.

No heldout efficacy claim, no new genetics, no behavioral rescue script, no post-hoc tuning from P5c validation worlds 96–119, and no activation of I3. The original P4 reserve stays closed. For stronger causal claims, use a separately preregistered design with independent genotypes/ecologies and fresh validation >=120; studying individual memory/history remains a separate goal under #1026.
