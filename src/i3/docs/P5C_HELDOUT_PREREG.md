## Scientific question and bounded causal treatment

The subsequent P5b [post-hoc decomposition](studies/p5b-paid-readout-heldout/POSTHOC_SCORE_TERMS.md)
found many changed *bonus-only* scores for the globally agnostic
effect model. It is explicitly hypothesis generating, not an
independent discovery. P5c now tests an intervention on the
**functional score component itself**, while preserving the
learned, personally grounded action-consequence predictions.

Every mode computes the same original paid decision prediction,
base projected return, curiosity bonus and uncertain-meal risk penalty;
only the component **actually retained in the score** differs.
The four conditions are:

| Mode | Computed curiosity applied | Computed uncertain-meal risk applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `normal` | yes | yes |
| `discard_curiosity_bonus` | no | yes |
| `discard_risk_penalty` | yes | no |
| `discard_both_bonus_terms` | no | no |

All modes retain their own observations, source detection,
personal learned effect statistics, TD and replay, orientation,
same learned integrity veto, first-person information, motor
admissibility and bodily v2 laws. No world oracle or fabricated
work is permitted. This **does not remove all risk avoidance**,
because the integrity veto remains active and the score penalty
is specific to uncertain meals.

## Prespecified outcomes, multiplicity and censoring

The **primary endpoint** is alive and censored at physical step 400.
Within each original independent population, each mode has 72
life records. The three comparisons are **normal minus** each
specified ablation:
(1) `discard_curiosity_bonus`,
(2) `discard_risk_penalty`,
(3) `discard_both_bonus_terms`.

For every comparison, report all four population differences,
their mean, an exploratory Student-t 95% interval (df 3),
exact two-sided paired-population sign-flip reference p,
and Holm exploratory adjustment across the three original
contrasts. **Minimum attainable exact bilateral p with n=4
is 0.125**, so even unanimous directional results are NOT
conventional confirmatory significance. No mode is declared
an evolutionary winner from this screen, including if removing
risk appears beneficial.

**Secondary functional endpoints**, reported without choosing
favorable outcomes: deaths and causes; steps lived; physical
inactivity; admitted actions; decision-funding failures;
actual *requested/executed/paid* energy and vigilance per
decision and learning phase, real replay counts and update counts.

**Mechanistic endpoints**: for the first 12 real prepaid
decision steps of every life, record each legitimately scored
candidate's original projected base return, originally computed
curiosity and risk terms, applied curiosity/risk terms, final
score, and same individual's pre-decision body. In each paired
normal-versus-intervention history, count (a) the first physical
action divergence within 12 steps, and, **before that change**,
the first difference in the *same genuinely funded option*
under an *identical five-channel pre-decision body* for
final score, original base return, applied curiosity and
applied meal risk. Report null/unchanged pairs explicitly.
Matched bodies do **not** mean identical hidden mind state
or perceived world, and these are descriptive diagnostics,
not a second randomized intervention on mental state.

Identical **initial work prices** do not imply identical
**realized cognitive costs** once an intervention changes action,
activity, energy, vigilance, learning opportunities or death.
Do not charge unexecuted work, post-hoc equalize bills, or
interpret the resulting survival contrast as a pure score
effect at identical realized energy. No postmortem learning,
unpaid prediction or extra observer cognition is permitted.
