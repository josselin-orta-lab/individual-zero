# I3 P5c: paid curiosity and risk score-addend controls

**Status: DEVELOPMENT-ONLY engineering diagnostic.** Not a preregistered
P5c survival experiment, not independent genetic-population inference, and
not an I3 engine activation. The active engine remains I1 and the
P4 reserve is sealed. [Research issue #1046](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1046).

## Orthogonal intervention: which computed score addend is retained?

The existing `Mind` continues to predict the five-channel bodily
consequences of every actually funded candidate plan, with the same
first-person beliefs, stored action-effect model, paid simulated
steps, learned values, source detection, integrity veto, energy and
vigilance tariff, motor admissibility and mortality. The score
remains computed as

`score = projected_base_return + applied_curiosity_bonus + applied_risk_penalty`.

Both candidate addends are **always computed** using the original
formulas and the original funded planning path. The intervention
drops a designated addend **only before competition**, not before
computation. It does not change the innate genotype, inject a
world fact, alter the metabolic economy or grant a free motor command.

| Mode | Curiosity term applied | Risk penalty applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `normal` | yes | yes |
| `discard_curiosity_bonus` | no | yes |
| `discard_risk_penalty` | yes | no |
| `discard_both_bonus_terms` | no | no |

The existing optional `observer_decision_diagnostics` score witness
contains **both computed and applied terms** for each *already paid*
candidate: `curiosity_bonus_computed`,
`risk_penalty_computed`, `curiosity_bonus_applied`,
`risk_penalty_applied`, `base_return`, `total_score`,
`uncertainty` and `first_forecast`. No new prediction is invoked
to produce these records. The historical `normal` and no-observer
paths preserve baseline physical actions and original evidence hashes.
The P5c modifier is separate from the P5b `effect_readout` modifier.

**Limits:** Retaining the genuinely executed prediction and original
prepaid planning work is not equivalent to equalizing realized work
after an intervention changes the individual's choices, body,
sleep, learning opportunities, and lifespan. The risk penalty
in this first prototype covers *uncertain meals*; the action
controller's low-integrity learned veto remains enabled in all
modes. This is a test of an addend, **not** all defensive behavior.

## Fixed small DEVELOPMENT-only panel

`PYTHONPATH=src python -m i3.lab.p5c_development --run-development`

Six development-world keys **26..31** across the three original
ecological families (two repeats), original G0 engineering genome
seeds 901/902, fixed life seeds **660026..660031** and 120-step
horizon. There are **12 paired short lives per score mode, 48 lives
total**. These are engineering fixtures, NOT four independent G2
genetic lineages or new scientific validation data.

| Mode | Censored at 120 steps /12 | Physical inactive steps across lives | Paid cognitive energy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Normal | **11** | **7** | 858.2648 |
| Discard curiosity only | **7** | **0** | 744.7836 |
| Discard risk penalty only | **12** | **20** | 864.1054 |
| Discard both terms | **7** | **0** | 745.0160 |

Within these 12 paired engineering histories, both curiosity
ablations show changes in paid option scores and physical actions
in **12/12 pairs**, earliest observed divergence **step 1**.
Discarding only the risk penalty affects paid option scores in
**8/12 pairs** (earliest score change **step 3**) and physical
actions in **8/12 pairs** (earliest action change **step 9**).
Those earliest times can come from different pairs; they are
not paired causal chains.

**Adverse and null outcomes are retained.** The apparent difference
between 7/12 and 11/12 surviving short lives is not a powered
scientific survival result. Dropping only the risk penalty gives
12/12 short survivors in this *particular engineering panel*,
but this neither validates unsafe eating nor establishes risk
penalties as harmful generally. Development observations must not
be used to select favorable worlds, genomes or score coefficients
for a later held-out cohort.
