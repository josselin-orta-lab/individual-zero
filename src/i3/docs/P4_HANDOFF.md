# I3 P4 handoff: what was tested, what failed, what became informative

Date: 6 November 2026. **Current engine registration: I1 only.**
I3 remains a prototype research proposal, not an activated system.
Primary tracking: [#1002](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1002),
[#1018](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1018).

## Succinct scientific state

**P4a did NOT validate a robust evolutionary advantage.**
All 48 frozen G2 genomes were evaluated prospectively on 24 new
worlds (288 lives). Selected-parent vs random-parent survival:
47/144 vs 42/144, but independent population contrasts
**-2, +9, -1, -1** of 36 each. The aggregate gain relies on one
population; n=4 exploratory interval includes harm and benefit.
[Registered protocol](P4A_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) and
[unfiltered outcome](studies/p4a-g2-heldout/RESULTS.md).

**P4b found a large bounded causal intervention effect within the model.**
All 48 frozen G2 genotypes were compared in 576 paired new lives, on
24 *other* fresh worlds, normal outcome-learning vs a paid execution
of the same learned-outcome update on a discarded copy. Censored
survivors: **84/288 normal vs 0/288 shadow-discard**; population
contrasts **+17,+35,+17,+15** of 72 each. All 144 planned same-genome
different-lived-history pairs had different terminal outcome-model
fingerprints. [Registered protocol](P4B_LEARNING_PREREG.md) and
[unfiltered outcome](studies/p4b-paid-outcome-learning/RESULTS.md).

These are **two different questions**. Do not turn the P4b learning
effect into a claim that parent selection worked. Nor does divergent
memory prove a philosophical, subjective or biological self.

## Essential limitations

- P4b preserves active **spatial-belief learning, mapping, sensorimotor
  orientation and innate prediction** in both modes. It removes durable
  outcome-effect/value/replay updates only.
- Shadow computations are genuinely executed and prepaid, but the
  independent experience and future memory occupancy differ greatly.
  Normal and shadow thus have identical initial opportunities and cost
  *rules*, not exact realized cognitive expenditure. The surviving
  difference also includes altered inactivity and physical economy.
  Do not call it an energy-matched proof of information alone.
- Four independent populations are a small sample; do not use the
  288 or 576 within-world lives as independent population replicates.
- No learning tests here disentangle the incremental role of effects
  versus value updates versus replay or sleep consolidation.
- Original P2 laboratory heuristic/oracle is still NOT a matched-cost
  survival ceiling (0 of 7,729 individual static-tariff work vectors
  identical in the earlier development audit).
- **P4 reserve is sealed**; previous P3/P4a/P4b world keys should
  not be recycled for tuning or declared fresh evaluation.

## Next research intervention, if pursued

A focused future protocol should vary **one retained learned mechanism
at a time** (outcome-effect inference, learned values, sleep replay,
and spatial beliefs) while preserving physical laws, body budget,
sensorimotor position bookkeeping and actual prepaid cognition. Add
an explicit energetic-mediation analysis before attributing survival
changes to information quality; if striving for equal realized costs,
the control must execute genuinely comparable paid computations,
not receive imaginary invoices.

Use more independent populations and wholly fresh, preregistered
ecologies for any future generalization claim. For the individuality
question, move beyond different final memory hashes: prospectively
measure enduring differences in behaviour, self-relevant evaluations
and response to new problems **within the same genotype** after
different life histories. The unit of inference must match the
scientific question and the memory effects must survive fresh
challenge without inheritance across births.

Priority is falsifiable, functional lives, not unlimited infrastructure
or a green checkmark. Preserve adverse outcomes and freeze code before
testing. No automatic I3 activation follows.
