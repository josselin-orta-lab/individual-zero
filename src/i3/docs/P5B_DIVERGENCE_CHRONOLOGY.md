# I3 P5b: first paired decision, body, funding and paid-work divergence

**Status: observer-only DEVELOPMENT instrument, not a held-out scientific experiment.**
I1 is the only active generation, I3 is unregistered and the sealed reserve
remains unopened. This continues [#1035](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1035).

The source-neutral [P5b action-agnostic prior](P5B_AGNOSTIC_PRIOR_FINDINGS.md)
supplies the existing effect model's personally learned global outcome mean
to every prospective action, rather than the action-specific mean.
Development-only trials produced 894 inactive steps, 891
`NO_PROCESSING_BUDGET` events, and only 476 admitted actions in 12
lives, versus respectively 109, 109 and 1261 normally. At the 120-step
horizon both conditions censored 8/12 lives. This is not held-out survival evidence.

## New chronological observer, without changing the animal

The same fixed `development` worlds 20–25 and G0 genome seeds 901/902
are replayed *for engineering diagnosis only*, with existing v2 rules and
identical first-person histories at birth. From the complete post-run trace
the observer extracts, for each of 12 genome/world pairs and readout
mode, the **first different physical action or parameter**, **first
different decision funding status**, **first different post-step body
vector**, and **first different actual paid decision energy/vigilance**.
A shorter life is treated as divergence when the comparator has a
subsequent step.

All comparisons are performed after birth/life execution, and never feed
into the mind. No new cognition or fictitious billing is introduced.

| Readout masked, vs `full` | Paired action divergence | Earliest action divergence | Earliest body divergence | Earliest paid-decision-work divergence | Earliest funding-status divergence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Action-agnostic global personal prior | 12/12 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 33 |
| Zero simulated action prediction | 12/12 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 37 |
| Learned sleep forecast only | 12/12 | 6 | 6 | 7 | 46 |

All displayed times are **minimum steps across different pairs**, not
necessarily a within-pair temporal chain. The first budget-status
difference is also **not** the first difference in physical capability
or body reserves. Different actions can immediately change physical
states and cognitive work, long before the exact status flag differs.

On this fixed engineering fixture, the readout intervention changes
physical behavior before widespread `NO_PROCESSING_BUDGET` failures.
That is consistent with a choice → bodily/economic trajectory →
processing-budget cascade, but does not identify the complete causal
mechanism or prove that an informed predictor improves survival.
A future study should analyze early vitality and later choice
opportunity without conditioning away post-treatment mediation.

## Verification and research boundary

[GitHub CI 42213296798](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/actions/runs/42213296798)
passes 151 I3 tests on Python 3.11 and Python 3.12, engine-generation
isolation, synthetic assertions on all paired chronology fields, and
SHA-256 verification of original P4a/P4b/P5a full evidence.
No selection, held-out validation or reserve world was opened.

Before a P5b held-out pilot, prospectively define a falsifiable
comparison between genuinely action-specific predictions and an
uninformed/agnostic but structurally intact interface; independently
freeze source, world indices >=72, original G2 genotypes,
population-level endpoints and actual paid-work mediators.
There is no scientific GO, no broad generalization or individuality
claim from this development diagnostic.
