# I3 P5b: funded option-score witnesses before bodily divergence

**Status:** DEVELOPMENT ONLY. This is not a prospectively registered P5b
held-out scientific study. I1 active, I3 unregistered, P4 reserve sealed.
Research tracking: [#1035](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1035).

## New instrument

The already optional `observer_decision_diagnostics` sidecar captures
only **actually computed, prepaid** per-option projections in the existing
`Mind.decide` loop. Each paid score record contains the first simulated
action key and its already read predicted five-channel delta/uncertainty,
base projected value, curiosity/integrity bonus, and final score. The
sidecar additionally snapshots the physical five-channel body at the
start of the decision. It never performs an additional prediction,
reorders options, changes intention, consumes randomness, requests
extra cognition, injects world knowledge or modifies the living mind's
score. The historical `full` default still generates unchanged
decisions and hashes when the observer is disabled.

A plan with failed funding has **no invented score record**.
Early no-processing-budget steps record an empty score witness, as
required by the fact that no options were actually evaluated.

## Fixed paired development diagnostic

On original, previously designated `development` indices 20–25,
G0 genomes 901/902 and 120-step horizons, the already documented
84 engineering lives across seven readout controls were run with
observer-only trace. All old P4a/P4b/P5a full evidence hashes remained
unchanged. No validation, selection or reserve world was opened.

For each of 12 paired genome/world/seed cases and each non-default
mode, post-life comparison now reports (a) first physical action
divergence, (b) first paid score witness difference, and (c) first
score difference for the **same option** at an **identical pre-decision
body**. This latter test avoids attributing differences due solely to
an already divergent physical state to the predictive readout.

| Readout condition vs full | Pairs with matched-body shared-option score difference | Earliest such score difference | Earliest divergent physical action |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `blind_action_predictions` | 12/12 | Step 2 | Step 4 |
| `action_agnostic_prior` | 12/12 | Step 2 | Step 4 |
| `blind_sleep_forecast` | 12/12 | Step 5 | Step 6 |

These are **minimum observations across possibly different paired
cases**, not a within-pair ordered causal chain. The score difference
itself can combine changes in the projected return and its curiosity
or risk contribution; the witness preserves both terms for later
falsification. We cannot automatically claim the selected option
changed due *only* to one isolated predictor: perceived source beliefs,
RNG sequence and future bodily work may subsequently differ.

Still, the verified engineering observation is stronger than a
late-inactivity correlation: the **same named candidate at a matched
body state can be scored differently before a changed physical choice
appears anywhere in these pairs**. The feedback between changes in
first-person predictions, evaluation, embodied choices and subsequent
vigilance/energy remains the central mechanistic question.
