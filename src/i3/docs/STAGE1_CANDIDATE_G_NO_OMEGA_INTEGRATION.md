### Decision projection rule

The Mind's persistent/default projection mode remains P1.3 reference.

Before option scoring, the candidate may buy only the already-declared
first-person support reads needed to decide whether an information path exists.

A decision is `INFORMATION_ACTIVE` only when:

1. at least one ordinary P1.3 frontier explorer option exists; and
2. either:
   - before personal drink support, paid personal water-recurrence estimation
     has at least one retained recurrence gap; or
   - after personal drink support, the existing paid actionable frontier
     belief contains positive actionable-water probability.

When a decision is `INFORMATION_ACTIVE`, **every option score in that decision**
uses `simulation_mode="candidate_f"` and
`projection_accounting="event_local_quadratic4"`, including ordinary
non-frontier options and presence-failure branches. This keeps scores
commensurate.

When it is not active, every score uses the unchanged P1.3 reference projector.

The projector switch is decision-local and must be restored to reference before
`decide()` returns, including fail-closed exits.
