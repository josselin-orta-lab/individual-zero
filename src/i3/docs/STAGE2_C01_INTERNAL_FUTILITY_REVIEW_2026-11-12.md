## 1. Question and review discipline

The original hypothesis (see [Stage 2 launch](STAGE2_LAUNCH.md) and [development plan](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md)) is not simply that a computation can be performed, that it moves a score, or that a lower budget can save energy. It is: can an individual, using first-person history and its own body-state distress, **choose when another actual calculation is worth its expected benefit relative to its bodily price**, preserving its authority over motor action? Scientific stages 2.1/2.2/2.3 concern optional replay, Competition cycle and simulation depth respectively. The old engineering phase P2 is separate.

Review rules: (a) no post-hoc change of hypothesis or statistical margin; (b) no pseudo-replication of synthetic cases or correlated life steps; (c) no mixing of different interventions (replay count, replay identity, effects readout, information visibility); (d) no treating a projected or detached counterfactual motor as an executed World motor; (e) no claim of independent review just because it was generated by another agent; (f) no new bodies, seeds or effects chosen to reverse a frozen negative.

The document tests two competing closure arguments and sets out where they fail. It is an internal futility **triage**, not the external C01 adjudication requested by the [full audit](STAGE2_COMPLETION_AUDIT_AND_ROADMAP_2026-11-11.md) and its [post-E3 addendum](STAGE2_POST_E3_AUDIT_ADDENDUM_2026-11-11.md).

## 3. Falsification of two tempting stage-completion arguments

**Argument A: 'E3 has 0/2 motor changes, so thinking has no survival value and Stage 2 is negatively complete.' REJECTED.** The two informed histories are synthetic, not drawn independently from a target population, and neither was born/lived. They do not implement endogenous selection of extra work. The frozen E3 intervention provides no real World motor/bodily consequence. Failing a necessary predeclared *screening* condition for that candidate says nothing general about all information computations. **Counter-hypothesis not ruled out:** a different personally learned, affordably acquired information source could affect later memory, action, and bodily value. A missing immediate action change also does not logically exclude a delayed effect unless that delayed outcome was examined under an appropriate design.

**Argument B: 'B1 GATE survival is up and conditional 95% lower bound exceeds a 5pp margin, so Stage 2 succeeds.' REJECTED.** The threshold is retrospective and unapproved; the world mechanism and population independence are not established; heterogeneous family outcomes and expenditure in both body currencies remain adverse/uncertain; the outcome is a full policy comparison, not identification of marginal information value. The reported variation among B1, 80/81, RANDOM and content arms is not an independent multi-study meta-analysis and cannot be pooled or silently treated as matched interventions.

**Other design threat:** The native P1.3 Mind's original TD replay buffer records absolute TD error, before/after body and death, **not action identity** (see [mind.py](../modules/mind.py) `learn_from_outcome`). Its replay cannot, by its present input schema alone, recover a new action-conditional effect forecast. The separate [ReferenceBranchMemory](../modules/reference_branches.py) retains action-linked empirical effects but frozen E3 already tried **one exact source transformation**. Retuning E3 on favorable initial body/action data would select on the endpoint. For any genuinely distinct source, pre-committed, non-retuned action discrimination must precede a scientific life.

## 4. Internal C01 disposition and gates

**C01 internal result: `HOLD_NO_VERDICT_FOR_GENERAL_STAGE2`.**
1. **Accepted candidate-specific negatives:** E1 fixed-grid native depth screen, E3 fixed-grid paid branch decision screen; preserve their original frozen code/receipts. Descriptive evidence on 2.1 replay quantity/priority remains mixed; no confirmatory cost-aware replay advantage.
2. **Rejected as unsupported:** global adaptive-thinking benefit, global failure, population-level survival non-inferiority, cross-family generalized learning, autonomous choice of whether to buy extra computation, completion of technical 2.2/2.3.
3. **External methodological review still unperformed.** An independent, source-pinned review may accept a *bounded negative closeout* **only if the original hypothesis and inference target genuinely match the tested scope**; an internal author cannot award that independence to itself.
4. **Operational decision:** no new scientific birth, no E3 rescue, no 2.2/2.3 development solely on E3, no new architecture rewrite, no registry/manifest change. I3 architectural reference; I1 active runtime; I3 P1.3 funded-v2 scientific reference; Stage 2.1 NOT_ISSUED.
5. **Conditional reopening gate:** provide a **genuinely different** first-person evidence variable/estimator and its mechanistic derivation *before* any trial; specify why it was not captured by E3, the operator workload/tariff, a no-World natural native-action discrimination under equal operator work VISIBLE/PAID_DISCARD, death/low E/V/null-source controls and immutable stop rule. Without such a source, do not execute C02 or open a World. A candidate that merely changes a score has failed this action-screen criterion.
6. **If C02 passes:** only then preregister actual born experience -> native decision -> World motor -> post-paid-learning body, complete energy/vigilance/opportunity costs, a defensible ecological independent-unit estimand/uncertainty model, exact-source G/R rehearsal and new specific scientific authorization, as in C03–C06.

## 5. Decision on allocation of development resources

The most scientifically useful near-term act is to **stop spending on indistinguishable metareasoning variants**. Maintain the negative and inconclusive findings as a publishable bounded result. Continue independently the reference I3 generation's R1/R2 M11 engineering and cold durability work in its own technical release stream; it must never be used as evidence that Stage 2 adaptive thinking succeeded.

**Stop point:** no eligible genuinely new evidence source has been identified by this review; therefore **0 new Stage-2 Worlds, 0 simulated scientific lives, 0 additional cognitive candidate implementations**. C01 is completed **as internal futility triage only**; the requirement for an actually independent review remains OPEN. This is not Stage 2 closure.

**Short answer:** preserve native agency and self-stakes by withholding untestable controllers; either a defensible independent scoped negative verdict, or one genuinely distinct first-person source that prospectively changes a native decision under equal real cognitive effort, then one causal living study if legally/technically authorized. Do not turn an absence of evidence into a false claim of autonomous cognition.
