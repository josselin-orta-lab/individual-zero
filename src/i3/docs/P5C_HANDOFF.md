# I3 P5c handoff: bounded curiosity score dependence and next science

**6 November 2026. Status: P5c first prospective screen COMPLETE, original
evidence independently verified.** The active engine is **I1**, I3
remains an unregistered research prototype, and the original P4
reserve remains sealed. Main issue
[#1046](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1046),

## What the first registered screen actually established

[Exact P5c preregistration](P5C_HELDOUT_PREREG.md) merged before
the one-shot source-locked experiment; [unfiltered, original
1152 paid lives and independently audited findings](studies/p5c-paid-score-addends/RESULTS.md).
24 new validation worlds (96–119), all 48 frozen G2 genomes,
four historical genetic populations, four score-addend
retention interventions and 400 physical steps maximum.

| Mode | Censored alive at step 400, /288 | Inactive physical fraction |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Normal curiosity + uncertain-meal risk | **110** | 18.65% |
| Keep risk, discard scored curiosity | **4** | 1.13% |
| Keep curiosity, discard scored risk penalty | **105** | 18.69% |
| Discard both scored terms | **7** | 1.90% |

The observed within-I3 curiosity retention contrast is
**[+21,+41,+22,+22] survivors per 72** independent
genetic-population lives, mean +26.5, exploratory t interval
[11.10,41.90], exact paired n=4 two-sided sign-flip p=0.125
and three-comparison Holm p=0.375. The risk-only contrast
**[+5,+1,+3,-4]** is mixed, exact p=0.625.
These are **exploratory** results. Four independent source
populations cannot give a two-sided exact p below 0.125;
the apparently decisive aggregate survivor count must not
be used to claim conventional significance, selection GO or
universal ecological robustness.

All four modes compute/pay the original learned action-consequence
projection, base return, curiosity and uncertain-meal risk; only
the *application* of specific already computed score terms differs.
The original first-person cognition, spatial map, source signs,
cost prices, learned effect statistics and integrity veto stay.
In matched first-12-step paid option-score evidence,
276/288 curiosity-ablated paired opportunities differ in
early score and action. Curiosity is therefore associated with
a real downstream choice mechanism, not simply a different
stored memory hash.

## Crucial contrast to P5b and energetic limitation

P5b showed that masking **action-specific consequence predictions**
caused high physical inactivity (roughly 76% of lived steps) and
0/288 survival in two forecast-blind conditions. P5c curiosity
ablation also sharply reduces survival, but with **only 1.13%**
physical inactivity in the curiosity-only control. The
intervention changes *active behavior and its life history*,
not merely the ability to obtain a processing budget or propose
actions. An individual can be active and still fail to meet
its needs, but the exact death/resource pathways need a
**new preregistered study** rather than retroactive selection
of favorable stories.

Because life lengths, future choices, vigilance and learning
opportunities diverge, realized cognitive energy for the
four P5c modes is respectively **47,725.8014; 24,942.1638;
47,168.8064; 24,579.5536**. Nominal work prices and initial
genomes/worlds are paired, but **actual billed work is not
equal**. Never claim a controlled equal-realized-work effect,
post-hoc charge unexecuted cognition or adjust away mediators
to manufacture equivalence.

## What to prioritize next

The immediate mechanistic uncertainty is **why the absence
of scored curiosity produces rapid *active* mortality**.
First analyze the **existing original P5c receipts without
running any new life**: by mode/population/ecology and
within-genome paired controls, report death causes, early
physical action families, resource contacts if actually
available in the paid trace, lifetime, later bodily reserves,
actual cognition and offered/funded opportunities. Distinguish
what the original archive can actually support from data
never recorded. This is post-hoc and descriptive, not a
change to the registered primary endpoint.

A genuinely new hypothesis, to be separately registered
before validation exposure, could separate **general
uncertainty curiosity** from **need-weighted curiosity**
without injecting true world facts or pretending to pay
for work not done. Before any held-out replication,
use development-only fixtures, independent genetic
populations beyond the original four if claiming
broader inferential confidence, new ecological mechanisms
and unseen validation-world indices **>=120**.
Do not choose high-survival genomes, worlds or parameters
by consulting P5c outcomes. Never reopen old worlds 0–119
or the sealed reserve.

A separate crucial research objective under #1026 is
**same inherited genome, different first-person life
histories, then identical novel challenges faced by the
same continuing surviving individual**. This is an
experiment about acquired behavioral individuality,
not automatically established by curiosity-driven
survival or different terminal state hashes.

**No I3 activation, no general scientific GO.**

## Original-data-only post-hoc active mortality diagnostic

The subsequent [P5c post-hoc active-mortality and early-command
audit](studies/p5c-paid-score-addends/POSTHOC_ACTIVE_MORTALITY.md)
used only the unchanged 1152 original compact receipts and
generated **no new life**. It found, across all 288 lives per
mode, curiosity ablation produced **239 zero-water and 45
zero-food deaths**, versus **146 and 32** normal; in
the first 12 steps of each original life, only **132
resource-related commands** were observed without curiosity
versus **392** normal, while early rest/wait commands
increased from 383 to 809. These are repeated observations,
not new independent genetic populations, and the
original compact receipts do **not** contain every later
resource contact, successful consumption or full physical
trajectory. The first scientific hypothesis is **actively
choosing without sufficiently discovering/acquiring vital
resources**, not mere paralysis or zero processing budget.
A future diagnostic must prospectively record personally
available and actually acquired resources with real costs;
this post-hoc audit does not establish that mechanism
causally or allow reusing old validation worlds.
