# I3 scientific Stage 2: evidence audit, closure criteria, and minimal roadmap

**Audit date:** 2026-11-11. **Read baseline:** main commit 9bae12732a4bdfda5ed98983a70dbcfe7fe9cb98 (2026-11-11 12:12:53 UTC). **Nature:** documentation and research planning ONLY; no cognitive policy, genome, World law, study protocol, cohort, index, result, or engine-registry change. **Decision:** Stage 2 is NOT SCIENTIFICALLY COMPLETE. This is an audit recommendation, not a retroactive amendment to a frozen protocol or permission to open Worlds.

## 1. Executive decision and source hierarchy

Keep **P1.3 funded-v2** as the reference, I3 an unregistered research proposal, I1 the active engine. Do **not** rewrite the architecture, relaunch closed cohorts, adopt the replay gate as generally beneficial, or open Stage 2.2 merely because Stage 2.1 has a software capacity verdict.

The narrowest scientifically productive next step is to resolve **whether any additional, physically paid computation yields information that changes a later living, unforced choice and improves outcomes measured through the individual's own intrinsic distress**. First use existing evidence and software falsifiers. Only if that mechanism is discriminating, implement one optional generalized computation-budget control and run **one** independently designed prospective study. A sound negative or infeasible verdict is acceptable; a merely underpowered, non-independent, or counterfactual-only positive is not an established result.

The fundamental research goal is functional self-determination: a system acts from its own bodily state, needs, first-person experience, generic inherited dispositions, and real effects on its continued existence. Stage 2 addresses the **allocation of its own cognitive expenditure**, not all of generalized agency. Stages 3-6 cover environmental change, transferable representations, delayed credit, topology, and multi-family evolutionary generalization. This is not a consciousness or AGI claim.

Authority precedence, as required by [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and [I3 AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md): [engine registry](../../../config/engine-registry.json) -> [I3 engine contract](../engine.json) -> accepted [ADR-0014](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0014-i3-lignee-enjeu-de-soi.md), [ADR-0015](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0015-i3-portes-sous-selection.md), [ADR-0016](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0016-i3-generalization-without-imposed-rules.md), [ADR-0017](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0017-i3-moratoire-deux-phases-repetition-scellee.md), [ADR-0018](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0018-i3-cloture-etape-1.md), [ADR-0019](../../../docs/decisions/ADR-0019-i3-exact-transport-rehearsal-before-lift.md) -> [development plan](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md), [active manifest](../ACTIVE_SURFACE.json), [current status](STATUS.md) -> executable source -> source-locked original experiments and their independently checkable receipts. A plan, passing software test, status label, or GitHub issue closure does not establish behavioral science.

**Critical distinction:** historical engineering phase **P2** (ecologies, comparators, cohort separation, completed as methodology for P3/P4) is NOT scientific **Stage 2** (choose how much to think). This document audits the latter. Historic P2's diagnostic exact cost-vector equivalence was **0/7,729**, not a positive matched-probe ceiling: [P2 cost audit](P2_COST_AUDIT_RESULTS.md), [equal-opportunity decision](P2_EXIT_EQUAL_OPPORTUNITY.md).

## 5. Gap diagnosis: implementation, model and protocol, not interchangeable

- **P0 scientific-identifiability gap:** personal replay changes TD residuals but not EffectsModel one-step predictions under replay-identity ablation; 48 synthetic controls showed zero one-step prediction differences. Using that forecast's error to rank replay memories would be structurally vacuous. The optional shadow-distress head is currently action-agnostic because the historical TD replay buffer does not store action identity; it cannot be promoted to an action-conditional off-policy oracle. Old next-action alternative was never physically executed. **Consequence:** no net information value or causal bodily gain identified. **Hypothesis:** the tested cognitive outputs rarely change choices, or measured targets are orthogonal to what replay learns. Source: [predictive negative control](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/REPLAY_PREDICTIVE_NEGATIVE_CONTROL_2026-11-10.md), [next-action bridge](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/ARCHIVED_NEXT_ACTION_ACTUAL_VS_SHADOW_BRIDGE_RESULTS_2026-11-11.md).
- **P0 inference gap:** no independently attested fresh ecological sampling frame, justified tolerated survival-loss margin, valid sample-size and mean-NI test, or confirmation outside F0. **Consequence:** favorable DEVELOPMENT signals cannot meet the frozen scientific purpose; a green test or exact original hash confirms execution custody, not iid allocation or claims. **Hypothesis:** the effect could vanish or reverse at independent ecology level. Source: [#1226](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1226), [#1239](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1239).
- **P0 modeled-cost gap:** costs are exactly prepaid and checked for declared CognitiveWork but extra head clone, buffer sort, HMAC and prequential readout CPU/memory overhead are not mapped to a validated bounded tariff, nor is the full expense of losing later learning/freeing food through sleep reflected by a one-step two-currency number. **Consequence:** apparent “cognition saved” may hide foregone learning or food exhaustion. **Hypothesis:** complete model-price and future opportunity costs remove the putative gain. Source: [#1283](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1283), [256-cell study](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/COMPUTATION_VALUE_IDENTIFIABILITY_RESULTS_2026-11-11.md), [sleep transfer](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/SLEEP_COGNITION_METABOLIC_TRANSFER_RESULTS_2026-11-11.md).
- **P1 missing general metareasoning:** the reference's deliberation count and horizon are genetically/body limited but not earned by expected D gain. Stage 2.1 has a candidate replay gate but not a coherent cross-operation value-of-computation arbitration. **Consequence:** “self-determines thinking expenditure” is not true for all advertised processes. **Hypothesis:** stopping additional cycles/simulations when they do not change rank improves actual bodily economy. Source: [mind.py](../modules/mind.py), [launch](STAGE2_LAUNCH.md).
- **P1 generalization and long-life gaps:** all studied F0 families share a known generation system; changed-source failure, multiple simultaneous needs, repeated deprivation, longer survival, and future transferable worlds are not shown generally. **Consequence:** no strong claim of generalized survival or self-determination. **Hypothesis:** budget allocation that adapts to viability may transfer even when learned object priors do not; must test on a genuinely withheld ecological **mechanism**, not just unused seeds. This narrow transfer gate is **not** a substitute for Stages 3-6.
- **P2 known projection discretization risk:** [#1111](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1111) found a chunk-end forecast bias up to 0.169 against paid 255-step diagnostic. The Stage-2 launch's restricted 126-state probe found 0 changed top choices for P1.3; precision-on-demand is not the next funded intervention. Reopen only if a Stage-2 prospective decision-discriminating witness proves this bias materially affects the value-of-computation result. Never quietly replace the frozen reference projector.

Scope changes to scientific policy are new, opt-in, versioned proposals, **never** edits to previously frozen R0-R4 or historical P1.3.

### S2-C00 | Decide and freeze Stage 2 closure charter | P0 | ~0.5-1.5 agent-days, zero lives

- **Problem/evidence:** [Stage-2 issue #1062](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1062) remains broad, [old selected gate](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/INFERENCE_POWER_AUDIT.md) is statistically non-identifying, Stage 2.1 source protocols are frozen while later developmental results proliferated.
- **Scientific hypothesis:** a single predeclared estimand and stop rule will allow a bounded negative, positive or infeasible conclusion without choosing favorable post-hoc outcomes. **Falsifier:** no defensible independent unit, margin, outcome or feasible discriminating experiment can be specified; declare NO_GO/INFEASIBLE honestly rather than claim positive closure.
- **Deliverable:** one versioned **future** protocol supplement, never rewriting old original studies, that defines expected D-gain and total net work cost, separate mechanism and full-policy estimands, F0-vs-novel-family scope, exact population unit, death/terminal/censor/no-exposure, statistical target, feasible maximum births, all costs, selection/validation boundaries, choice of adoption and negative-stopping verdict.

Owner approval of this audit alone is not new World permission.

### S2-C01 | Discriminating personal information and fully modeled price | P0 | ~2-4 agent-days, zero scientific lives

- **Problem/evidence:** [Stage-2.1 software R3](studies/stage2-replay-gate-software/RESULTS.md) flat 16/16 across S1; [#1276](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1276) one-step effect forecast invariant under TD replay; [#1283](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1283) uncalibrated optional head overhead; [256 cost cells](studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/COMPUTATION_VALUE_IDENTIFIABILITY_RESULTS_2026-11-11.md) show benefit not identified.
- **Hypothesis:** a bounded forecast computed from **personal prior outcomes**, sealed before later first-person experience and scored using the actually observed intrinsic D target, can distinguish option-relevant computation **after** charging it. **Falsifier:** no valid same-state differing forecast, no funded decision discrimination, or honest full model work exceeds expected personal benefit; record a **negative 2.1 candidate result**, not a rescue.
- **Smallest technical surface:** reuse [mind.py](../modules/mind.py) predicted/body D, [body.py](../modules/body.py) CognitiveWork, [p13_replay_gate_mind.py](../modules/p13_replay_gate_mind.py), existing observer-only [prequential tests](../../../tests/i3/test_stage2_replay_predictive_negative_control.py) and [next-action custody](../lab/stage2_next_action_mediation_custody.py). **Do not** create a general predictive head by default: choose one of (a) a replay-sensitive D/value output already present and correctly targetable, or (b) a single external, opt-in, empty-at-birth head only if (a) fails and the expected information question cannot otherwise be asked. In both cases count copy/sort/train/forecast/meta read work, allocate energy/vigilance, include future learning opportunity and sleep/food mediation, keep original P1.3 motor unchanged in software.
- **Controls/acceptance:** fixed identical pre-decision personal state and RNG, zero-work/no-information, equal paid-count priority/random-content, visible versus fully paid computed-then-discarded, renamed tokens, low E/V, terminal/censor. **0** unpaid work, hidden-world reads, forbidden postmortem operations, stolen motor reserves, fictitious next actions; **100%** source/forecast/outcome chronology validation; at least one prospective cost-sensitive **decision** and one nontrivial on/off effect before proposing any new scientific life. A lower loss on synthetic fixture alone is not enough. **Risks:** extra auxiliary head does not influence decisions, evaluator CPU is silently free, model-price confused with measured CPU joules.

### S2-C02 | One optional adaptive computation-budget mechanism (2.2 and 2.3) | P0 if C01 shows capacity; otherwise close this candidate negatively | ~3-6 agent-days, no scientific lives

- **Problem/evidence:** [mind.py](../modules/mind.py) uses body-dependent n_base cycles and h_max horizon ceilings but lacks a *personal expected marginal D improvement* criterion to buy one more Competition cycle or simulation depth step. [STAGE2_LAUNCH.md](STAGE2_LAUNCH.md) identifies them as 33% vigilance and 30% energy portions, respectively, in its historical 56/57 reduction; these are not current universal costs.
- **Hypothesis:** individually estimated marginal decision-value from one more *affordable* simulation/cycle, net of that actual projected bodily cost, allows adaptive spend without a rule tied to resource type, route, world name, or scripted action. **Falsifier:** extra computation never changes an admissible option rank, or total metareasoning/cost exceeds gain across the predeclared relevant states.
- **Implementation:** one versioned opt-in budget controller, with **two separately attributable gates**: first only the existing Competition cycle ceiling (2.2), then the existing simulation horizon ceiling (2.3) if its own software gate is informative. Reuse native P1.3 candidates, forecasts, Competition, Action Control, genome; retain current n_base/h_max as strict upper bounds. Buy the next bounded meta-evaluation before inspecting its result, with at most one declared bounded extra evaluator per candidate slice; do not plan recursively for free. The replay-gate reference is frozen and can be analyzed separately, not silently retuned or bundled.
- **Code candidates:** [mind.py](../modules/mind.py) through a subclass/isolated opt-in policy, [body.py](../modules/body.py), [life.py](../runtime/life.py) after admitted source-specific engineering transport, new focused tests under tests/i3. **Dependencies:** C00-C01 and explicit recorded scientific decision about closing/replacing Stage 2.1, not an automatic Stage 2.2 GO. **Acceptance:** forced-open parity against P1.3 on identical initial state, model-paid charge exactly equals actual debit, both mandatory zero-budget and no-op states, non-decision-costing state, ≥1 state with different chosen computational budget from fixed and random, 0 hidden World reads or forced survival motors; two slices can be ablated independently. **Risks:** selection after viewing heldout worlds, repeated “almost good” patches, changed reference Competition, new arbitrary utility bonus.

## 8. Definition of Stage 2 done, including a legitimate negative result

Stage 2 has four separate closure predicates; none is satisfied merely by all issues being closed.

- [ ] **Technical completion.** P1.3 and funded v2 intact; options/Competition/Action Control unchanged except declared opt-in candidate; replay, deliberation cycles and simulated depth each receive an explicit, paid, bounded candidate-or-negative-falsifier disposition; all mandatory work/inactivity/death/censor paths pass focused tests; no hidden World or inherited learned state.
- [ ] **Experimental completion.** A registered prospective program answered the predeclared **discriminating** mechanism question, or software/engineering falsification/independent infeasibility review explains why further organism births cannot answer it under an honest budget. Where biological claims are made, all executed G/R originals are source pinned, checked, admitted, independent-unit-aware, and have relevant physical cost outcomes. A failed/omitted run is not an observed death.
- [ ] **Scientific completion.** Either (a) a valid scoped positive result shows an individual, from its own state/experience, chooses useful additional thinking at full cost and retains prespecified viability/activity within valid independent ecological inference; or (b) rigorously bounded negative evidence/futility demonstrates that the tested reference and one minimal candidate **do not** achieve this under the stated resource/ecology scope. Negative is a scientific answer **only with explicit scope and tested falsifier**. A merely missing statistically powered result is NOT_ISSUED, not a negative theorem.
- [ ] **Transition readiness.** One accepted end-of-stage decision states exact retained reference, exposure/source checksum and reproducible protocol, what was learned about cognition/cost/mortality, in-scope generalization limit and the independent unresolved questions for Stages 3-6; original data and sealed reserve remain preserved, I3 stays unregistered until a separate engine promotion decision.

**Point of diminishing returns / stop rule:** after a cost-complete, outcome-identified software falsifier and **at most one** newly authorized, predeclared Stage-2 DEVELOPMENT intervention for a surviving viable candidate, stop candidate proliferation. Proceed to confirmatory replication **only** if an independently approved inference design is both valid and computationally feasible. If it is not, close with a *negative or feasibility-limited* finding at the **tested** scope; do not claim positive generalizable survival, and do not create another curiosity rule to get a favorable outcome. If the software candidate is already non-discriminating, spend **zero** new scientific lives. This proposed stop rule requires explicit adoption for future studies; it does not retroactively modify the frozen Stage-2.1 protocol.

## 9. Architecture recommendation and smallest path

**Conserve and extend narrowly.** Existing BodyLaws/CognitiveBudget/World, Mind's experience and Competition/Action Control, genome-only inheritance, G/R custody and original archives are valuable, integrated substrate. Do **not** replace them with a large neural network, impose action scripts, import Stage 1 candidates, switch to a new World, or rewrite the general planner without a source-bound demonstration of a defect that blocks the stage.

**Critical path:** (1) C00 honest estimand/stop charter and original evidence lock -> (2) C01 inexpensive personal-outcome and two-currency software falsification -> if negative, document a scoped negative 2.1 disposition and proceed only to one bounded 2.2/2.3 attempt when separately approved; if informative, (3) C02 one optional paid marginal-value controller (cycles first, then horizon only if warranted) -> (4) C03 independent causal design + exact-source rehearsal/owner-scoped permission -> (5) C04 one carefully limited living developmental test; confirm independently only if a feasible positive inference path survives -> (6) C05 source-based decision and stop.

**Can wait:** true latent-cause/change detection (Stage 3), successor features/delayed credit (Stage 4), learned topology/obstacle planning (Stage 5), ten-generation multi-family evolutionary selection (Stage 6), full Competition replacement, projection-precision optimization absent decision-changing witness, larger body/reward changes, and a new auxiliary predictor that does not alter an actual admitted choice. These questions matter for eventual generalized self-determination, but adding them to Stage 2 would make the conclusion harder, not stronger.

### Explicit answer to the mission question

The shortest, most rigorous and least expensive route to finish scientific Stage 2 is **not another series of exploratory cohorts or a rewrite**. Retain P1.3, freeze an independently reviewable estimand and no-retry stop rule, use current original evidence to reject unsupported replay-utility claims, perform one **paid, first-person, decision-discriminating** software falsifier of marginal computation value, and only if it survives test one opt-in generic cognition-budget mechanism through a single prospective causal lived experiment and a justified independent replication. Publish a bounded negative result and stop when it fails; promote no candidate or general survival claim on synthetic, detached, underpowered or merely F0 evidence. This preserves the possibility of emergent autonomy because the organism's decisions remain its own: no external rescue policy, no implanted strategy, only the opportunity and physically priced means to learn from consequences.
