# I3 P5b: learned-effect readout versus control dependency, development-only contract

**Scope:** engineering probe on `development` worlds ONLY. NOT a
scientific validation cohort, not a survival result, and not authorization
to reopen P3/P4/P5a worlds or the sealed P4 reserve. This work continues
[#1035](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1035)
under [#1026](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1026).
I1 remains active and I3 remains unregistered.

## The uncertainty left by P5a

P5a removed durable action-effect statistics entirely and found
**0/288** surviving lives, versus **112/288** normally, with high
physical inactivity (72.43%). That intervention could be damaging
either because action-effect information is useful, or because this
particular mind uses the learned model as a structural input to
source recognition, option valuation, sleep forecasting and vetoes.
P5a did not isolate these mechanisms; its small n=4 exact
two-sided sign-flip p is 0.125, Holm adjusted 0.375.

## First experimental interfaces

P5b introduces a **separate readout gate** that does NOT delete or
disable the learning and retention of action-effect statistics:

| Gate | Projection prediction | Learned source-positive checks | Learned sleep forecast | Integrity veto |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `full` | normal | normal | normal | normal |
| `blind_predictions` | zero unknown prior; uncertainty=1 | normal | unknown passive/sleep forecast | normal |
| `blind_action_predictions` | only simulated action deltas unknown; passive/sleep forecast intact | normal | normal | normal |
| `blind_sleep_forecast` | normal action deltas; learned sleep restoration hidden | normal | sleep unknown, passive drift retained | normal |
| `blind_sources` | normal | no known-positive learned sources | normal | normal |
| `blind_all` | unknown prior | unknown | unknown | no effect-statistics-based veto |
| `action_agnostic_prior` | same empirical global delta for every proposed action, based solely on this life's observed outcomes; unknown before any observation | normal | normal | normal |

These are deliberately *ablations*, **not** restoration or rescue
policies. A masked readout supplies neither privileged physical world
data nor learned effects fabricated from P5a outcomes. All six modes
still execute and pay the actual learning work, store the learned
effect statistics and pay the actual decision computation. First-person
perception, motor position, map and place beliefs, innate genetics,
funded body laws and deaths are unchanged.

**Caution:** `blind_predictions` blinds both passive action
drift and sleep restoration. The `blind_action_predictions` and
`blind_sleep_forecast` controls are therefore separate follow-ups
that allow the earlier bundled readout to be decomposed. `blind_all`
also removes the learned integrity veto. None of these controls
simulates physically impossible effects or injects future knowledge.

## First action-agnostic empirical control

`action_agnostic_prior` leaves the **entire historical effect-model
representation, its paid updates, source recognition, sleep forecast,
admissibility and the ordinary paid planning loop intact**. Only a plan's
predicted action delta is read from the existing global `("*",)`
running mean of the individual's own observed outcomes, irrespective
of action label or token. Before observing anything this yields zero
delta with maximal uncertainty. As a life unfolds, the distribution
can learn a general empirical *body-change scale*, but it cannot use
the action-specific association required to predict whether drinking
or sleeping has a particular effect. No world constants, privileged
resource types, artificial action benefits, fabricated work or
retroactive P5a optimization are supplied.

This is a **partially informed** comparator, not an absolute no-learning
baseline: it keeps aggregate personally acquired information. Its
difference from `blind_action_predictions` isolates some consequences
of preserving a grounded prediction scale/interface, not perfectly
the causal information content of each individual action. Like all
readout variants, downstream decisions, memory occupancy, work and
physical energy may diverge, and no exact realized-cost match is claimed.

## Observer diagnostics

The optional observer report records: number of offered candidate
options, counts by resource/movement/exploration/rest category, funded
scored options and their corresponding categories, motor admission rejections,
`no_processing_budget`, `no_funded_score`,
`control_rejected`, and `action_admitted`.
The observer does not influence ranking, RNG state, admission,
perception, cognitive charges or choices. When disabled and the
readout is `full`, the previous `run_life` behavior and source
evidence hashing are unchanged. Existing I3 provenance and original
P3/P4/P5a archives are immutable.

Development-world comparisons must inspect onset of inactivity,
no-funded-score events, offered versus evaluated options and physical
death, before interpreting any survival contrast. A physical command
not funded stays unfunded, and no postmortem cognition is introduced.

## Explicit missing controls

This slice does NOT yet provide an empirically calibrated structural
baseline or a novel P5b heldout protocol. It now separates
simulated action predictions from the learned sleep forecast,
and counts offered and scored option categories in the observer.
It does not provide an independently justified alternative policy
or a causal demonstration of information-only benefit.
A future step must define minimal first-person priors without fake
work. New worlds must have indices >=72 in the untouched validation
cohort, independently preregistered with frozen code and population
uncertainty before any life is executed.

No scientific GO, no inheritance of individual experiences across
births, no engine activation.
