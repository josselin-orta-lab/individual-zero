# I3 P5a handoff: separate action-effect memory from value/replay and cost

Date: 6 November 2026. I1 active; I3 unregistered. No global GO.
The P4 reserve remains sealed. Primary tracking [#1026](https://github.com/josselin-orta-lab/individual-zero/issues/1026).

## What is now established in the original model

[P5a was preregistered](P5A_SCREEN_PREREG.md), source pinned to
`ee2e97b1949fd94548fe6c5908ff411d37cb3f76`, then executed **once**
on 24 never-used validation worlds (48–71), 48 unmodified G2 genotypes,
four original independent genetic populations and four paid retention modes.
[All 1152 original life receipts and unfiltered analysis](studies/p5a-paid-component-screen/RESULTS.md)
are stored durably.

| Mode | Censored lives /288 | Relative to normal by independent population |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal | **112** | reference |
| Discard learned action-effect statistics | **0** | normal minus ablated **+17,+36,+21,+38** /72 |
| Discard learned TD value coefficients | **117** | **+2,-3,+2,-6** /72 |
| Discard incremental replay TD retention | **119** | **+1,-5,+3,-6** /72 |

This is a strong **within-substrate action-effect-model retention signal**:
removing long-lived action-effect statistics produces total mortality and
72.43% physical inactivity, versus 19.80% for the full learner. The other
two targeted retention ablations do not reproduce that collapse, but neither
can be called universally unnecessary or harmful. Replay was genuinely
executed and billed (422,400 replay work units in the replay-ablation mode)
and therefore the weak contrast is not just zero treatment exposure.

The inferential unit is **four** independent genetic populations, not
1152 independent experimental individuals. The strongest contrast has
exploratory t interval above zero but exact two-sided sign-flip
p=0.125, multiplicity-adjusted p=0.375. No conventional confirmatory
significance or independent-family replication is established.

## Why the large signal must not be overstated

- The effect-model statistics may be both an informative learned predictor
  **and an enabling input to the action-selection pipeline**. The ablation
  cannot separate a genuinely less informative animal from a control
  system that falls into inactivity when an expected learned input is
  absent. This is a priority architecture/behavior question, not a
  reason to tune P5a to recover survival.
- All four conditions start with the same world, body, genome, life seed,
  physical rules and nominal v2 work prices. But once behavior diverges
  the actual charges diverge: effects-ablated cognition paid 6,568.554
  energy versus normal 45,747.782, with much shorter lives. Do not
  post-hoc equalize these charges or equate conditional cost differences
  with a matched-computation information effect.
- Discarding TD values retains innate forecasts and learned consequence
  statistics. Discarding replay retains ordinary per-step TD value
  learning. These targeted interventions are not general proofs against
  values, sleep, consolidation or replay in other environments.
- P4a genetic selection remained inconsistent on independent populations;
  P5a's learning finding cannot retroactively validate evolutionary
  generalization, nor turn differing terminal memories into a
  persistent subjective identity.
- Original P2 fixed-charge heuristic still lacks actual per-step
  cost equivalence; no oracle or static tariff is an optimality bound.

## Priority bounded follow-up

A new, independently preregistered study should first **instrument action
proposal/selection pathways**, recording why agents abstain without giving
privileged external world facts to the mind. A targeted control might
preserve the learned model *interface* and provide a minimal uninformed
first-person prior instead of deleting the whole statistical representation.
Both arms must execute and pay genuine comparable computations. Measure
the onset of inactivity, choice diversity, nearest available opportunities,
life length, debt and all five bodily channels. Freeze these semantics on
development cases only before opening new validation worlds.

Only then test the causal role of action-effect **information quality**
versus action-generator integrity with new independent populations and
separately allocated worlds. Keep the G2 reference lineages unselected
unless prospectively stating a new genotype-generation protocol.

For **individuality**, a distinct experiment is needed: identical genome,
different lived histories, then a prospectively identical common challenge
*within the same surviving individual* without reinitializing the mind
or transferring personal memory across births. Compare persistent
differences in decisions, self-relevant priorities, adaptation and
counterfactual experience responses; final memory hashes alone are not
sufficient.

Keep failed runs and null outcomes. Do not open reserve worlds or
activate I3 to accommodate P5a. The scientific objective is an
autonomous, particular, embodied life, not merely passing tests.
