# Stage-1 historical document catalogue

**Stage 1 is CLOSED NEGATIVE (ADR-0018).** This catalogue belongs to the
inactive Stage-1 archive and is not a task queue. Current work belongs to
[Stage 2.1](../../docs/STAGE2_REPLAY_GATE.md) and the
[active surface](../../docs/ACTIVE_SURFACE.md).

Historical command examples in the frozen protocols are **not live entrypoints**;
see [historical CLI custody](HISTORICAL_ENTRYPOINTS.md) before attempting
to locate their retired implementations.

The 54 historical Stage-1 protocols/analysis notes and
38 Stage-1 results are indexed below. Their original repository
paths were **not moved**: those paths are used in original scientific
references, source locks, and evidence custody. Copying or relocating them
would add broken links and ambiguous duplicate authorities. Their executable
candidate implementations have been moved to
[the Stage-1 package](README.md), with old Python imports repointed.

Historical “next step” sentences in these documents are superseded by the
Stage-1 closure and current STATUS.md.
