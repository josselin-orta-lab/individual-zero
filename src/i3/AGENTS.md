2. [Active surface](docs/ACTIVE_SURFACE.md) and [machine-readable bounds](ACTIVE_SURFACE.json).

Stage2 scientific decision remains `NOT_ISSUED/HOLD_NO_VERDICT`. **Current Stage-2.1 E3 research handoff:** [frozen post-E3 scientific stop](docs/studies/stage2-replay-gate-selected-design/STAGE2_POST_E3_SCIENTIFIC_STOP_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-11.md), where **0/2** fixed E3 cases changed the legal motor. - Owner [ADR-0022](../../docs/decisions/ADR-0022-i3-owner-authorizes-prospective-experiments-internal-evidence.md) permits **new** internally witnessed prospective laboratory research without repeated discretionary approval, but **each study still requires a new frozen source, planned allocations, cost/control, non-retry rule and protocol-specific full rehearsal**. ## Handoff

Record baseline SHA, affected owned paths, hypothesis/falsifier, tests run, costs/controls, original receipts, scientific claim/status, blockers and next independently justified action in the task PR/issue. Use the [canonical handoff convention](../../docs/engineering/AGENT_HANDOFF.md), not a competing chronological narrative.

The [historical full instructions](AGENTS_HISTORY_2026-11-16.md) remain available for older permissions and one-shot decisions but cannot override a later accepted ADR or the live registry. Do not alter historical documentation to make an earlier intervention appear authorized.
